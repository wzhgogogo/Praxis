It's coated in a sweet homemade Korean ginseng sauce, and you can enjoy a flavor twist with the Den Kushi spice on the ...table! You can order 10, 21, or 36 sticks, served in a fun "Den Kushi Pyramid" presentation. We also offer other specialties like "Doru Yaki" and "Princess Yaki". The lively and vibrant atmosphere of the restaurant is perfect for a drink after work or various gatherings. Enjoy a delightful time with friends, satisfying both your stomach and heart!

Food Crispy on the outside! Chewy on the inside! Our signature Chicken Skin Skewers "Denburi"

Enjoy our crispy yet chewy chicken skin skewers, known as "Den-kushi." They pair perfectly with our homemade sweet Korean ginseng sauce, making them irresistibly delicious. Add some of our special "Den-kushi spice" from the table to enhance the flavors even more. You won't be able to stop reaching for more! Try our signature taste, available in sets of 10, 21, or 36 skewers, served in a beautiful pyramid shape.

Food Juicy "Doru-yaki" and "Princess-yaki" made with white outside skirt.

The moment you take a bite, the juicy meat flavor bursts in your mouth! Our popular dish, "Doru-yaki," uses our special rock salt and carefully selected chicken, allowing the deliciousness to spread with every bite. Enjoy it with a touch of yuzu pepper for an extra flavor kick! Also, don't miss our "Princess-yaki," which lavishly features the white outside skirt from 10 chickens! This dish offers a tender, juicy texture and rich umami that fills your mouth. It's a must-try for meat lovers!

SpacePerfect for large groups! Enjoy a fun time in a lively space where you can gather casually.

Our restaurant boasts a casual and warm atmosphere that's perfect for a lively gathering! Whether it's a fun night out with friends, a drink after work, or a girls' party or reunion, our vibrant space is just right for you. The table seating is always buzzing with energy, and we can easily accommodate larger groups by connecting tables. Enjoy delicious food and drinks together, and create unforgettable memories! *Please note that the images of the interior are from our sister restaurant.*

As a reservation bonus, we offer a coupon for a complimentary first drink! Make sure to take advantage of this great deal. *Not valid with other coupons or services, and cannot be combined during events.
