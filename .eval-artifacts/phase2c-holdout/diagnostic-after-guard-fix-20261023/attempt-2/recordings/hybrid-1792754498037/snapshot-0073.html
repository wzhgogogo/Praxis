Food Crispy on the outside, chewy on the inside! Our signature Chicken Skin Skewers "Denburi"

Enjoy our crispy yet chewy chicken skin skewers, known as "Den-kushi." They pair perfectly with our homemade sweet Korean ginseng sauce, making them irresistibly delicious. Add some of our table-side "Den-kushi spice" to enhance the flavor even more. You won't be able to stop reaching for more! Try our signature taste, available in sets of 10, 21, or 36 skewers, served in a beautiful pyramid shape.

Food Juicy "Doru-yaki" and "Princess-yaki" made with white outside skirt.

The moment you take a bite, the juicy meat flavor bursts in your mouth! Our popular dish, "Doru-yaki," uses our special rock salt and carefully selected chicken, allowing the deliciousness to spread with every bite. Enjoy it with a touch of yuzu pepper for an extra flavor kick! Also, don't miss our "Princess-yaki," which lavishly features the white outside skirt from 10 chickens! This dish offers a tender, juicy texture and rich flavor that fills your mouth. It's a must-try for meat lovers!

SpacePerfect for large groups! Enjoy a fun time in a lively space where you can gather casually.

Our restaurant boasts a casual and warm atmosphere that's perfect for a lively gathering! It's an ideal spot for a fun night out with friends, a drink after work, or even a girls' night or reunion. The table seating is always buzzing with energy, and we can easily accommodate larger groups by connecting tables. Enjoy delicious food and drinks together, and create unforgettable memories! *Please note that the images of the interior are from our sister restaurant.*

DrinkEnjoy a complimentary drink on us! Please make a reservation using the coupon♪

As a reservation bonus, we offer a coupon for a complimentary first drink! Make sure to take advantage of this great deal. *Please note that this cannot be combined with other coupons or services, and is not valid during events.
