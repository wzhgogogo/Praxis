Food Kumamoto Horse Sashimi, a pride of our Kitasenju location

The moist, rich flavor and sweetness of the red meat will have you hooked after just one bite. Pair it with our delicious sour cocktails or shochu from Kyushu, and enjoy a taste journey through Kyushu.

SpaceEnjoy a relaxing time in our cool private rooms! We happily welcome daytime drinks!

We have private rooms available for 2 to a maximum of 30 people, providing a space where you can relax from the afternoon! It's also conveniently located near the station, so feel free to drop by.

Food ◆Start with This When You Visit Kimuraya◆

We offer three types of handmade gyoza, inspired by the flavors of Hakata street food! Enjoy them grilled, fried, or boiled, each with its own unique taste. Our gyoza features thin skin and a flavorful filling, making them light and easy to eat—perfect for enjoying as many as you like. They pair wonderfully with our signature Kyushu Umaka Sour and Kagoshima Yoka Tea Highball!

Food 2-Hour All-You-Can-Eat Specialty Main Dish Party Course from JPY 3,980!

Enjoy a fantastic banquet course with 2 hours of all-you-can-eat and 3 hours of all-you-can-drink at a reasonable price! Starting from JPY 3980, you can savor 7 delicious dishes. Choose from our free all-you-can-eat hot pots, including our famous Motsunabe and special Pork Shabu-Shabu, and indulge to your heart's content. Our all-you-can-drink menu includes draft beer and a variety of options to keep the drinks flowing. Relax in our comfortable tatami seating, perfect for long gatherings without feeling tired. We look forward to welcoming you in our cool, inviting space!
