Just a minute from Ryogoku Station, enjoy a hearty and luxurious chanko nabe in a serene traditional Japanese setting. Perfect for various gatherings!

At our restaurant, you can enjoy a luxurious selection of nine types of "Chanko Nabe" made with carefully chosen ingredients, along with a sashimi platter featuring fresh fish delivered directly from ...the market. Our flavorful hot pots are hearty and perfect for banquets. The sashimi is selected based on the day's catch and served at peak freshness. With both tatami seating and counter seats, our space is ideal for various occasions, including company parties and girls' gatherings. Experience a relaxed atmosphere where you can savor the authentic flavors that come from our long-standing tradition.

Food Perfect for any gathering! Featuring our signature "Yokozuna Chanko Nabe" in the "Ozeki Course".

Our pride, the Ozeki Course, features our signature Chanko Nabe, Yokozuna Chanko, as the main dish, along with a total of six satisfying items including sashimi and appetizers. It's perfect for various gatherings like company banquets or girls' parties. With its impressive presentation and balanced flavors, it's popular among a wide range of ages. You can also add a 2-hour all-you-can-drink option for an extra JPY 2,200, making it a great ally for organizers. Don't miss out on this value-packed and generous course!

SpaceJust a stone's throw from Ryogoku Station! Enjoy a relaxed atmosphere that's perfect for casual dining.

Just a minute's walk from Ryogoku Station, our cozy spot is perfect for a casual visit anytime. Out of our 30 seats, 28 are spacious tatami areas where you can relax comfortably. We also have 2 counter seats ideal for a quick break. Being so close to the station makes it easy to meet up with friends. Whether you're stopping by after work or on your day off, feel free to drop in!

Food Assorted Sashimi Platter from JPY 1,600 - Featuring the freshest catch of the day

We source fresh seafood from Toyosu Market, offering a daily selection of sashimi platters. Each platter is crafted based on the season and market availability, ensuring the best combinations for you. Pair it with our wide variety of Japanese sake to enhance the flavors of the ocean's bounty. During lunchtime, we also offer great value seafood set meals. Enjoy a luxurious experience with us, whether it's for lunch or dinner!

Food 9 Varieties of Chanko Nabe ◆ Packed with Ingredients and Flavorful Broth!

Our pride is the "Chanko Nabe" with a generous selection of 9 ingredients. Among them, the "Yokozuna Chanko" features incredibly fresh seafood and seasonal vegetables, making it a visually stunning dish. The rich broth, infused with the flavors of the ingredients, delivers deep umami in every bite. It's crafted with a focus on nutritional balance, making it a great choice for health-conscious diners. Come enjoy our special hot pot that warms both your body and soul!
