Experience the full charm of Sushi Planet with this satisfying course! Start with a selection of six unique appetizers, including corn potage, sushi-style potato salad, and grilled oysters in oil. Others Casual Sushi Tavern You Can Easily Visit

A popular sushi tavern from Musashi Kosugi, selected as a "Hot Restaurant 2025" by Tabelog, has just opened in Suidobashi! Enjoy authentic sushi made with red vinegar and tempura in a cozy, casual tavern style. It's perfect for a quick bite or a budget-friendly meal! Whether you're dining solo or with a group, it's a great spot for any occasion. Born from the desire to spread truly delicious sushi across the universe, this is the Sushi Planet. We can't wait to welcome you!

Food Using Edo-Style Red Vinegar Rice

All our sushi is made with a special red vinegar rice that has a mild tanginess and rich flavor. We offer authentic Edo-style sushi at reasonable prices, highlighting the harmony between the rice and the toppings. We take pride in our ingredients, selecting only the freshest tuna and white fish sourced daily. Come and experience the depth of flavor for yourself!

Food Tempura: From Appetizers to Sweets

Our signature tempura features seasonal ingredients lightly battered and fried to perfection. In addition to classic shrimp and vegetable options, we offer unique choices like cheese-filled sausages and simmered daikon, as well as even "dessert tempura." Enjoy our piping hot tempura, which pairs wonderfully with drinks! Experience sushi restaurant-style tempura in a casual, snack-like way.

Food Perfect for your post-Tokyo Dome activities!

Our signature "Planet Bottle Sour" features different flavors and names based on each color, perfect for showing off your favorite hue! Whether you're coming from a live show, a game, or an event, raise a glass with your "support color" in hand. We're conveniently located within walking distance from Tokyo Dome, making it easy to enjoy a little escape from the everyday while soaking in the afterglow of your event.
