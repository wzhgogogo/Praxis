Food [Matsusaka Beef Association Member] Our sourcing power is different! Enjoy authentic Matsusaka beef at a reasonable price.

<Designated Store for Matsusaka Beef> We want to spread the word about real Matsusaka beef! We source our beef directly from "designated farms" that are committed to meticulous breeding methods. Here, you can enjoy truly delicious Matsusaka beef that is not typically available in Tokyo, along with rare cuts that are hard to find elsewhere. By cutting out all middlemen, we offer prices that are about half of what you would pay in Mie Prefecture, where it originates! While it usually takes at least two weeks from slaughter to delivery, we can get it to you in as little as two days, ensuring unmatched freshness! Indulge in the finest "raw tongue" and experience the difference!

Food "All Meat is Hand-Cut by the Shop Owner: A Skilled Technique that Enhances Flavor."

Our restaurant takes pride in saying, "Our commitment to meat is the best!" We provide meat sourced in the best condition, served at its peak. All the meat we handle is carefully hand-cut by the owner in the shop. With skilled techniques, we assess the cuts and their condition, adjusting the cutting style and thickness accordingly. We only serve A5 and specialty-ranked meat that meets our high standards. Enjoy fresh dishes like yukhoe and aburi sashimi, as well as "smoked charcoal" that will impress even meat connoisseurs, and various other ways to enjoy it, including sukiyaki. The "floating meat," which has a big impact and is perfect for social media, is definitely worth seeing!

Food 【100% Matsusaka Beef】Matsusaka Beef Raw Hamburger 4 Pieces / 1 Serving 680 Yen

【1-minute walk from Suidobashi Station♪】We directly source Matsusaka beef from a "designated farm" that is thoroughly committed to its breeding methods. We offer cuts of Matsusaka beef that are truly delicious and not typically available in Tokyo, as well as rare cuts that are seldom tasted, all crafted into hamburgers by our skilled artisans in-house. Naturally, our hamburgers are made with 100% Matsusaka beef. Since we never freeze our products, you can enjoy super fresh raw hamburgers. Savor the inherent "sweetness" of Matsusaka beef. Please note that quantities are limited.

Space"Great Location Near Tokyo Dome & Station: Enjoy the Best Meat Casually"

Our store is just a minute from Suidobashi Station, right next to the station. It's also close to Tokyo Dome and LaQua, making it a great spot to visit after watching a baseball game or attending an event. The interior is centered around tables, providing comfortable seating that can be easily adjusted for the number of guests. You can enjoy the highest quality Matsusaka beef in a casual setting without any pretensions. We also serve lunch, offering a range of carefully crafted dishes such as 100% Matsusaka beef hamburgers (with no fillers), rare steak bowls, and Matsusaka beef curry, all at reasonable prices!
