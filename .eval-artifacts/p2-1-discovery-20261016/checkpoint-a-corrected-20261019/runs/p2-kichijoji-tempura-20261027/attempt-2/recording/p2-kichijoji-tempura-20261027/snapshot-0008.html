Tempura Specialty Shop "Kichijoji Tempura Wakayama" Featuring Seasonal Ingredients

Our restaurant, "Tempura Wakayama," is conveniently located just a short walk from the station. As you step inside, you'll find a cozy counter seating area with smooth jazz playing in the background, ...where you can hear the unique sound of tempura frying in high-quality oil right in front of you.
In addition to the casual counter seats, we also offer the only tatami mat tempura seating in the Tama area, perfect for gatherings, memorial services, or business meetings in a private room.

Food Our Strengths

Our restaurant sources seafood from one of the top specialty shops at Toyosu Market. We get our sweet shrimp from a dedicated shrimp wholesaler, while our vegetables are supplied directly from our own produce division at the auction. Additionally, we use ingredients that are delivered straight from various regions and from our own farm in Akishima.

SpaceThe Only Tatami Room Tempura in the Tama Area

In addition to our main counter with 9 seats, we also have the only completely private tatami room for tempura in the Tama area. In this horigotatsu private room, we will fry seasonal ingredients one by one at your pace right in front of you. It's perfect for business meetings, family gatherings, or memorial services, accommodating groups from 4 to a maximum of 9 people.

DrinkDrinks that Pair Perfectly with Tempura

We also run a sake shop, and our owner is not only a sake collector but also a certified sake sommelier. We offer a wide selection of premium and seasonal sake.
