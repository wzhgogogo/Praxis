A wooden hideaway in Ginza 7-chome. Enjoy carefully selected Wagyu beef at this sophisticated yakiniku restaurant.

Nestled in a charming wooden hideaway on a back street in Ginza 7-chome, "Yakiniku Date" offers a unique dining experience. We carefully select high-quality Wagyu beef, not just focusing on brands, bu...t on the best available at the time. Enjoy a relaxed atmosphere perfect for dates, anniversaries, or important business meals. Take your time to savor our special dishes along with the signature Morioka Cold Noodles to finish off your meal.

Food Casually enjoy each cut of A5 Wagyu selected and sourced from across the country.

We carefully source high-quality A5 grade Wagyu from all over the country. Our selection includes classic cuts like short rib and loin, allowing you to enjoy the natural flavors and textures of the meat. Savor these exquisite dishes that truly highlight the essence of the ingredients.

Food High-Quality Wagyu, including Sirloin and Spencer Roll, à la carte

Indulge in carefully selected A5 Wagyu beef available a la carte. Our top recommendations include premium cuts like Strip Loin, Spencer Roll, and Tenderloin. You won't want to miss the exquisite marbling and sweetness of the high-quality fat that comes with A5 grade. We take pride in offering a lineup that lets you savor a truly special taste. Additionally, we serve unique dishes crafted using French and Italian techniques. Our menu changes with the seasons, so you can look forward to discovering delightful new plates each time you visit.

DrinkCarefully selected wines from around the world that pair well with meat dishes. Also featuring freshly squeezed Tiger Makgeolli.

We carefully select wines mainly from France and Italy, along with a variety of options from around the world. We have about 10 types that pair perfectly with our savory meat dishes. Our popular "Tiger Makgeolli" is freshly squeezed and served after you place your order. Why not enjoy a meal with a drink that suits your taste?

SpaceAccessible from 2 stations ◆ Enjoy your meal in a retro-modern space. Fully private rooms available.

Our restaurant is conveniently located with easy access from both Shimbashi and Ginza stations. Inside, you'll find a warm atmosphere created by brick decor and antique lighting, giving it a modern retro vibe. Enjoy your meal in a relaxed setting that invites you to unwind. On the first floor, we have fully private rooms for 2 to 4 guests, while the second floor features spacious table seating with high ceilings that create an open feel. Whether it's for business meetings or sightseeing meals, we cater to a wide range of occasions.
