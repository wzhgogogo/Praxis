5-Minute Walk from Shinbashi Station ◆ Enjoy Fresh Sushi and Sashimi with Drinks ◎ Perfect for Parties with All-You-Can-Drink Course

At our restaurant, directly operated by a long-established fresh fish shop in Hakodate, you can enjoy incredibly fresh seafood dishes at reasonable prices. We offer a variety of options, including sus...hi, sashimi, and seafood bowls, perfect for sharing!

Don't miss our selection of Hokkaido craft beers and weekly changing sake, which are great for alcohol enthusiasts.

We also have all-you-can-drink courses ideal for parties and affordable "chill drinking" sets.

Our newly created open-air space, designed like a food stall, provides a refreshing atmosphere.

Whether you're looking for a casual solo drink or a lively gathering with friends, we have the perfect setting for every occasion!

Food Enjoy a course with all-you-can-drink! Fresh seafood from our directly-operated Hakodate sushi restaurant.

Our restaurant, directly operated by a long-established seafood shop in Hakodate, takes pride in our expert selection skills and the freshness of our ingredients. Enjoy sushi and sashimi made with fresh seafood, perfect as a side dish for your drinks! We also offer courses with all-you-can-drink options, making it ideal for parties. Come and indulge in the fresh flavors of the sea!

Food Perfect for a quick drink after work! Sashimi & small dishes with a drink set.

How about our "Choi Nomi Set" for a quick drink? Starting at just JPY 1,500 to JPY 2,000, you can enjoy fresh seafood and drinks! Set A includes small dishes and simmered fish, plus a choice of either 5 pieces of sashimi or 5 sushi rolls, along with beer or highballs for a luxurious experience. Set B takes it up a notch with edamame and an assortment of fried snacks. Perfect for a drink after work, so come give it a try!

DrinkA Must-See for Beer & Sake Lovers! 5 Types of Craft Beer and Weekly Sake Selection

We offer a selection of 5 craft beers from Hokkaido, each with its own unique flavor profile for you to enjoy. Plus, we feature 15 to 20 different types of sake that change weekly. With seasonal varieties, there's always something new to discover on each visit. Whether you're a beer lover or a sake enthusiast, our lineup is sure to satisfy!

Space【Near the Station】Newly Established Open Space for a Relaxed Atmosphere ◎ Perfect for Group Gatherings and Parties!

Our restaurant features an open space that offers a relaxed atmosphere for everyone. The counter seats provide a front-row view of our chefs in action, making it a special spot to enjoy the live cooking experience. Many regulars love to drop by solo, which adds to the charm of the place. In our newly created open-air stall area, you can expect a lively and fun vibe! Whether you're stopping by for a quick drink after work or hosting a welcome party or gathering, we cater to all kinds of occasions. Come and enjoy!
