Premium Yukhoe x Rare Tongue x Donabe Rice at a Hidden Yakiniku Spot, Just 2 Minutes from Nakameguro Station

The specialty here is Black Wagyu Yukke. We serve A5 rank female beef and top-quality offal directly sourced from the prestigious Kabasawa Shoten in the Tokyo meat market, cut and seasoned perfectly f...or each part. Enjoy rare cuts like black tongue and Wagyu harami. The lively counter where you can grill right in front of you is perfect for dates. We also have table seating for groups of four or more, making it great for larger gatherings. Open for lunch every day! Just a 2-minute walk from Nakameguro Station.

Food A Place Where You Can Enjoy Premium Wagyu Beef Yukhoe!

We serve raw meat with the approval of the health department, so you can enjoy it with peace of mind! Our Yukhoe, which you won't find easily elsewhere, is served fresh and is our top seller! It's so fresh that even those who usually dislike it might end up loving it! Be sure to order it when you visit us!

Food Special Meat Sourced from Top-Quality Wholesalers

We source high-quality beef offal from the top-notch Kamasawa Shoten located in the Tokyo Meat Market. Enjoy our specially selected cuts and seasoned grilled meat to your heart's content!

Food Delicious rice cooked fresh upon order

We source Koshihikari rice directly from producers in Minami Uonuma, Niigata Prefecture. Enjoy our freshly cooked rice, made to order!
