SpaceChic and Stylish Space with Private Rooms ◆ Accommodating Individuals to Groups

Step into our chic and stylish space, where a warm atmosphere invites you to linger a little longer. We have two popular private rooms that can be connected to accommodate up to eight guests. Our tables are also easy to combine, making it flexible for group gatherings. For those dining solo or couples, we have two cozy counter seats that are perfect for a more intimate experience. Enjoy a relaxing moment in our calm and inviting setting.

Food Enjoy premium rare cuts at a great value! Made possible through our carefully selected sourcing routes.

We want our customers to enjoy high-quality meat and rare cuts at reasonable prices! That's why we put a lot of effort into sourcing our ingredients. Our chef, who has trained at several renowned restaurants, carefully selects meat only from trusted suppliers. Take your time and savor the special flavors we have to offer.

DrinkCarefully Selected Wines to Enjoy with Yakiniku ◆ Our Staff Will Recommend the Perfect Glass

At our restaurant, you can enjoy the perfect pairing of grilled meat and wine. We have a wide selection of carefully chosen wines, allowing you to savor them alongside your meal. Our staff is also happy to recommend the ideal wine to complement your meat dishes, so feel free to ask for suggestions!

Food The irresistible flavor and smoothness of beef bone broth! Enjoy "Morioka Cold Noodles" in Tokyo.

At Yakiniku Reimen Daiju, we proudly serve Morioka-style cold noodles. This dish features a rich flavor from beef bone broth and an irresistible texture! Enjoy a generous bowl that brings the authentic taste of Morioka right here in Tokyo.
