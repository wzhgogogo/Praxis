【3-minute walk from Kichijoji Station】Enjoy Edo-style sushi made with carefully selected fresh fish, along with delicious sake and side dishes. Open all day from lunch to dinner!

Our restaurant offers sushi made with fresh fish sourced every morning from Toyosu. In addition to nigiri and sashimi, we have a variety of grilled dishes, tempura, and seasonal ingredient courses. We...'re open for continuous service from lunch, so feel free to drop by for a late lunch or afternoon drinks!

Enjoy a stylish time at our sushi bar paired with daily selections of sake and other beverages.

The interior features a wood-based design with counter seating, tables, and tatami rooms, making it perfect for solo diners, dates, or business meetings.

Food "Daytime Drinks, Late Lunch, Early Dinner: Open All Day from Afternoon to Night"

Our store is open without breaks from 12:00 PM to 10:30 PM. We can accommodate late lunches and early dinners, so please feel free to visit at your convenience. You can order from the entire menu from the time we open, allowing you to enjoy daytime drinks and parties as well. Additionally, during lunch hours, we recommend menu items such as scattered sushi, seafood bowls, and lunch nigiri.

Food "Enjoying Sake and Side Dishes, Ending with Exquisite Edomae Sushi Showcasing the Chef's Skills"

The sushi, prepared using traditional Edomae techniques, features fresh fish sourced every morning from Toyosu Market. Additionally, to cater to various customer requests, we offer a variety of grilled dishes and tempura. We recommend enjoying our a la carte dishes and drinks, and finishing off with sushi. Furthermore, we have three types of course menus that highlight seasonal ingredients, as well as carefully prepared hairy crabs available year-round. Please enjoy the sushi, meticulously crafted by our chefs, along with seasonal dishes.

Drink"From Classic to Seasonal, a Wide Variety of Fresh Sake Delivered Daily"

We have carefully selected a variety of sake that pairs well with sushi. Every day, we procure fresh sake from local sake shops, offering a complete selection of 12 types. In addition to classic brands, we also have many seasonal sakes that reflect the changing seasons. Besides sake, we can also provide shochu and whiskey by the bottle. We also offer beer, wine, and sour drinks, so please enjoy your favorite drink alongside our proud sushi◎

SpaceSpacious and Tranquil Space ◆ Perfect for Individuals and Groups alike!

In a calm interior characterized by wood grain, we offer a variety of seating options to accommodate diverse scenes. At the counter, where you can relax alone, please enjoy conversations with the chef about the day's recommendations and suggestions. The tatami and table seating are recommended for small group dining, family gatherings, or for those who want to take their time with a course meal. Additionally, two tatami rooms can be connected to serve as a private room for 5 to 10 people. We invite you to use our space for gatherings with friends, dates, or business meetings.
