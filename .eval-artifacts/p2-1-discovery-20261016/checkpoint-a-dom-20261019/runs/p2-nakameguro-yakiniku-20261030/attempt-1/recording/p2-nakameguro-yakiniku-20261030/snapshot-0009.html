A hidden gem restaurant by the pioneer of aged meat, awarded one Michelin star for seven consecutive years.

Located along the Meguro River, a famous cherry blossom spot in Nakameguro, Tokyo, "Nakusei" is a restaurant specializing in aged meat.The entrance is situated behind "Chokusou Nama Hormone Nakasei" a...long the river.
Combining the expertise of meat specialists with the long-term aging techniques, we create unique dishes using domestically sourced Wagyu beef.
Our skilled artisans pay meticulous attention in our exclusive aging chamber, providing the ultimate meat that has been aged for two months, concentrating its aroma and flavor.
Enjoy a luxurious moment in a calm, hidden atmosphere.

Food Pioneer of Aged Meat

About 10 years ago, when aged meat was not as popular as it is today, the butcher shop "Nakusei Honten" opened in Denenchofu, Tokyo. Our sister store opened in 2013. We carefully select domestic black wagyu beef that can withstand long-term aging in our own aging facility, where it is aged for about two months. During this time, our artisans meticulously manage the temperature, humidity, and bacteria to enhance the umami and flavor of the ingredients. The chefs then serve the meat simply as steaks or grilled dishes, ensuring that the natural qualities of the ingredients are preserved.

Food "Rare Cuts Available Thanks to Whole Animal Aging"

At our shop, we age about 40 cows annually and offer rare cuts that cannot be found elsewhere, such as the "neck tie," which comes from only about 400 grams of the rear lamp of a single cow, and the "marukawa," which yields only about 3 kilograms per cow. Each cut is unique and flavorful, catering to a wide variety of customer preferences. While there are many parts that you may not be familiar with, please feel free to ask our concierge for more information.

Food "We always have 8 to 10 types of aged meat available in our showcase."

In the showcase, we always have 4 to 5 types of red meat and 4 to 5 types of marbled aged meat available. When you place your order, we will guide you to the showcase. Our meat consultant will introduce the characteristics of each cut, so please choose your preferred cut and doneness. The flavor, aroma, and texture can vary greatly depending on the cut, so we recommend trying different ones. Additionally, even within the same cut, there are individual differences in the cattle, which means the taste can change each time.

Space"Relaxed Atmosphere: Enjoy Meals at Counter and Table Seats"

Our establishment exudes a calm atmosphere reminiscent of an adult hideaway. We have five counter seats where you can enjoy the kitchen's activities up close, making it perfect for a date. Additionally, our 18 table seats offer a luxurious experience where you can leisurely savor steak and wine.
