Food "Carefully Selected Wagyu and Exquisite Craftsmanship"

At Beef Kitchen, we procure high-quality black wagyu beef and meticulously prepare it with precise knife work according to the cut, ensuring that our customers can enjoy delicious grilled meat without any stress. We also take pride in our commitment to handmade dishes, from appetizers to desserts, offering a variety of enticing a la carte options. Please enjoy our carefully selected drinks, including a wide range of wines, makgeolli, sours, and sake, paired perfectly with our meats and dishes.

Food Special Course & Great Value Lunch Menu

We offer a variety of courses featuring popular cuts like "Upper Tongue" and "Thick Sirloin," as well as rare parts of Wagyu such as "Wagyu Fillet," "Wagyu Nigiri," and "Yukke." Each course is packed with our commitment to freshness, carefully selected ingredients, and expert cooking techniques. Don't miss out on our signature beef kitchen courses! During lunch hours, we also have great deals on Yakiniku set meals and lunch sets. Perfect for any gathering, come enjoy a delicious meal with us!

ServiceLet us take care of your anniversaries and birthdays!

We can prepare a special celebration plate for you with a reservation in advance (tax included JPY 1,100). Seasonal fruits and ice cream are beautifully arranged to help you celebrate your special day. Whether it's for friends, a partner, family, or cherished companions, enjoy a wonderful time at Beef Kitchen with high-quality meat, delicious dishes, and warm hospitality.

Space"Chic and Stylish Space for Various Scenes"

Our stylish and chic interior features comfortable sofa seating, creating a relaxed atmosphere perfect for adults. Whether you're enjoying a summer get-together, treating yourself to a rewarding lunch, celebrating a birthday, or just having a casual drink during the day, our space is ideal for any occasion. With spacious tables that don’t feel cramped and a sophisticated ambiance, you can savor melt-in-your-mouth Wagyu beef while enjoying a wonderful time.
