ChefMasayuki Sano - Japanese Kaiseki Cuisine Reflecting on the Seasons

Born in Tokyo in 1969. Entered the culinary world at the age of 21 and trained in the United States for two years. After returning to Japan, he learned about ingredients in Hokkaido and trained for ten years at a traditional Japanese restaurant in Kyoto. He has since gained a wide range of experience, including Italian, French, and overseeing school cafeteria menus. In 2016, he opened "Shinsen Kappo Sano-ya." He creates dishes that are not bound by tradition, incorporating techniques such as espuma and liquid nitrogen, while focusing on traditional styles of kaiseki that emphasize dashi and seasonal ingredients, providing a culinary experience that delights the eyes, palate, and heart.

Food "Carefully Crafted: Savoring Broth and Ingredients"

The eight-course meal served in a masu adorned with seasonal flowers features旬の食材 centered around vegetables. The specialty, dried herring, is a flavorful delicacy that has been carefully cooked over the course of a week.
