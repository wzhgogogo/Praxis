Just a 3-minute walk from Okachimachi Station, check out the popular yakitori spot "Tori Kei" with its third location. Enjoy a wide variety of yakitori, sake, and wine!

Nestled in a back alley of Okachimachi, "Ueno Kuromon Torikei" is the third branch of the popular chicken restaurant "Torikei," known for its reservations that never seem to stop. This location is pro...duced by Toshimasa Ozawa, who honed his skills at the renowned "Sumibi Yakitori Soten" in Otsuka.
The menu features three types of chicken: Daisen Chicken, Ehime's Himekko Chicken, and duck, all expertly grilled by skilled artisans. A wide selection of Japanese sake and wine is also available. Enjoy the counter seats for a chat with the owner, or opt for private rooms that are perfect for dates, business meetings, or gatherings.

Space"Torikei" Opens Its Third Location: "Ueno Kuromon Torikei"

Toshimasa Ozawa apprenticed at the renowned Otsuka restaurant "Sumibiyaki Tori Soten," where he acquired grilling techniques and the necessary know-how for independence. He then confidently opened his first store, "Tori Kei Honten," in Yushima. Following that, he opened his second store, "Ueno Hirokoji," which was featured in a certain gourmet magazine and became a popular restaurant with constant reservations. He then opened his third store, "Ueno Kuromon Tori Kei," in Okachimachi. The flavor of his hand-prepared yakitori is certainly noteworthy, but attention is also being drawn to his management policy, which is not confined to a specific genre.

Drink"Wide Selection of Rare Labels: Carefully Selected Japanese Sake and Sommelier-Selected Wines"

Our staff, certified as sake sommeliers, has carefully selected a wide variety of popular sake and rare brands. We offer exceptional choices such as "Sugata," handpicked by the chef, and "Daishinshu," known for its impressive lingering finish, all of which enhance the flavor of our yakitori. Additionally, we have a selection of wines chosen with a focus on pairing with our dishes. The wines, curated by our sommelier, range from French to domestic varieties, providing a rich and diverse selection. Each one is the perfect glass to highlight the nuances of our cuisine.

Space"【Private Room Available】A Versatile Space Perfect for Various Occasions"

The interior of the restaurant, which exudes a soft atmosphere with a wood grain design, features a U-shaped counter with 16 seats, one table for 4 to 6 people, and a semi-private room at the back of the restaurant that can accommodate 8 people. The counter seats are perfect for enjoying conversations with the owner or relaxing with friends, making them truly special spots. There is also a semi-private room, making it ideal for business meetings or dining with important guests. As these seats are popular, we recommend confirming availability by phone in advance when planning your visit.

Food Main Dish: Soboro Don, Perfect as a Main Course or a Finale.

Depending on the day, we change the parts of chicken we use, and our pride and joy, the grilled chicken skewers, are cooked over Kishu Binchotan charcoal. Of course, our carefully selected side dishes pair perfectly with our drinks. Among them, our signature dish, the "Soboro Don," is a carefully crafted item made with Ehime free-range chicken, duck, and Daisen chicken. It's so delicious that you won't get tired of eating it every day. It's so popular that some people come specifically just for this dish!
