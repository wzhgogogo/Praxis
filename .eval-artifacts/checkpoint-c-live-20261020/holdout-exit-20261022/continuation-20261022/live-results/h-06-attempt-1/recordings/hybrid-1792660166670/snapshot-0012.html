The nostalgic bathhouse-themed interior creates a cozy atmosphere, perfect for girls' nights out, dates, or large gatherings!

Food Everyone, young and old, loves our affordable "Side Dishes"!

At our restaurant, we offer a wide variety of dishes for everyone to enjoy! From classic snacks to seasonal specialties and unique desserts you can only find here, our skilled chefs prepare exquisite meals with a personal touch! If you're looking for affordable yakitori, all-you-can-drink options, and the popular tap highball around Ikebukuro Station, come visit us at Taisyu Syuusen Terumae!

DrinkQuick Drinks with Tap Highball & Grilled Chicken Perfect for a quick drink or solo dining!

Enjoy our customizable draft highballs and grilled chicken skewers made in-house. We also offer great value all-you-can-drink courses starting from JPY 2,500, perfect for banquets! Our spacious and open atmosphere is designed for everyone, whether you're dining solo or with a group. Plus, we're conveniently located near the station, making it an ideal meeting spot!

SpaceThe retro space inspired by a public bath is perfect for a wide range of occasions, from banquets to dates. Private reservations available!

Our restaurant features a unique design that recreates the atmosphere of a public bathhouse, with flooring reminiscent of a changing room and tiled walls that evoke the feeling of a bath! The tables can be connected, making it perfect for both small and large gatherings. We can accommodate private reservations for groups of 80 to 120 people, making it ideal for large events and various banquets. Whether you're enjoying drinks with friends, going on a date, or hosting a girls' night out, our space is perfect for all occasions!
