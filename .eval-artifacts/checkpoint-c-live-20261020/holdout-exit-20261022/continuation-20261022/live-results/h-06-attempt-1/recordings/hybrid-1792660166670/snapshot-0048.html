Just 2 minutes from Shimokitazawa Station! Fresh seasonal fish and homemade specialty dishes straight from the market! A variety of rare Japanese sake available, and daytime drinking is welcome!

Enjoy the king of white fish, Nodoguro, along with fresh live shrimp, Kombu-wrapped grilled Kyoto chicken, and our homemade chicken paitan oden, all showcasing our delicious handmade dishes.We also of...fer a variety of daily fresh fish delivered directly from the market and seasonal limited-time menus!
Our manager has carefully selected over 20 rare types of Japanese sake, along with more than 20 popular umeshu (plum wine) and fruit wines for women!
We have courses that include all-you-can-drink options. Whether for a date at the counter or a banquet in a private room, this is a perfect "slightly grown-up izakaya" for any occasion!

Our environmental and social efforts

Using sustainable ingredients

We use aged bamboo shoots, produced from abandoned bamboo forests by LOCAL BAMBOO, as part of our menu! By consuming these shoots, you help solve bamboo-related issues and contribute to environmental conservation. Not only are they eco-friendly, but they are also made from domestically sourced Moso bamboo, which has about seven times the umami and 9.6 times the richness compared to Chinese Ma bamboo. These delicious aged bamboo shoots are even featured in ANA's first-class in-flight meals. We invite you to try them!

Food "Direct from the Market! Seasonal Fish" Specialty 'Nodoguro' and 'Live Shrimp Sashimi'

We are committed to freshness and offer a wide variety of dishes made with seasonal seafood that is delivered directly from the market every day! Among our recommendations is the "King of White Fish," the 'Nodoguro'! Its melt-in-your-mouth texture and rich, flavorful fat are simply exquisite◎ Another specialty is the 'Dancing Live Shrimp,' which is caught from the tank after you place your order! Have you ever eaten live shrimp? The sweetness and bouncy texture will surely amaze you◎ Please note that our recommended menu changes daily based on our supply, so we look forward to welcoming you!

Food Charcoal-Grilled "Jidori" Chicken & Salt-Baked Chicken with Homemade Chicken Paitan Oden!

In addition to fresh fish, our restaurant boasts a lineup of signature dishes! The "Salt-Baked Kombu-Wrapped Kyoto Red Chicken" is slow-cooked to perfection after you place your order, so it takes a bit of time, but one bite will definitely make you think, "It's worth the 30-minute wait!" Our homemade "Chicken Bone Broth Oden," made with over 10 hours of simmering, is also a huge hit! We highly recommend the daikon radish, which bursts with flavorful broth. Don't miss out on our "Smoky Grilled" dishes, which pair perfectly with drinks!

Drink"Over 20 Rare Local Sakes from Across the Country Available at All Times! A Delight for Sake Lovers!"

The store manager personally selects over 20 types of fine and rare sake from all over Japan! We also have rare limited-time and limited-quantity sake, so it's first come, first served! The selection changes depending on the season and time, so our lineup is always changing! (About half of it gets replaced every two weeks!) Please feel free to visit us anytime to enjoy! If you have preferences or need recommendations to pair with your dishes, don't hesitate to ask our staff!

Space[Fully Private Rooms] A Lively and Relaxing Izakaya Space [Daytime Drinking OK]

The interior, featuring a chic wooden design, creates a lively yet relaxing izakaya atmosphere. We offer not only tables but also counter seats perfect for casual dates, and spacious tatami seating for banquets and gatherings. Our popular private rooms add to the charm, making it easy to enjoy everything from casual visits to various events. We also welcome daytime drinkers! If you're looking to savor delicious food and drinks even during the day, come check us out!
