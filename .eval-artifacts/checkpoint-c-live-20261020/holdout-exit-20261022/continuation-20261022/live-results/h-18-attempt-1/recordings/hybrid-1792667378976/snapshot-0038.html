Just 2 minutes from Shimokitazawa Station! Enjoy the delightful fluffy texture of our exquisite "Square Okonomiyaki" made with professional skill.

In the bustling area of Shimokitazawa, you'll find the beloved establishment, Nanban-tei. When it comes to our okonomiyaki, the "square okonomiyaki" is a must-mention! After much trial and error, our ...owner from Kansai has perfected this unique square shape to deliver the most delicious okonomiyaki. We use carefully selected ingredients that trap air for a fluffy texture. Watch as our skilled chefs cook each one right at your table, showcasing their professional techniques. Enjoy the taste from the moment it's served, and savor every bite!

Food Fluffy and Hot! Delicious Square Okonomiyaki That Captures All the Umami!

Not only loved by locals in Shimokita, but also adored by many okonomiyaki enthusiasts, the okonomiyaki from [Nanbantei]! Our okonomiyaki is a unique square shape that you won't find elsewhere. It has a fluffy texture that captures the deliciousness of carefully selected ingredients... all wrapped up in that square form! Each one is grilled by our staff right at your table. With eggs and sauce... the flavor crafted by our owner, who hails from the Kansai region, is truly authentic and uncompromising. We invite you to try this deliciousness for yourself!

Drink"When it comes to okonomiyaki, it's definitely beer! Enjoying divine foam made by a super expert◎"

Bite into a piping hot okonomiyaki and take a sip of beer! At our restaurant, Nanbantei, we take pride in pairing the best companion for okonomiyaki, draft beer. We are a "Premium Super Expert Store" certified by Suntory! This title is awarded only to establishments that are thoroughly committed to deliciousness and serve their products in the ultimate condition. While there are many places that serve Suntory products, only a handful are Super Expert Stores. Enjoy the creamy foam unique to the perfect bubbles and experience the best balance!

ServiceThe Essence of Teppanyaki: Enjoy the Unmatched Skill of Professional Chefs!

The cooking method alone can greatly change the texture and deliciousness of "okonomiyaki and teppanyaki"! At our restaurant, Nanbantei, our skilled staff take responsibility to bring you the best flavor. We bring the ingredients to your table and our staff will cook it from scratch right in front of you. You can enjoy the professional techniques that leave no room for deception, right at your table. Watching the cooking process while we explain the steps will surely enhance your appetite—it's truly an entertainment experience! Please enjoy the visually appealing and skillful cooking!

SpaceA Lineup of Many Signs! A Famous Okonomiyaki Restaurant Frequented by Celebrities and Connoisseurs◎

Upon entering the store, what catches your eye is the array of signs lined up! Our establishment, "Nanban-tei," is a renowned okonomiyaki restaurant frequented by famous personalities and gourmets alike. Many customers have been coming here for years. Once you try our okonomiyaki, it’s unforgettable! The retro atmosphere of the place makes it a welcoming spot that you’ll want to drop by casually. This inviting ambiance is one of the reasons why it is loved. Please feel free to visit us without any hesitation!
