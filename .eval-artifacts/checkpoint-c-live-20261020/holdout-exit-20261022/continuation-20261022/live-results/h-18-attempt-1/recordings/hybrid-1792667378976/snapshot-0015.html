Just a 5-minute walk from Shimokitazawa Station, enjoy Kumamoto's fresh horse sashimi and water-boiled beef offal hot pot in a charming traditional house. Private rooms for 2 to 4 guests are also available.

Dondaku, a long-established Kyushu cuisine restaurant in Hakata, has been serving its traditional flavors for 40 years. Enjoy Kumamoto's fresh horse sashimi, Fukuoka's famous sesame mackerel, and beef... offal hot pot, all paired with Kyushu shochu and local sake.
The interior retains the charm of an old folk house, featuring a U-shaped counter for close interaction with the chefs, private rooms for 2 to 4 guests, a sunken kotatsu seating area for up to 12, and an open veranda seating. Whether you're dining for two or hosting a gathering with friends, you can enjoy a meal that suits your group size and mood. We also offer courses with all-you-can-drink options.

Space"Private Room Inspired by a Hidden Retreat"

As you proceed down the hallway and pass through the curtain at the very end, you will find a tatami room that evokes the feeling of a secret base. Inside, there is a sunken kotatsu table for four, which can be reserved for two or more people. Please note that same-day reservations are not accepted, so we kindly ask for prior contact. We also offer various seating options, including table seats, sunken kotatsu seats, terrace seating, and an open counter in a U-shape surrounding the kitchen.

DrinkA Grand Gathering of Sake from All Over Kyushu!!

We carefully select and procure sake and shochu through our unique routes that are characteristic of Kyushu taverns. Kyushu is famous for shochu, and we offer a wide variety of shochu, while our sake selection mainly features rice from Kyushu and other rice-producing regions across Japan, with some items available for a limited time only!

Food "Fresh Horse Meat Sashimi from Kumamoto with a Sweet Flavor"

Kumamoto's representative local dish. The horse meat, sourced through unique channels, is fresh and has a natural sweetness. This dish features a perfect balance of marbled red meat, along with the rare and valuable "tategami" (mane), making it a delightful plate.

Food "The Taste of a Prosperous Hakata Shop in Tokyo....."

Utilizing the skills honed at a bustling restaurant in Hakata, this Kyushu tavern offers ingredients and flavors from all over Kyushu. The summer recommendation is "Wagyu Motsuyaki." Here, only carefully selected Maruchou of A4.5 grade is used, and it is enjoyed with Kyushu's charred soy sauce for a blissful taste. Definitely a perfect companion for beer this season!!!
