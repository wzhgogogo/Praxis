Enjoy fresh seafood and seasonal delicacies sourced daily, paired with local sake from across the country. Private rooms available.

Located just a short walk from Gotanda Station, Umibozu offers a fantastic selection of fresh seafood dishes. True to its name, the restaurant serves a variety of seasonal fish and high-quality ingred...ients. Not only does it feature traditional Japanese cuisine, but it also introduces new seasonal dishes that are highly recommended. You'll also find a wide range of Chinese dishes on the menu. The generous portions relative to the prices reflect the chef's dedication and passion. Umibozu aims to be a warm oasis in Gotanda, welcoming guests with heartfelt service and hospitality.

Food All seafood is sourced directly from the production area.

Our chef uses fresh, locally sourced ingredients to create exquisite dishes that will delight you. Enjoy unique flavors that you can only find here! We offer a variety of courses, from casual izakaya options to authentic Chinese cuisine.

DrinkAll-You-Can-Drink Course Available!

We offer a carefully selected range of premium sake from all over the country. You can find some rare seasonal brews that are hard to come by, so feel free to ask our staff for recommendations. They pair perfectly with our fresh seafood delivered directly from the fishing port.

SpaceWe have private rooms available! You can also reserve the entire floor for groups of 50 or more!

Enjoy a relaxing time in our private rooms, where a serene atmosphere infused with Japanese elegance awaits you. The indirect lighting and meticulously designed interiors create a sophisticated vibe. You can have a great time in complete privacy, free from distractions. Our private rooms accommodate small groups of 6 up to a maximum of 50 guests, making them perfect for business meetings, dining, dates, or special anniversaries.
