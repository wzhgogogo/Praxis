【1 minute from Meguro Station】Enjoy delicious pan-fried gyoza and authentic Chinese cuisine at great prices! All-you-can-eat and drink for JPY 5,000♪ Perfect for year-end and New Year parties!

This restaurant offers authentic Chinese cuisine crafted by chefs who have trained in the field, all at reasonable prices! Enjoy a wide range of dishes, including the founder's special feathered gyoza... and juicy xiaolongbao that burst with flavor. For gatherings of four or more, we recommend our all-you-can-eat and drink course for JPY 5,000, featuring 90 dishes and 30 drink options! The clean and inviting interior includes table seating and private rooms suitable for various occasions, accommodating everything from small get-togethers to large private parties.

Food Crispy and Juicy♪ Must-Try Gyoza with a Perfect Crunch!

The original羽根つき餃子 (winged dumplings) created by the founder of Kamata【Nihao】is an irresistible dish with a crispy exterior and juicy interior. The ingredients, including cabbage and beef, are carefully cut with a knife to maintain their texture. They pair perfectly with beer, and once you try them, you'll be hooked on their deliciousness!

Food "Juicy Soup Dumplings That Burst with Flavor and Exquisite Mapo Tofu with a Spicy Kick◎"

One of the most popular dim sum dishes is the soup dumpling (xiaolongbao). When you bite into it, the hot meat juice bursts in your mouth! Enjoy the rich flavor created by the perfect balance of spiciness, aroma, and saltiness! Additionally, I recommend the mapo tofu, which offers a deep umami flavor amidst its numbing spiciness.

Food "Outstanding Value All-You-Can-Eat and Drink Course for 5,000 Yen! Perfect for Various Banquets!"

We offer five courses that you can choose from based on your budget and occasion! All courses come with unlimited drinks, making them perfect for your gatherings. Our especially popular all-you-can-eat and drink course allows you to order from 90 different dishes and 30 types of drinks for just 5,000 yen! You can fully enjoy our restaurant's proud Chinese cuisine and drinks to your heart's content!

Space"New Year's Party Reservations Welcome! From Small Groups to Large Gatherings!"

Our store is just a 1-minute walk from JR Meguro Station, making it very accessible. We have a clean and tidy interior with table seating and private rooms available. We can accommodate a wide range of events, from small gatherings to large parties! Please consider us for your reunions, workplace welcome and farewell parties, year-end parties, New Year parties, and other events!
