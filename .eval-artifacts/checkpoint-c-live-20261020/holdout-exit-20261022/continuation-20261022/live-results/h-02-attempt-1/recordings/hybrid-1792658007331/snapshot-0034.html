Drink"We offer a wide variety of natural wines!"

The chef has personally selected a variety of Italian natural wines, including white, red, rosé, and orange! Each bottle is unfiltered and unclarified, allowing you to savor the unique characteristics of the grapes! We hope that wine lovers will especially enjoy this selection. If you're unsure about which wine to pair with your meal, please feel free to ask our staff for recommendations!

Space"We offer a daily changing menu of Italian cuisine in various forms."

We offer dishes based on Italian regional cuisine, incorporating elements of Japanese food culture! We have carpaccio inspired by sashimi, thick slices of beef prosciutto [bresaola], and the famous Roman dish saltimbocca made with fish! We prepare a daily changing menu with various options.

ServiceThe main attraction is the dishes being prepared right in front of you♪ A wine bistro that delights the eyes!

The counter where you can watch the dishes being prepared right in front of you is the best seat in the house! You can enjoy sipping wine and having conversations with the chef while taking in the sights and aromas... Many guests find themselves wanting to order something just by watching! There are friendly staff members who are happy to answer any questions you have about the dishes and which wines pair well with them!

Food "We Have Prepared Appetizers to Excite Your Expectations!"

We offer carefully crafted dishes and small wines as appetizers to our customers. We hope that our customers can experience our concept of "pairing food and wine" right from the first appetizer! This might be the opportunity for you to fall in love with wine!
