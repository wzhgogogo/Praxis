Restaurant highlights

【2-Minute Walk from Sangenjaya Station】Our pizzaiolo bakes pizzas with love.

This restaurant proudly serves pizzas baked in a wood-fired oven. As you enter, you'll see a Japanese-made wood oven where the pizzaiolo is busy crafting delicious pizzas. Enjoy our signature dishes, ...including Neapolitan pizza, homemade gnocchi, and grilled Angus beef and Iwakan pork, all paired perfectly with Italian wine.

We also have terrace seating, allowing you to relax and enjoy your meal in a spacious atmosphere.

Don't forget to try our homemade desserts, so you can savor every moment, even until the last coffee!
