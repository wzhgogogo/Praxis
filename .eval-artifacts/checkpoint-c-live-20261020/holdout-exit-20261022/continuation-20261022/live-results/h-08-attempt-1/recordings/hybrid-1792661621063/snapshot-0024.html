Just 3 minutes from Sanchō Station! Experience authentic seafood Italian cuisine with the world's best pasta recipes and top-quality brand fish!

Hidden away in Sangenjaya, "Pescheria" offers a unique dining experience. The biggest draw is the exquisite menu crafted by the first champion of the Pasta World Championship, paired with the finest b...rand fish "Aimi no Megumi" delivered directly from Oita Prefecture.
Southern Italian cuisine shines in its simplicity, leaving no room for deception. You must try the powerful dish that brings out the umami of the fresh fish to its fullest, along with the world-renowned pasta.
We promise a cheerful and fulfilling culinary experience that feels like visiting an Italian port town.

Food A Professional Fish Expert Says "The Best in Japan!" Premium Natural Fish Directly from Oita

Freshly delivered from Marusho Suisan in Saiki City, Oita Prefecture, our natural brand fish, "Aimi no Megumi," is a true delight. Raised in the rough waves of the Bungo Channel, these fish boast exceptional freshness and flavor. At our restaurant, you can see the fresh catch of the day and enjoy it prepared in your favorite style, whether it's Aqua Pazza or Carpaccio. Savor the authentic deliciousness that only top-quality ingredients can offer.

Food The Ultimate Dish Created by the First Champion of the Pasta World Championship

Our menu is crafted by Chef Tsuyoshi Yamada, the first champion of the Pasta World Championship held in Parma, Italy. His technique of transforming rich seafood broth into a sauce that perfectly clings to the pasta is truly world-class. With just one bite, you'll experience the flavors of the ocean and the aroma of wheat filling your mouth. Don't miss the chance to savor this meticulously calculated authentic dish.

DrinkCarefully Selected Wines and Fine Sake to Elevate Your Fish Dishes

Strong Southern Italian fish dishes deserve the perfect drink to complement them. Our chef has carefully selected a range of wines, starting with crisp, chilled Italian white wine, to enhance the potential of each dish. We also offer a variety of Japanese sake and shochu that pair wonderfully with seafood. Enjoy the blissful pairing of exquisite cuisine and fine drinks.

SpaceChoose from 3 floors for your scene! The first floor offers special seats with half-price glass wine.

The first floor features casual counter seating, perfect for enjoying a relaxed meal. During dinner time, glasses of wine are half price, making it ideal for solo diners or a quick drink. The second floor offers cozy table seating for a more laid-back experience. Plus, the third floor has a private space that accommodates up to 12 guests, perfect for exclusive parties. Whether it's a casual meal, a welcome or farewell party, or a special anniversary with someone important, enjoy exquisite seafood Italian cuisine in the perfect setting tailored to your needs.
