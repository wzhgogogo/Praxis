[Toraemon Ueno] A private room izakaya boasting robatayaki and freshly cooked kamameshi rice. (Smoking allowed)

~ A Relaxing Space for Fine Sake and Delicious Food ~ Just a short walk from Ueno Station! Whether it's for two people or a large banquet, leave it to us! All seats are private rooms with doors, all...owing you to enjoy a calm and relaxing time.
Our robatayaki, made with fresh fish delivered directly from Aomori and delicious ingredients from across the country, is exceptional!
Enjoy our carefully crafted oden available all year round.
We take pride in our wide variety of alcoholic and soft drinks that you won't find anywhere else!
Spend wonderful moments with your loved ones!

All you can drink

"Toraemon Trial Course" with Draft Beer, 7 Dishes, and 3-Hour All-You-Can-Drink JPY 4500 ⇒ JPY 35007 items

Enjoy our popular casual course featuring a total of seven dishes, including an assorted platter of appetizers, organic vegetable salad, marinated squid and tomatoes, crispy crab cream croquettes, and our signature vegetable rolls. The meal concludes with ume-jako rice and a sweet treat for a satisfying finish. This course includes a three-hour all-you-can-drink option, making it perfect for various banquets.

Toraemon Robatayaki Course with Draft Beer: 8 Dishes, 3-Hour All-You-Can-Drink JPY 4,0008 items

Enjoy our popular Manzoku course featuring delicious robatayaki. This satisfying menu includes appetizers, an organic vegetable salad, and fresh salmon yukke, along with our signature deep-fried Japanese radish oden, vegetable rolls, and grilled mackerel with spicy cod roe, totaling eight exquisite dishes. To finish, indulge in salmon and ikura kamameshi and a sweet treat for a complete experience. This course also includes a three-hour all-you-can-drink option, making it perfect for banquets!

Food We also offer Kamameshi made with carefully selected rice!

Directly delivered from our contracted fishing port! Enjoy a blissful moment savoring incredibly fresh seafood. Available in both course and à la carte options.

Food We carefully skewer every day to serve authentic vegetable rolls!

We have a wide selection of drinks, including both regular and premium brands, to ensure every guest is satisfied. Our tasty snacks pair perfectly with your drinks. Whether you're looking to enjoy a quiet drink with a small group or want to have a lively time at a banquet, we've got you covered!

Food From premium sake to unique local brews, we have it all!

We offer a wide selection of carefully chosen local sake from all over Japan. From crisp, dry varieties to rich, sweet options, you can enjoy them paired with your meal. We also regularly stock rare seasonal bottles. Be sure to savor the deep flavors of sake alongside our fresh seafood and charcoal-grilled chicken. We have tasting sets available, so why not find your favorite cup?
