Service"Smoking Allowed at Your Seat♪ Enjoy Food and Drinks in an Open Atmosphere on the Terrace!"

In our establishment, both table seating and private rooms are available for smoking. Smokers can relax without hesitation in a comfortable space, enjoying their meals and drinks at their leisure. For those who are sensitive to smoke, we offer a completely private room with a designated non-smoking area, ensuring a安心 (safe) environment.

Space【Modern Japanese Private Room Available】A Luxurious Space of Tranquility

Our store's top floor, which has three levels, is equipped with private rooms where you can enjoy your time! Due to its popularity, it fills up quickly, so please make your reservations early! One of the features of our store is that you can enjoy smoking in a relaxed atmosphere while chatting and dining. You can enjoy it as a private space without worrying about those around you! Why not enjoy a fun time with friends and companions along with delicious food and drinks?

Drink【Celebration OPEN Project◎】Free Coupons for Organizers Now Available♪

We are currently distributing coupons that offer great deals for the organizer! For more details, please check the coupon section! We also have a variety of course options available, ranging from an affordable plan at 2980 yen to luxurious courses that include seafood and horse sashimi. All courses come with an all-you-can-drink option, allowing you to relax and enjoy your time. Perfect for workplace parties or gatherings with friends, our course offerings are diverse! Spend a special moment with your loved ones at our establishment.

Food "【Prepared for Red Ink◎】All-You-Can-Eat and Drink for 3 Hours at an Unbelievable Price!"

You can enjoy a fantastic all-you-can-eat and drink course for three hours at an unbeatable price. This special plan allows you to freely enjoy delicious dishes and a wide variety of drinks. Additionally, you can indulge in our restaurant's pride, the juicy and aromatic charcoal-grilled chicken, as part of the all-you-can-eat offerings! With the three-hour all-you-can-eat and drink plan, please take your time to enjoy exquisite dishes and a fun time with drinks. We sincerely look forward to welcoming you all.
