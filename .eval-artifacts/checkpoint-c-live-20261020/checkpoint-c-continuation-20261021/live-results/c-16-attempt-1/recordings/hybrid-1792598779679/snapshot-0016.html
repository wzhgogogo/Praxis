Food "The Gentle Rice of Red Vinegar and the Sincere Craftsmanship of Edomae: Savoring the Supreme Sushi Piece"

The dish is finished with a mild red vinegar that has a rounded flavor, enhancing the individuality of the ingredients and harmonizing them in your mouth. Enjoy the exquisite balance of the gentle texture that melts in your mouth and the harmony of the ingredients.

Space"Enjoying Warm Hospitality in a Richly Expressive and Serene Space"

Passing through the noren curtain, I make my way down a corridor illuminated by soft lighting. The elegant white wooden counter stretches out gracefully, with only eight seats available. In the pleasant tension that makes one sit up straight, the warm hospitality of the owner gently loosens the strings of the guests' hearts.

Food "An Encounter with a Grip that Surpasses Imagination, Born from Elegant Movements"

Artistic creations of exquisite hand-formed sushi, born from techniques that leave no room for waste. The commitment to the ingredients is unwavering, and there is no compromise when it comes to the rice. The rice, shaped with a perfect balance of pressure, gently dissolves in the mouth, leaving behind a sensual sweetness. The temperature of the rice is adjusted daily to match the varying conditions of the ingredients.

Space"Private Rooms Available - Perfect for Face-to-Face Meetings and Entertaining Guests"

In addition to counter seats, we also have private rooms, making it ideal for face-to-face meetings and important business entertaining.
