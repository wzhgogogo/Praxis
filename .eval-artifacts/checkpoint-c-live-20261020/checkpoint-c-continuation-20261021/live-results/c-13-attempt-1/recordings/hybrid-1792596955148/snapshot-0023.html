Food "Enjoying the Allure of Wagyu: A New Japanese Cuisine Experience 'Meat Kaiseki'"

At Yukawa, we propose various ways to enjoy Japanese Wagyu, which Japan proudly presents to the world. While preserving the delicacy and tradition of Japanese cuisine, we deliver innovative and bold interpretations of seasonal Japanese dishes, known as "Niku Kaiseki."

Drink"Famous Japanese Sake Renowned Worldwide and Selected Quality Producer Wines"

The sake served with the meat kaiseki is carefully selected with a focus on "rich and delicious" flavors, featuring renowned Japanese sake recognized worldwide, as well as wines crafted by excellent producers from around the globe that offer a reliable taste. We will provide options tailored to your preferences and the dishes.

Space"Experience a Comfortable Flow of Time in a Unique Japanese Modern 'Private Room'"

The interior of our traditional-style house is composed entirely of private rooms, accommodating up to 12 guests. We cater to various needs, such as business meetings and anniversaries. Please enjoy a comfortable flow of time in our uniquely designed Japanese modern spaces.
