Food 【Live Experience】Craftsmen carefully prepare each dish right in front of you.

Experience the excitement of live performances right before your eyes at Shikai. Enjoy sushi crafted by skilled chefs, the art of Peking duck preparation, sizzling Teppan-yaki with flames dancing, and the impressive spectacle of whole tuna being expertly filleted. You can savor every moment until the dishes are ready. With over 200 varieties available for all-you-can-eat and all-you-can-drink options, indulge in a luxurious culinary entertainment that engages all your senses—sight, sound, and aroma.

Food [Ginza 1-Chome's Largest] All-You-Can-Drink Seafood Buffet | Yotsumi

A grand premium seafood buffet has opened in Ginza 1-chome. Indulge in fresh bluefin tuna, succulent king crab packed with meat, and melt-in-your-mouth A5 Wagyu, all featuring carefully selected high-quality ingredients. With over 150 diverse dishes blending Japanese, Western, and Chinese cuisines, enjoy all-you-can-eat delights to your heart's content. Of course, all-you-can-drink options are included. From seafood to meat dishes and exquisite specialties, we offer an overwhelming selection to treat you. Experience a luxurious buffet that’s perfect for special occasions in the refined atmosphere unique to Ginza.

Food [Luxury Ingredients All You Can Eat] A Special, Upscale Luxury Buffet

Indulge in all-you-can-eat A5 Wagyu beef, featuring a variety of cuts for you to savor and compare the unique flavors and textures. Enjoy the exquisite sweetness of high-quality fat and the melt-in-your-mouth experience that is truly exceptional. Our restaurant boasts a sophisticated atmosphere that exudes luxury and elegance, with meticulous attention to detail in materials, lighting, and colors. Experience a top-tier buffet in a refined setting that reflects the quality of Ginza, where you can fully enjoy A5 Wagyu and carefully selected premium seafood.

SpaceEnjoy the Night View of Ginza | Private Reservation Available Fully Equipped Private Rooms

Our sophisticated interior exudes luxury and elegance, featuring fully private rooms for uninterrupted enjoyment, spacious sofa seating, and comfortable table arrangements. Whether it's for business meetings, special occasions, or casual meals with family and friends, we cater to a wide range of settings. Experience a refined atmosphere with comfortable seating and soft lighting, where you can unwind and savor a luxurious buffet unique to Ginza, forgetting the passage of time.
