Enjoy udon for lunch and a Nagasaki izakaya experience with udon for dinner! Featuring Nagasaki Goto udon made with a unique double boiling method!

We offer a variety of dishes primarily from Nagasaki and Goto. The name "Baraka" means "lively and energetic" in the local dialect, reflecting the bright and robust spirit of the islanders and the war...m, rustic atmosphere of the island. Goto Udon is a high-quality noodle that is thin and chewy, perfect for health-conscious diners.
You can also enjoy Nagasaki's famous gourmet dishes and Goto wine! It's ideal for business lunches on weekdays and perfect for family outings or girls' nights out on weekends. We welcome you with delicious meals and heartfelt service!

Food Goto Udon, boiled twice for that perfect chewy texture.

We proudly serve Goto Udon, known for its strong chewiness thanks to the hand-stretched method. Sourced from Goto, one of Japan's top production areas, our udon is made with camellia oil and seawater, making it the thinnest among udon varieties. By boiling these carefully crafted noodles twice, we achieve an even more delightful chewy texture. This light bowl is popular among women and the elderly, making it a great choice for health-conscious diners.

Food Be sure to enjoy the local gourmet food from Nagasaki!

Hatoshi, or shrimp toast, is a dish made by sandwiching shrimp paste between slices of bread and frying it until golden brown and crispy. The contrast of the crunchy exterior and the juicy interior is truly delightful. Originating from Nagasaki's Chinatown, it pairs perfectly with udon thanks to the use of Nagasaki's famous fish paste. Our bite-sized gyoza is perfect for everyone, including women, children, and seniors. These little dumplings are easy to eat in one bite, making them a fun and tasty treat!

DrinkWe offer Nagasaki's sake, including our own Goto Wine.

At our restaurant, you can enjoy sake from Nagasaki. We have a selection that pairs perfectly with our dishes. Our own wines include two types of sparkling wine from our winery in Nagasaki. These wines have won gold at the Sakura Awards, a wine competition judged entirely by women, making them very popular among female customers. We also offer shochu and Japanese sake, so you can enjoy your meal with your favorite drink!

SpaceFrom lunch breaks to private banquets, we've got you covered!

Our restaurant is just a 3-minute walk from Kichijoji Station, making it a convenient stop for lunch or after work. Enjoy your meal in a lively atmosphere filled with the sounds and aromas of freshly fried dishes. We offer a vibrant counter seating perfect for solo diners, as well as spacious table seating for a more relaxed experience. Private reservations are available for girls' parties, business meetings, and welcome or farewell parties, so feel free to reach out to us!
