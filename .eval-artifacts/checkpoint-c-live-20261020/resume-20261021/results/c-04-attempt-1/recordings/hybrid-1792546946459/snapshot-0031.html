Earn TC Points

Earn TC Points by completing bookings on TableCheck Market place at all venues participating in the program. You can use these points for prepaying courses and use for your bill at participating venues and save money.

Points expire at the end of the 6th month from the month they were earned (For example, points earned in January will expire on June 30th.)
