[2-Hour Premium Course | All-You-Can-Eat & Drink] Ultimate Seafood Buffet Shikai <*Children's Pricing Available> <Year-End Party & New Year Party>

Experience one of the largest seafood buffets in Ginza, featuring over 200 exquisite dishes available for all-you-can-eat enjoyment.
Indulge in a selection of premium ingredients such as tuna, king crab, A5 Wagyu beef, and the ultra-rare Japanese pufferfish.
The restaurant boasts a luxurious and sophisticated interior, perfect for a special occasion.
Located in the heart of Ginza 1-chome, this spacious venue occupies the entire 9th floor for private reservations.
