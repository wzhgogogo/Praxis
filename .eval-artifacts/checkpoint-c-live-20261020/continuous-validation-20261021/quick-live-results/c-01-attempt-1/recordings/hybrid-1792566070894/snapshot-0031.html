Directly connected to Shibuya Station, enjoy the craftsmanship of our "Omakase" with grilled, marinated, and aged dishes. Relax and unwind with local sake.

"A little bit of seasonal ingredients, a lot of variety"—this concept is embodied in a refined, luxurious sushi restaurant. In the "Omakase" course, skilled chefs showcase their techniques through gri...lled, marinated, and aged sushi, alternating with appetizers to create a delightful rhythm for your taste buds. Enjoy carefully selected tuna from a specialty shop in Toyosu, paired with local sake from across the country, for a quality experience that sparks great conversation. Indulge in a memorable sushi experience in the heart of Shibuya.

Food A Masterful "Omakase" Style Where Snacks and Sushi Intersect

We offer a seasonal omakase course featuring the freshest ingredients of the day, beautifully arranged with a rhythm of appetizers and sushi. Our skilled chefs use various techniques like searing, aging, and marinating to bring out the best flavors of each ingredient, delivering a unique experience and surprise with every piece.

Food Fresh Bluefin Tuna from Oma and Iki. Savor the melt-in-your-mouth fat.

We source our premium tuna from a specialized shop at Toyosu Market, bringing in the finest quality from top regions like Oma in Aomori, Toi in Hokkaido, and Iki in Nagasaki, depending on the season. We focus on serving rich and flavorful pieces, especially the rare cut known as "Belly No. 1," which beautifully combines taste and fat.

Space3rd Floor, Shibuya Stream at Shibuya Station - A Luxurious and Quality Space

Located directly connected to Shibuya Station on the 3rd floor of Shibuya Stream, our restaurant offers a calm and luxurious atmosphere. With both counter and table seating, you can enjoy the live craftsmanship from any spot. It's perfect for business dinners, dates, or meals with guests from abroad. We invite you to make use of our space for a variety of occasions.

DrinkA carefully selected variety of sake from across Japan, harmonizing with seasonal sushi.

To complement our seasonal sushi, we offer a carefully selected range of sake and local brews from all over Japan. Each sip enhances the delicate flavors of Japanese cuisine, making your dining experience even richer. Enjoy your favorite drink to your heart's content.
