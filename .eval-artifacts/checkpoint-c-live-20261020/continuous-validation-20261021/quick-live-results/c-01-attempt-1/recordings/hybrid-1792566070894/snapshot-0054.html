Indulge in the creative sushi of a stylish reverse-import brand. Experience new sensations with Aburi Sushi and Mosaic Sushi!

Introducing a creative sushi izakaya that brings a taste of Canada back to Japan. Experience a new style of sushi that blends traditional Japanese flavors with North American culinary culture. Enjoy o...ur signature Aburi Sushi "Aburishasu," which tantalizes your senses with flames, aromas, and a melt-in-your-mouth texture. Don't miss our beautifully crafted "Mosaic Sushi," a true work of art.
Pair your meal with original Japanese cocktails and seasonal limited cocktails crafted by our skilled bartenders.
Relax in a modern space designed by a professional, and savor the essence of "evolved sushi."

Food 16 Varieties of Toppings on One Plate. Edible Art "Mosaic Sushi"

Our unique "Mosaic Sushi" is not just about flavor, but also about presentation. Using 16 different toppings, we create a colorful and luxurious dish that embodies the meaning of "stacking good fortune." This photogenic plate is perfect for special occasions like anniversaries or girls' nights out. Don't miss our grilled sushi, available in four options: shrimp, salmon, mackerel, and eel. You can choose from nigiri, pressed sushi, or rolls, all grilled to perfection right in front of you.

Space"KINKA" is Full of Casual Chic Vibes☆

Kinka is a place where you can casually enjoy delicious food and drinks even if you're alone as a woman. It's perfect for dates and business meetings, but you can also drop by the counter for some tapas and a glass of wine in a relaxed manner. 【Ultimate Nigiri Sushi】 We use plenty of seasonal and fresh ingredients for our nigiri. Lobster, fatty tuna, A5 Wagyu beef, snow crab, sea urchin, salmon roe, fresh scallops... We sincerely look forward to your visit!

Food A Rich Variety of Course Menus Available♪

Our stylish space is perfect for important girls' nights out, dates, farewell and welcome parties, and company gatherings. We offer a fantastic course starting from JPY 4,000, along with a private rental plan that accommodates up to 66 guests. Our signature dish, "Aburi Shas", prepared right in front of you, and our modern Japanese anniversary plates are ideal for surprising the guest of honor or impressing your important guests.

DrinkOriginal Japanese Cocktails to Complement Sushi. Seasonal Specials Available!

At KINKA, enjoy a variety of sushi paired with unique and exquisite drinks. Our bartenders craft innovative original cocktails, including a sake sangria that combines figs and citrus. We also offer seasonal cocktails inspired by ingredients like cherry blossoms and strawberries. Experience our exclusive drinks that you won't find anywhere else, and feel free to explore pairing options. The more you choose, the more your taste experience transforms—so dive into this creative pairing adventure!
