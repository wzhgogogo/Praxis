Food "Expertise Backed by Experience - Direct from the Market - Selection of Seasonal Ingredients"

The owner, who has dedicated himself to Edo-style sushi for many years, takes pride in his exceptional ability to select the finest ingredients. He sources carefully chosen ingredients through unique channels that he has maintained over the years. Please enjoy the distinctive flavors of our Edo-style sushi, featuring ingredients that can only be experienced at our restaurant.

Food "Hand-formed Sushi Rice - Using Niigata Prefecture Uonuma Koshihikari with a Focus on Temperature"

The important thing about sushi rice is that it should not fall apart before it reaches your mouth, and once it enters your mouth, the sushi rice should spread gently. (It is shaped so that there is a hollow in the center of the rice.) This creates a good balance with the topping, allowing both to bring out the best flavors in each other. We are particular about "the temperature of human skin" and provide freshly made sushi.

Space"Bringing Customers Closer - High-Quality Counter Seats -"

We will carefully explain everything, from sushi to selected local sake and ingredients. Our restaurant values conversation with our customers and aims to convey the charm of Edomae sushi to first-time visitors. We invite you to enjoy authentic Edomae sushi at our calm and high-quality counter seats.

Drink"Carefully Selected Local Sake Sourced from All Over the Country"

Our shop is well-stocked with a wide variety of recommended local sake that even sake lovers will appreciate, including seasonal Japanese sake. We have carefully selected premium sake and shochu such as "Dassai" and "Kokuryu." For beer, we offer Asahi Super Dry Jukusen. If you have any questions about Japanese sake, please feel free to contact us.
