Food Nigiri

In our spacious and calming restaurant, both at the counter and in private rooms, our friendly chef showcases seasonal fresh fish in delightful sushi. We purchase our ingredients daily, ensuring that only carefully selected items make it to your plate. We tailor our preparation methods, choose the right condiments, and vary the types of sushi rice vinegar to serve you the best possible experience. If you're new to counter sushi, don't worry! We focus on providing a relaxed atmosphere and attentive service, so feel free to reach out to us anytime.

Food Dishes

Our course includes a variety of dishes that are nearly equal in quantity to the sushi. While the dishes listed here are just a small selection, each one is carefully prepared with attention to detail. We use seasonal fish to bring out the best flavors of the ingredients. The warm dishes are served between the sushi, enhancing your dining experience. They pair wonderfully with our proud selection of sake and shochu, making for a satisfying course with plenty of options.

DrinkAll-you-can-drink

Our restaurant is unique for a counter sushi establishment as we offer a delightful all-you-can-drink system. We have a wide selection of beverages, including the sake shown in the photo, as well as Japanese whiskies like Hakushu and Chita, shochu, and wine. Of course, we also offer premium wines, champagne, and premium sake at an additional charge. For details on the all-you-can-drink options, you can check the menu on Tabelog. We encourage you to take a look!

SpaceA Relaxing Space

Located just a 2-minute walk from Shibuya Mark City, Sushi Ki offers a serene escape from the hustle and bustle of the city as soon as you step inside. The warm wooden decor, complemented by carefully selected floral arrangements, creates a calming atmosphere. We maintain a clean environment and implement thorough infection control measures to ensure a safe dining experience. Not only is the interior inviting, but our chef and staff are dedicated to providing a soothing space through exceptional service.
