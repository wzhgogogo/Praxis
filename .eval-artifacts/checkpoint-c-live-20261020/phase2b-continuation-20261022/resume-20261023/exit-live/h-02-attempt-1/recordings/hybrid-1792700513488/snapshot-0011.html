"Creative Monjayaki with Japanese Ingredients" Private Room Overlooking the Charm of Downtown

Slowly simmered pork bone broth made from Kagoshima Kurobuta and Kyoto Fukushima katsuobushi, concentrating the umami flavors. Combined with natural water from Mount Fuji and Biei wheat from Hokkaido,... this creative monjayaki features a special tempura batter from Michelin-listed "Tempura Abe" that enhances the taste. Enjoy a unique experience in a semi-private space overlooking the charm of Tsukishima, where you can savor the rich flavors of fresh seafood delivered directly from Toyosu and the essence of quality ingredients.

Food "Tsukishima Classic Monjayaki and Creative Monjayaki"

We offer a wide range of confident creations, from classic Tsukishima monjayaki to innovative monjayaki inspired by authentic Western cuisine. True to our name, our monjayaki is topped with “ingredients overflowing,” making it visually striking. We use a generous amount of fresh, high-quality ingredients that we have sourced directly from local producers across the country through exclusive partnerships.

Food Ultimate Broth (Pork Bone Base Broth) - A Broth Simmered for 5 Hours with 10 Different Ingredients

We use dried ingredients specially blended for our store from a long-established dried goods shop in Kyoto. We source ingredients such as mackerel flakes, urume flakes, sardine flakes, boiled dried anchovies, and kelp, all made using traditional methods that bring out their umami flavors. Unusually for monjayaki, we base our dish on pork bones and combine it with chicken bones and vegetables, simmering a total of 10 different ingredients. We also scientifically validate the umami flavors. To maximize the quality of our carefully selected ingredients, we thoroughly research their components and provide the optimal levels of amino acids that contribute to umami.

Space"Seating Design Focused on Private Rooms: Enjoying Meals While Taking in the Scenery of Tsukishima"

Located on the 6th floor, which is rare in the downtown area, our restaurant allows you to enjoy your meal while taking in the charming atmosphere of the neighborhood through the windows. The interior, inspired by "Japanese modern" aesthetics, exudes a sense of luxury, with most seats being semi-private. In line with the new normal, we ensure social distancing and have designed spacious seating arrangements. Additionally, the air in the restaurant is refreshed every 3.3 minutes, so you can enjoy your meal with peace of mind.
