Restaurant highlights

Right near Asakusa Kaminarimon, enjoy exquisite A5 grade Japanese wagyu sukiyaki in the heart of Asakusa for a luxurious experience.

Located near Asakusa's Kaminarimon, our restaurant offers a luxurious experience of traditional Japanese "Sukiyaki" made with carefully selected domestic A5 grade Wagyu beef. The tender and flavorful ...Wagyu melts in your mouth, releasing a rich aroma and deep taste with every bite, providing an exquisite experience for all your senses. Enjoy the charm of Asakusa while savoring this high-quality Wagyu, perfect for sightseeing, anniversaries, or special gatherings.
With our signature Sukiyaki and warm hospitality, we promise to deliver unforgettable memories of Asakusa.
