Just 5 minutes from Azabu-Juban Station, enjoy uncompromising handmade 100% buckwheat soba that reflects the changing seasons. Experience a special dining moment in an adult atmosphere.

Soba Kappo Tatsu, located in Azabu-Juban and Motoazabu, is dedicated to preserving traditional Japanese cuisine while pursuing unique dishes you can only enjoy here. We focus on seasonal ingredients, ...presenting plates that may look unfamiliar, but each bite is undeniably Japanese. Enjoy the flavors of the seasons alongside our homemade 100% hand-made soba, milled in-house.
Chef Sano Tatsuhiro crafts each dish with care in a calm atmosphere, perfect for business meetings, special dinners with loved ones, or anniversaries. This is a reservation-only, tranquil soba kappo.

Food A seasonal tasting menu that changes monthly. Special plans available on Sundays and holidays.

We offer a chef's omakase course featuring 9 delightful dishes. Starting with an appetizer, you'll enjoy a variety of flavors including Karasumi Soba, a soup, sashimi, assorted seasonal delicacies, meat, a hearty main dish, and a choice of soba, finishing off with a sweet treat. Our menu changes with the seasons and regions, allowing you to experience different tastes each month. We also have special courses available on Sundays and holidays, so feel free to join us for any occasion.

SpaceA refined space with a counter and fully private rooms, perfect for business meetings and special occasions.

The dimly lit interior offers a calm and sophisticated atmosphere. We have 9 wooden counter seats and one fully private room for 4 guests. The counter seats provide a front-row view of the cooking process, making it an exciting spot where you can see everything except the dishwashing area. It's perfect for business meetings, entertaining guests, or celebrating special occasions with loved ones. We can also accommodate birthday plates, so feel free to reach out if you're interested!

Food Carefully Selected Buckwheat Flour and Diligently Brewed Dashi Create Our Special Soba

At our restaurant, we use carefully selected seasonal buckwheat flour from all over Japan. We source fresh ingredients from Hokkaido to Kyushu, ensuring the best flavors of the season. Our dashi is made without any chemical seasonings, using only natural honkarebushi and kombu kelp for a refined taste. We prioritize bringing out the natural flavors of the ingredients, which is the foundation of all our dishes. Enjoy our freshly ground and handmade ju-wari soba, made daily!

DrinkA Variety of Drinks Selected by Our Sommelier, Including Wine and Japanese Sake

We take great care in pairing our dishes with the perfect drinks. Our knowledgeable female owner, who is a certified sommelier, has carefully selected a wide variety of wines, champagnes, and Japanese sake. We offer around 100 types of wine, about 30 types of Japanese sake, and around 7 types of Japanese whiskey, along with 6 selections from Suntory and Asahi. Our lineup changes with the seasons, and we also have wines tailored to the preferences of our regular customers. Enjoy your favorite drink served in beautiful Kyo-Yaki pottery from Kyoto.
