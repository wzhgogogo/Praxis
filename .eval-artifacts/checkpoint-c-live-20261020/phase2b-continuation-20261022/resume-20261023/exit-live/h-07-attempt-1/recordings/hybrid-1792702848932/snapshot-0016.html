Food Indulge in Rich and Delicious Flavors! All-You-Can-Eat King Crab and Snow Crab

We carefully select fresh snow crabs and king crabs, raised in the bountiful sea. Snow crabs are known for their delicate sweetness and melt-in-your-mouth texture, while king crabs boast a robust firmness and rich flavor. We boil them gently to bring out their unique qualities, allowing you to enjoy their juicy meat and savory aroma. Indulge in a luxurious all-you-can-eat crab experience!

Food Enjoy a course featuring a delightful comparison of A5 Rank Satsuma Nishiki Beef and Nankoku Barley Pork.

At our restaurant, you can fully enjoy a variety of carefully selected domestic beef and premium pork cuts in sukiyaki style. The A5-ranked Satsuma Nishiki beef is known for its fine marbling and melt-in-your-mouth texture. Additionally, the Nankoku Barley Pork offers a delightful sweetness from its rich fat and juicy flavor. Both pair perfectly with our special dipping sauce, delivering a deep taste with every bite. Come experience the luxurious "all-you-can-eat meat" in Kyoto!

Food All-You-Can-Eat Luxury Hand-Rolled Sushi with Sea Urchin, Salmon Roe, Crab, and Wagyu

Enjoy unlimited hand-rolled sushi made with luxurious ingredients like sea urchin, salmon roe, crab, and Wagyu beef. Depending on the price, the variety of toppings changes, allowing you to savor even higher quality ingredients. Indulge in the melt-in-your-mouth Wagyu, flavorful crab, rich sea urchin, and bursting salmon roe, all combined to create your perfect sushi experience. With a quality that exceeds typical all-you-can-eat offerings, this is a perfect choice for special occasions.

SpaceA chic, modern, and calming space. Perfect for various occasions such as banquets and dining.

The chic interior, featuring dark brown tables and decor, creates a calm atmosphere. Enjoy the luxurious flavors of Satsuma Nishiki beef and high-quality crab while relaxing in comfort. Feel free to use our space for various occasions, including banquets, welcome and farewell parties, and celebrations.
