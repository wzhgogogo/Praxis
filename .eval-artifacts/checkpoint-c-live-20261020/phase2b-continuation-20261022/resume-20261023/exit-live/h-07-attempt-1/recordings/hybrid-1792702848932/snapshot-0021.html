Founded in 1880, the oldest Sukiyaki restaurant in Asakusa.

Founded in 1880 as a restaurant in Asakusa, Tokyo, we became a specialty store for Sukiyaki in 1903. Over the generations, we have been cherished by our customers, but due to the aging of our building... and global circumstances, we closed in August 2021. On March 18, 2022 (the birthday of Asakusa), we reopened in a new location, determined to preserve our traditions and commitment to quality. Since our reopening, we have focused on delivering deliciousness. Our courses feature seasonal appetizers that allow you to savor the flavors of each season.

Others Chinya's Perfectly Marbled Meat

"Suitable marbled meat" refers to "moderately marbled meat," indicating that the marbling is not excessive. When heated, the boundary between the marbling and the lean meat releases a delightful "Wagyu aroma," making "Ko-zashi" a fragrant choice. The balance of the rich flavor of the lean meat and the sweetness of the fat, combined with aging, allows for a sukiyaki that is not heavy on the stomach and has a wonderful aroma.

SpaceGuide to Private Rooms

We have prepared a relaxing space reminiscent of a Meiji-era Western-style mansion and a serene pure Japanese room for entertaining important guests or for gatherings related to celebrations and condolences. We offer a Western private room "Matsunoyama" with 7 seats, a Western private room "Saruwaka" with 6 seats, and a pure Japanese room "Koshigata" with 8 seats. *A room fee of 4,000 yen will be charged.

Others "Guide to the Butcher Shop"

Please use the domestically sourced black-haired Wagyu beef, which is purchased whole, for items such as sukiyaki bento and hamburgers, whether for personal use or as gifts.
