Just 2 minutes from Ikebukuro Station! Enjoy an extraordinary atmosphere with authentic Thai vibes and Isaan cuisine! Perfect for lunch, dinner, or private parties!

The interior is adorned with decorations unique to Thailand, creating an exotic atmosphere. We offer authentic Isaan cuisine, characterized by its spiciness and tanginess. Enjoy a lunch set for JPY 1,...100, which includes four dishes, a daily dessert, and Thai tea. In the evening, we offer flexible banquet courses and can accommodate private reservations for 20 to 30 guests, making it perfect for dates or group gatherings. Experience the essence of Thailand, from casual lunches to evening meals, at our restaurant!

Food Get hooked on the spiciness and tanginess! Experience the charm of Thailand with authentic Isaan cuisine.

We've got a selection of Isaan dishes, known for their spiciness and tanginess, right here! A must-try is our unique "Naem Kook," featuring a fresh combination of plenty of vegetables and sausage. For spice lovers, our countryside Thai curry "Gaeng On" and the chewy Isaan-style fried noodles "Pad Mee Korat" are also popular choices. Plus, we offer a "Happy Set" for solo diners, which includes 4 dishes for just JPY 1,990, making it easy to enjoy a variety of flavors!

DrinkFrom local Thai spirits to Japanese sake! Enjoy aromatic cocktails too!

We offer a wide selection of drinks that capture the essence of Thailand, including Mekong whiskey, mango beer, and SPY wine. Our popular cocktails like cilantro highball and mint highball burst with the refreshing flavors of fresh herbs. We also have Japanese sake and shochu, ensuring there's something for everyone to enjoy. Don't miss our happy hour every Tuesday from 5 PM to 8 PM, where you can sip on draft beer for just JPY 390. If you're looking to toast without breaking the bank, this is the perfect time to join us!

Food Our recommended plan includes a comprehensive selection of Isaan cuisine for just JPY 3,500!

In our JPY 3,500 course, you can enjoy classic Thai dishes like Tom Yum Goong, Gai Yang, and Larb, which are hard to find in Japan. You’ll also love that you can choose between authentic papaya and the more familiar variety for Som Tum! Perfect for group gatherings or a fun night out!

SpaceEnjoy the night view and exotic atmosphere of Thailand. A perfect space for dates and group blind dates.

As soon as you step inside, the decor captures the essence of Thai culture and ambiance. The large windows offer a romantic view of the night skyline, making it perfect for dates or group blind dates! You’ll feel like you’re abroad while still in Japan, which is a big plus. We also offer private reservations for groups of 20 to 30, making it ideal for gatherings with friends or company events.
