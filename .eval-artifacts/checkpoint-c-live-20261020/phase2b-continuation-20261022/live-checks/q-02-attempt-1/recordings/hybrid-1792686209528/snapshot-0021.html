SpaceA warm space designed to provide the ultimate in comfort.

Once you pass through the entrance, you will see the counter overflowing with the energy of the grilling craftsmen. The dignified interior of the restaurant is equipped with private rooms and table seats, allowing you to enjoy your meal surrounded by a warm and friendly atmosphere. Watching the craftsmen at work is also part of the enjoyment. Attention to detail is also evident in the tableware and sake cups.

Food Enjoy Nagoya Cochin, which boasts freshness, in its most delicious form.

We use "Nagoya Cochin" and "locally farmed chicken" delivered directly from the production area. The meat is deep and flavorful, but chewy and somewhat firm. The appeal is being able to taste the fat and the delicious skin. We carefully adjust the butchering, heating and cooking methods to suit each individual animal. We bring out the maximum flavor of the ingredients and serve them in a course that allows you to enjoy them with seasonal ingredients. We hope you enjoy our carefully crafted yakitori.

Food We offer special courses for dates, business meetings, and group gatherings.

We offer four courses featuring plenty of chicken. Whether it's a special date, a business gathering, or a lively party with friends, our courses are perfect for any occasion. We're here to support you wholeheartedly, tailored to your needs.

Service"Helping You Create Memorable Celebrations. Wonderful Memories at Our Store."

Indulge in our luxurious rose chocolate melt cake, crafted by our talented patissier, as a delightful surprise for your celebrations. Whether it's a birthday, anniversary, or any special occasion, we’re here to make it memorable. Enjoy a special time with your loved ones in our cozy atmosphere. Feel free to let us know how we can help!
