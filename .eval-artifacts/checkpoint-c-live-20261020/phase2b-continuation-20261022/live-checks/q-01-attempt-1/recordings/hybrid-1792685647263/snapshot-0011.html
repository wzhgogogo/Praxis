Food Delicious! A Dashi Shabu That Will Leave You Amazed!

At our restaurant, our shabu-shabu is uniquely enjoyed with a special broth crafted from traditional Japanese culinary techniques, rather than ponzu sauce. We carefully extract the umami and flavor from domestic natural kombu kelp, bonito flakes, and niboshi, creating a fragrant broth enhanced with our special "kaeshi". We serve it with plenty of green onions, which pair perfectly with the pork. Feel free to ask for as many green onions as you like. Once you try our signature "Dashi Shabu," you'll find yourself craving it again and again. Enjoy it to your heart's content!

Service"Storage for Souvenirs and Gifts Available. Celebration Courses Also Offered."

If you have a gift for your guests during ceremonies such as weddings, funerals, or receptions, we can hold it for you at our store until the time of presentation, so it doesn't become a burden. Additionally, we offer courses with individual hot pots, such as the "Celebration Kaiseki," perfect for occasions like face-to-face meetings, and the "Seasonal Kaiseki," which is also convenient for receptions. If you plan to use our services for an important event, please feel free to consult us as we also allow for site visits.

Food "Ingredients can be changed to suit the customer. Vegan courses are also available."

If you have any food ingredients that you cannot eat due to allergies, religious reasons, or personal preferences, we can accommodate changes to the course menu. We also offer a special course for vegan customers that does not use any animal products. Additionally, we have foreign language menus available, and our staff can assist you in simple English, so international customers can feel comfortable using our services.

DrinkUnlimited drinks with no time limit. Relax without worrying about the clock.

All-you-can-drink options are available for groups of four or more and can be added to your course as desired (excluding some courses). Our staff will assist with pouring drinks. There is no time limit, so you can relax and enjoy your time without worrying about the clock. We offer a carefully curated selection of alcoholic beverages, including rare whiskies and sake. Enjoy a variety of fine drinks along with your course.
