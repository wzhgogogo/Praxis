Space"Celebrating 50 Years in Akasaka: Enjoy Your Time in Complete Privacy"

■The first floor features tables for four, primarily used by small groups of two to three close customers in a non-smoking area (there is a smoking room on the first floor). ■The second floor offers private rooms for four to eighteen people, as well as a large hall with a traditional Tosa plaster relief that can accommodate up to forty people. ■The third floor has traditional Japanese sunken kotatsu-style tatami seating for four to thirty people. ■The fourth floor has a garden-view Western-style private room, limited to one room, accommodating six to twelve people. *Time restrictions apply only to banquet and all-you-can-drink courses; otherwise, there are no time limits.

Food "The Best Bonito in Japan, Premium Hand-Caught Bonito."

Founded 100 years ago, "Nehoki" offers a carefully crafted tataki of bonito. The selected bonito is freshly caught using the traditional single-line fishing method. This method allows for the selection of truly delicious bonito from the school, while also being environmentally considerate. We only use bonito that is not only fresh but also of the perfect size for tataki. The unique charcoal grilling method enhances the exquisite flavor of the carefully selected top-quality bonito. The skin is crispy and fragrant, while the inside is perfectly rare. Enjoy the delightful aroma of the grill and the rich, overflowing umami.

Food Commitment to Fresh Natural Fish...

At Niboshi, we source "wild-caught" fresh fish for our sashimi. We are particular about freshness, and every morning we prepare live fish, which is delivered to our restaurant by that evening. We invite you to experience our commitment to natural fresh fish for yourself!

SpaceFully Equipped Private Rooms for 4 to 40 People

■Two private rooms with gardens located on the 4th floor. A space to enjoy special food and drinks with special people. Located on a barrier-free restroom floor. ■The 3rd floor has a sunken kotatsu private room that can accommodate up to 30 people. ■The 2nd floor has a Western-style private room that can accommodate up to 40 people. We offer fully private rooms that can cater to various scenes.
