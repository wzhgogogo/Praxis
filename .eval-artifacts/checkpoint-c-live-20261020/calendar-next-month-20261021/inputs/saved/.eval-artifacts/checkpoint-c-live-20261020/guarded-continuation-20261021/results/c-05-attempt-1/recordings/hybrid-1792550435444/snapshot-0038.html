Experience seasonal flavors through the artistry of a master chef. Enjoy carefully selected sake and kaiseki cuisine in a refined setting, perfect for gatherings and special occasions.

Chef Mitsuhiro Komuro, a master of the Enshu style, presents kaiseki cuisine that highlights the natural flavors of the ingredients. The rice is sourced from Hakushu in Yamanashi, and seasonal seafood... and mountain delicacies are carefully selected by the chef. The restaurant features exquisite works by renowned artisans like Seika Suda and antique Baccarat glassware that reflect the changing seasons. A selection of wines from Katsunuma and sake from Sakanaga are also available to complement the dishes. The serene atmosphere of the restaurant includes counter seating and private rooms on the second floor. Perfect for important dining occasions such as business meetings or anniversaries with loved ones.

SpaceA Sophisticated Adult Hideaway ◆ A Quality Time Adorned with Artisan Skills and Conversations

Our restaurant moved to a quiet residential area in Kagurazaka in 2018. Inside, you'll find a serene and calming atmosphere. The counter seats offer a special spot where you can enjoy watching the chef's skills up close while chatting with the owner. On the second floor, we have three private rooms with doors, perfect for business meetings or family gatherings. Take your time and enjoy a special moment in this relaxed space.

Food Enjoy Seasonal Delights ◆ A Selection of Dishes Showcasing Artisan Skills Paired with Fine Sake and Wine

Our chef carefully selects the finest ingredients to create dishes that truly highlight their natural flavors. The rice comes directly from the Ono Farm in Hakushu, Yamanashi Prefecture, nurtured by the pristine waters of the Southern Alps. We skillfully bring out the authentic tastes of seasonal seafood and mountain delicacies. Additionally, we offer a curated selection of wines from Katsunuma and sake from Sakanouen, perfectly complementing our dishes. Enjoy the deep, lingering flavors of our seasonal creations paired with exquisite beverages.

Others Craftsmanship of Japanese Aesthetics ◆ Enjoyed with Antique Glasses

The flavors of our dishes are enhanced by a carefully selected array of tableware, handpicked by the owner from various regions in Japan. You'll find exquisite pieces like ceramics from Suda Seika and Sawamura Tōya, pots from Unui Kiln's Nakagawa Ippen, and lacquerware ordered from Wajima. Our glassware features antique Baccarat as the centerpiece. We change the tableware with the seasons, creating moments that are memorable for both the eyes and the heart.

ChefTraditional and Innovative Japanese Cuisine by Mitsuhiro Komuro, Chef of the Enshu School

Born in 1966 in Akishima City, Tokyo, I was inspired to become a chef by my older brother. After graduating from Futaba Nutrition College in 1985, I joined a kaiseki restaurant called Wako in Kudanshita. After seven years of honing my skills, I began working as a private chef around 1993 and opened Kaiseki Komuro in Kagurazaka in 2000. As a chef affiliated with the Enshu school, I also participate in tea ceremonies, offering elegant hospitality and authentic Japanese cuisine. Come and enjoy the special flavors crafted from deep knowledge and rich experience.
