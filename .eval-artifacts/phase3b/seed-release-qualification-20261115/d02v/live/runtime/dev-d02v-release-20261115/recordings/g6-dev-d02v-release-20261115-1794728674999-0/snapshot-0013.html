【5-Minute Walk from Nakano Station】Motsunabe, Mentaiko, and Grilled Chicken Skin♪ Enjoy Authentic Hakata Cuisine at this Hidden Gem Tavern

Enjoy authentic Hakata flavors at the izakaya "Hakata Standard" in Koenji! Savor our soy sauce-based "Motsunabe" and "Mentaiko Motsunabe" topped with spicy cod roe, along with the delicious "Hakata Go...ma Buri" and yakitori made with Hakata jidori chicken.

We offer a wide selection of shochu and sake from Kyushu.

For a night of drinking, take advantage of our "Don Yasu" deal, where the more you drink, the cheaper it gets! Plus, until 5 PM, all drinks are included in the Don Yasu promotion, ensuring a truly satisfying experience.

Food "Delicious Motsunabe with Light Soy Sauce Soup! Topped with Mentaiko is Also Yummy♪"

Our restaurant in Koenji offers authentic Hakata gourmet cuisine. Our top recommendation is the proud motsunabe made with dashi from Hokkaido kelp! The light soy sauce-based broth has a deep flavor that makes you want to drink it all the way to the last drop. We also have a "Mentaiko Motsunabe" topped with spicy cod roe, so feel free to choose according to your preference! Orders can be made from just one serving, making it perfect for solo dining or when you want to try a variety of dishes! For the finale, you can choose between zosui (rice porridge) or ramen!

Food "Grilled Chicken from Hakata: Perfect Pairing with Sake♪"

The grilled chicken skewers, featuring the classic selections from Fukuoka, are also highly recommended. You can enjoy local specialty chicken "Hakata jidori" in the "Yotsumi" style, as well as "Gurukawa," which has been carefully prepared to reduce excess fat, and pork belly, among other unique Fukuoka menu items. You can easily order them by the skewer!

Drink"Kagoshima's Sweet Potato Shochu Abounds! The More You Drink, the More You Save with the 'Don-Yasu' System!"

The shochu that pairs well with our dishes features a rich selection centered around sweet potatoes from Kagoshima. We offer a variety of flavors, including the popular "Daiyame," which has a fruity aroma that appeals to women. Our sake, sourced from various regions, changes with the seasons, allowing you to enjoy something new each time without getting bored. For nights when you want to drink a lot, we recommend the "Don An" system, where the more you drink, the more you save! Target drinks like highballs and sours start at 550 yen for the first glass, and from the fourth glass onward, you can order them for just 110 yen each!

Space"Impressive Open Counter"

The impressive open counter with a view of the kitchen creates a welcoming atmosphere that's easy to enter even for one person. We look forward to your visit!
