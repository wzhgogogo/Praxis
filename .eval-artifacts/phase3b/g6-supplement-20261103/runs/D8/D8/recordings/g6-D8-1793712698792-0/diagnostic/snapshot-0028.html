Food Nodoguro Clay Pot Rice (3 go)

Experience the finest Nodoguro cooked in a clay pot. We prepare a whole fish for a luxurious meal! This is the perfect way to savor every bit of the deliciousness of Nodoguro rice. Please note that this dish is available by reservation only. If you make a reservation on the same day, availability may depend on the market supply. Feel free to reach out when you book!

Food "Charcoal-Grilled: The Thickest and Finest Nodoguro Prepared in the Most Delicious Way in Japan"

Enjoy the finest nodoguro grilled in the traditional way! Nodoguro, known for its black mouth, is a delicious fish rich in fat, often referred to as the "toro of white fish." The traditional cooking method called "genshiyaki" involves slowly grilling fresh fish and vegetables over an irori (a traditional Japanese hearth). The outside becomes crispy and fragrant, while the inside remains fluffy. We invite you to savor the traditional genshiyaki experience. Additionally, you can enjoy fresh fish delivered directly from carefully selected fishing ports and markets across the country.

SpaceWe offer a variety of private rooms in different sizes.

Enjoy a relaxing meal in our serene, Japanese-style atmosphere. We offer a variety of private rooms perfect for different occasions, whether it's a date or a business meeting. Choose from cozy rooms for two, convenient spaces for small gatherings of 4 to 6, or a tranquil room for up to 8 guests. Whatever the occasion, we have the perfect setting for you!

Drink"Specialty Shochu! A Selection of Sake Chosen as Famous Japanese Liquor"

Nakamatashuzō, boasting a history of over a hundred years in Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture, offers a variety of limited sweet potato shochu, including the renowned 'Kirino,' which has been selected in the Japanese Sake Encyclopedia. It has also received the Excellence Award at the Kagoshima Prefecture Authentic Shochu Evaluation for two consecutive years, earning recognition even among those who grew up in Kagoshima. We serve shochu that is crafted entirely by hand, infused with sincerity and gratitude, in Shinbashi. Enjoy a blissful moment with our rich variety of historically proud shochu and our proud specialties.
