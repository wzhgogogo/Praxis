Restaurant highlights

A delightful dish made with plenty of seasonal vegetables that will please both your heart and body. Enjoy a course that highlights the flavors of the season in a relaxing atmosphere.

Escape the hustle and bustle of the city and enjoy a relaxing meal and conversation at "Ito." We offer a variety of healthy dishes made with seasonal vegetables, all prepared from scratch. Don't miss ...our flavorful "Seasonal Buckwheat Galette," and we also have a course menu that includes everything from appetizers to desserts.

The interior features a cozy wooden texture, creating a calming atmosphere illuminated by candlelight in the evening. We can also customize dessert plates, making it a perfect spot for everyday dining as well as special occasions like anniversaries. We look forward to welcoming you!
