Food "Enjoy Lemon Pasta Inspired by LEMON!"

"Fettuccine with Lemon and Seasonal Vegetables in Cream Sauce" The spring vegetables that have endured the harsh winter are packed with nutrients. The lemon, which has its aroma and nutrients concentrated in the peel, is used generously in both the peel and juice for this lemon pasta. The acidity is mild, allowing you to enjoy the distinct flavors of each seasonal vegetable. Enjoy the chef's special lemon cream sauce with chewy fresh pasta.

Food "Reservations for Group Drinking Parties are Now Being Accepted♪"

This is a party plan recommended by Chef Kanazawa. The food-only plan is priced at ¥4,000 (tax included). You can also choose optional styles such as "all-you-can-drink" for an additional ¥2,000 per person (2-hour limit) and homemade desserts for an additional ¥500 per person. Please feel free to contact us!

Space"Trattoria Lemon: An Italian Restaurant Managed by Gaku Lemon"

The Italian restaurant "Trattoria Lemon," managed by Lemon Gasui, is a 5-minute walk from Ochanomizu Station. It is also close to Meiji University and offers an atmosphere that feels far removed from the hustle and bustle, reminiscent of a hidden retreat. The interior features a calm space created by a craftsman using a combination of walnut wood and iron, where you can enjoy Italian dishes that highlight the flavors of the ingredients, along with a selection of rich Italian wines.
