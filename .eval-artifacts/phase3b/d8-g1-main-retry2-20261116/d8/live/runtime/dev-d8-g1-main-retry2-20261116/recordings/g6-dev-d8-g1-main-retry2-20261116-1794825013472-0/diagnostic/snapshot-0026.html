Food The ultimate course featuring premium Nodoguro starts at JPY 12,100 (tax included)

We offer a luxurious banquet course featuring the specialty of our restaurant: Nodoguro Shabu-Shabu. While using plenty of Nodoguro, we present it in various delicious forms. Our banquet course is designed to ensure you enjoy a Nodoguro feast that will leave you satisfied. This is something that is hard to find in many restaurants, so we invite you to give it a try. We look forward to your reservation!

Space◆For Entertaining◆ Private Rooms Available for 2 Guests and Up / Sophisticated Interior Space

We have meticulously designed the interior to provide the best space for enjoying the highest quality domestic "Nodoguro" in the prime location of Ginza. We offer a completely private room that can accommodate up to 15 guests, as well as private seating for 2 to 8 guests. Whether you want to relax after work, savor Nodoguro, or host a business meeting or dinner, our space is perfect for any occasion. We invite you to take advantage of our offerings.

DrinkKagoshima Prefecture Satsuma Ibusuki: A Self-Owned Brewery with Over a Century of History Offers Fine Sake

Nakamata Sake Brewery, boasting a history of over a hundred years in Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture, operates this direct store. Under the guidance of the renowned master brewer Tsutomu Kurose, we serve shochu that is crafted entirely by hand, with sincerity and gratitude, in Ginza. Please enjoy our exquisite selections, including 'Kirino,' chosen for the Japanese Sake Encyclopedia, 'Satsuma Nakamata,' awarded the Grand Prize at the Kagoshima Prefecture Sweet Potato Shochu Competition, and 'Kakebishi Kurose Tsutomu,' the final work of the esteemed master brewer Tsutomu Kurose. If you ask, we can also provide seasonal sake and other offerings.
