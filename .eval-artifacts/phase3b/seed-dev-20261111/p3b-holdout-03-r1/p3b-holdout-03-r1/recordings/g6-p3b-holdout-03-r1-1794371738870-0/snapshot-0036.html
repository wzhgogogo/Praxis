Located above Shinjuku Sanchome Station, this casual space is perfect for day drinking or a drink after work, offering authentic Hong Kong dim sum.

Experience authentic Hong Kong dim sum crafted by skilled chefs at 【DimDimSum Shinjuku Marui Main Store】, all at reasonable prices. Our signature dishes, like "Chao Juku Roast Pork Bowl" and "Custard ...Pork Bun," are made fresh in-house. We also offer hearty courses featuring popular menu items!

The nostalgic ambiance reminiscent of Hong Kong includes both table and sofa seating. Whether you're stopping by for a drink after work or enjoying a daytime drink, we warmly welcome you to use our izakaya-style dining!

Food Enjoy shrimp to the fullest! We recommend our hearty course.

Our recommended course features a variety of dishes packed with shrimp. Enjoy a generous selection, including three popular dim sum items! During weekday lunch hours, don't miss out on our premium set, available at a 20% discount for an even better deal!

Food Introducing our carefully crafted specialties, including the photogenic "Custard Pork Bun"!

Among our diverse dishes, let us introduce you to our special favorites. The Custard Pork Bun is a signature item that has won the hearts of our customers with its adorable appearance and creamy flavor! The rich custard cream, made solely from duck egg yolks and sugar, offers an unforgettable taste that you won't want to miss. The Shrimp-Filled Fried Cheung Fun features a soft, chewy rice roll on the outside, with crispy shrimp paste wrapped inside, giving you a delightful combination of textures! Enjoy it with a light dip in soy sauce!

SpaceDirectly connected to the station! A casual space where you can enjoy a nostalgic Hong Kong atmosphere.

Our restaurant is conveniently located just 2 minutes from Shinjuku Sanchome Station. With direct access to the station, you can visit us without worrying about the weather. The interior evokes a nostalgic feel of Hong Kong, featuring 58 flexible tables that can accommodate groups of up to 20 people. We also have 20 cozy sofa seats for a more relaxed experience. This casual space is perfect for a laid-back lunch or a drink during the day.
