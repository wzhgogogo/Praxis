Since its opening in 1964, Ginza Minami has been a hidden gem with many loyal fans.

Ginza Minami has been loved by many as a hidden gem since its opening in 1964. We offer a menu that blends traditional techniques with dishes tailored to Japan's climate and culture, infused with the ...refined sensibility cultivated in Ginza.
With private rooms and semi-private spaces available, we cater to a wide range of occasions, from family gatherings to business meetings.
We aim to be "a delightful place that warms the heart," and we look forward to welcoming you!

Food A Century of Culinary Mastery: Braised Shark Fin in Its Form

At Ginza Aster, we use chicken oil and cook using our original method. By adding soy sauce all at once to the heated chicken oil, we create the flavor of burnt soy sauce. This charred soy sauce, familiar to the Japanese, is easy to eat and can be enjoyed by everyone, from children to the elderly. The shark fin, covered in gelatinous substance, is rich in collagen and is favored by women and those who care about beauty. We invite you to savor the deliciousness cultivated through skilled techniques.

ChefA Japanese Chef Showcases Their Skills Using Seasonal Ingredients from Japan

All the chefs at Aster are homegrown chefs. They have honed their skills from the ground up and cook with heartfelt dedication for our customers. We are flexible in accommodating flavor preferences and arrangements to suit our customers' tastes. Please enjoy our proud selection of dishes prepared by our skilled chefs.

Space"2 Minutes from Ginza Station: A Calm Date Spot for Relaxed Everyday Use"

The luxurious interior of our restaurant is perfect for anniversary dates, birthdays, special occasions, and family meals. We pay great attention to the decor, allowing you to enjoy your special day in a calm and relaxing atmosphere. We also have sofa seating available, so you can unwind comfortably. We look forward to welcoming you for casual dining or lunch as well.

Service"Various Scenes such as Receptions, Dining, Celebrations, and Class Reunions"

We offer courses and gifts tailored to the occasion. They are recommended for anniversary dates, birthdays, special days, and corporate dining. We also have birthday courses and plans suitable for memorial services, so please feel free to inquire. We can also flexibly accommodate reunions and parties.
