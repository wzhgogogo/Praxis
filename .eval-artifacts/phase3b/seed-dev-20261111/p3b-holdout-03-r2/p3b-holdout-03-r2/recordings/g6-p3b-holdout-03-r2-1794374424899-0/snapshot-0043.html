We provide a variety of seating options suitable for family gatherings, celebrations, and business meetings.
Ginza Aster aims to be "a delightful place filled with deliciousness," and we sincerely look forward to welcoming you.

Food Please enjoy the essence of 100 years of tradition and craftsmanship!

At our restaurant, we offer authentic Chinese cuisine based on Cantonese cooking, tailored to the Japanese palate. We carefully select seasonal ingredients from various regions of Japan, and our dishes, which express the four seasons of Japan using traditional techniques honed over many years, have received high praise each time. While enjoying exquisite Chinese delicacies such as shark fin and Peking duck, we invite you to also savor our seasonal recommendations. For celebrations, anniversaries, banquets, and welcome or farewell parties, we also recommend our course meals. We have plans that include all-you-can-drink options as well!

SpaceWe have private rooms perfect for family celebrations and business meetings.

We have a variety of calm, round private rooms perfect for celebrations like Shichi-Go-San, formal events, and business meetings. Gather around the table and enjoy our signature courses and dishes while creating warm memories.

SpaceConveniently located near the station! We welcome banquets for reunions and various gatherings.

Located along Meiji Street and directly connected to Shinjuku Sanchome Station, Shinjuku Hinkan offers a mix of classic dishes from Ginza Aster and our own original menu items. Our chic interior features a banquet room perfect for group parties. We highly recommend our all-you-can-drink plans for any gathering.

SpaceA Relaxed Date Spot for Casual Dining

Feel free to celebrate special occasions like anniversaries and birthdays with us. We also have cozy sofa seating in private rooms for small groups. Enjoy our signature Chinese dishes in a relaxed atmosphere, where you can forget the hustle and bustle of the city.
