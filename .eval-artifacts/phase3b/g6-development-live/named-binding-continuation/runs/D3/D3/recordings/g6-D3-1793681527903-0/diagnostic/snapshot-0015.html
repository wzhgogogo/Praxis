Food Crispy on the outside, chewy on the inside! Enjoy our piping hot Kushi Katsu!

By combining a special batter blended with yam, uniquely developed breadcrumbs, and carefully selected high-quality Dutch lard for frying, we create skewers that are crispy on the outside and chewy on the inside. Our homemade sauce, which is refreshing yet rich, enhances the flavors beautifully.

Food Tenderly simmered Doteyaki and a variety of Kushi Katsu for a drink!

Our signature dish, Dote-yaki, features beef tendon simmered in a rich white miso base! The deep flavors of the miso combined with the tender texture of the beef tendon will surely enhance your drinking experience. Be sure to give it a try!

Food Perfect for finishing off: Beef Soup

The rich flavor of beef combined with Kansai-style dashi is perfect to finish off your meal!
