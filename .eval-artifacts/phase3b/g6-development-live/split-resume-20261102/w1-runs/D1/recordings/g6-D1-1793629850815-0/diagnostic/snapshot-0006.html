Proudly serving Osaka's traditional Kushi-katsu and affordable drinks! Families with children and adult gatherings are all warmly welcomed!

Enjoy the vibrant atmosphere and traditional flavors of Osaka at Kushikatsu Tanaka! Bite into crispy, hot kushikatsu dipped generously in our secret sauce for an authentic taste of Osaka! At Kushikats...u Tanaka, don’t miss out on "Chinchiro"! If you're lucky, you might win a free drink, and even if you don’t, you can still toast with a mega cup! Making your own takoyaki is sure to be a hit too! With affordable prices, you can raise a glass every day!
We also offer great services for kids, making it a family-friendly spot. Experience Kushikatsu Tanaka, where both adults and children can have a fantastic time!
