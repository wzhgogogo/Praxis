Just a 1-minute walk from Akabane Station! Enjoy Sendai Beef Tongue and Aizu Horse Meat Sushi. ~Private rooms available~

Located just a minute's walk from Akabane Station, "Izakaya Motenashi-ya Akabane" is a hidden gem where you can indulge in the delicious flavors of Tohoku, featuring Sendai beef tongue, fresh sashimi,... and carefully grilled dishes.
All seats are private rooms, accommodating from 2 guests to larger groups. It's perfect for various occasions such as dates, girls' nights out, and company banquets.
Popular options include a 3-hour all-you-can-drink course and seasonal hot pot courses.
If you're looking for a stylish yet relaxed izakaya in Akabane, be sure to visit "Motenashi-ya Akabane."
