The vibrant dishes that make the most of seasonal vegetables are perfect for parties, gatherings, and special occasions.
We also accept catering requests, so feel free to reach out through the comments section!

Food A Menu Colorful with Fresh Ingredients ◇ A Course Delivering New Discoveries in Food

We use fresh ingredients sourced frequently to offer seasonal menus. Our goal is to provide delicious dishes that inspire new discoveries about food. We have multiple pricing plans for our courses, so you can choose one that fits your occasion. Each dish is prepared to be hearty and satisfying, ensuring you leave feeling full. First-timers to our courses are more than welcome!

ChefChef Toriumi, Who Knows Vegetables Inside and Out

Masahiko Toriumi, born in 1982 in Chiba Prefecture, started cooking at the age of 18. He began his career at the Michelin one-star vegetarian restaurant "JOIA" in Milan, Italy, at the age of 25. After working as a sous-chef in Italy, he returned to Japan at the age of 30. He became the chef at "Carpolavoro" in Takadanobaba during its opening and has been operating the current restaurant "Ristorante-EN-" as the owner-chef since 2015.

ServiceSpecial plate to celebrate your occasion. Add a message with a reservation made the day before.

We offer special plates perfect for various celebrations like weddings, birthdays, graduations, and welcome parties. By making a reservation by the day before, you can add a personalized message to your plate. Whether you're celebrating with family, friends, or a partner, or even hosting a company gathering, our plates are suitable for a wide range of occasions. We’ll make your special moments even more memorable with heartfelt hospitality. Feel free to reach out to us!

SpaceA relaxed space perfect for anniversaries and after-parties - semi-private room available.

Just a 2-minute walk from Takadanobaba Station, our restaurant offers a calm atmosphere with spacious seating and cozy semi-private rooms for your comfort. We focus on creating a casual vibe where you can drop by without any fuss and enjoy your meal at ease. Whether it's for anniversaries, celebrations, after-parties, reunions, or parent meetings, we provide a versatile space suitable for small gatherings or larger groups. Come visit us!
