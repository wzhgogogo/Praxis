Food [Signature Dish] A Variety of Specialty Dishes Featuring Carefully Selected Ingredients, Including Robatayaki

The essence of Sumika lies in the supreme robatayaki grilled to perfection by skilled artisans. We use seasonal seafood carefully selected from fishing ports across the country, and the unique infrared heat of charcoal grilling creates a delightful crisp exterior while keeping the inside juicy and incredibly tender. Every bit of flavor is locked in. The craftsmanship displayed right before your eyes, along with the enticing aromas, will engage all your senses and lead you to an extraordinary dining experience. Enjoy the unique flavors that only Sumika can offer, crafted from the finest ingredients and techniques.

Food 【Commitment to Ingredients】From our signature Robatayaki to seafood, Wagyu beef, and eel.

Of course, our popular robatayaki is a highlight, but we also have a wide variety of other dishes. Our menu featuring Hokkaido-produced Wagyu sirloin showcases beautifully marbled fat that melts in your mouth the moment you take a bite, along with the sweet and rich aroma unique to black-haired Wagyu. We take great care in sourcing our seafood as well, ensuring that we maintain the highest possible freshness through proper temperature management. Not only do we serve robatayaki, but we also offer sashimi and nigiri.

Food 【Abundant Course Dishes】Enjoy a selection of refined gourmet delights.

We offer a special course featuring carefully selected seasonal ingredients, including premium fish like Nodoguro and beautifully marbled Hokkaido Wagyu sirloin, tailored to fit your budget. Enjoy a delightful array of dishes crafted with skill by our chefs that will truly satisfy your heart and palate. We promise an unforgettable experience in a serene, elegant setting that combines the essence of Japanese aesthetics with delicious cuisine.

SpacePrivate Rooms Available: Enjoy your meal without worrying about your surroundings.

At Suihō, we offer private rooms for a luxurious dining experience where you can relax without worrying about your surroundings. Perfect for business meetings or special occasions, our elegant private spaces provide a memorable atmosphere. Enjoy our carefully crafted menu while spending precious moments with your loved ones. Whether you have a party of around 4 or a medium-sized gathering of 10 to 20, we can flexibly adjust the seating to accommodate larger groups as well. Feel free to reach out to us!
