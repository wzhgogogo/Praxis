Food Signature! Duck Shabu-Shabu☆

Thinly sliced domestic duck meat that has been grilled over straw, served briefly in a flavorful broth that enhances the duck's umami—a luxurious dish. Additionally, please enjoy it with a generous serving of Kujo green onions, which can also be briefly cooked in the broth. You can also have fun changing the flavor with three types of condiments: "Kurata Pepper," "Black Shichimi," and "Sansho Chili." This is one of our proud signature dishes. Once you try it, you'll be hooked!

Space"Private Room: Tatami Seating Available for Banquets♪"

A semi-private sunken kotatsu banquet seat that can accommodate 5 to 16 people♪ This seating area is located at the back of the basement, providing a space where you can enjoy your meal without worrying about those around you, even though it is semi-private. Enjoy delicious duck dishes with your friends in a fun atmosphere.

Drink"Delicious Selected Wines from Waragamo!"

As a representative of domestic wineries, we offer "Soryu" from Yamanashi in the Yamanashi style♪ Additionally, you can enjoy selected wines from around the world that are related to duck, specially chosen by Waragamo.

ServiceNot just the duck, but the broth is delicious too! The vegetables are great!

【Dashi】We use organic white soy sauce from Shichifuku Brewing in Aichi Prefecture, and serve it with dashi made from Hidaka kelp and carefully selected fish flakes. 【Vegetables】We prepare a generous serving of Kujo green onions, flavorful burdock root, and carefully selected smoked duck thigh and loin.
