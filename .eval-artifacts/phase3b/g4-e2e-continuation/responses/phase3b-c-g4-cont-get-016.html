Space"Experience the Nature of Musashino Here in Tachikawa, with Traditional Flavors."

The taste and skill of tradition! We cherish the teachings of the first generation that "sushi is made with sincerity and care," focusing on seasonal ingredients and welcoming our customers with heartfelt deliciousness. *The photo is from about 45 years ago at our establishment. At that time, water would drip from a pipe at the counter, and customers would wash their fingertips while enjoying sushi!

Food "Seasonal Delights: Carefully Selected Fresh Ingredients from Various Regions"

Every day, we visit the markets in Toyosu and Kawasaki, selecting only the ingredients that we have personally inspected and are satisfied with. Our sushi is served fresh and delicious, made to order. At Sushi Katsu, we source fresh ingredients daily, so our offerings change every day, but we strive to accommodate our customers' preferences as much as possible. We aim to be a place where customers can express their desires freely. We also offer courses that are perfect for banquets.

Chef"Sushi Katsu: 'On-Site Sushi' for Celebrations and Parties!"

You can use our services for birthday parties, wedding anniversaries, celebration events, and other festivities. Sushi Katsu will come to your home or company and prepare sushi on-site! We enhance your party with fresh ingredients, skilled techniques, and heartfelt service. 【Example of Budget】 ● Up to 15 people… 15,000 yen per person ● Up to 25 people… 12,000 yen per person ● From 30 people… 10,000 yen per person. Please feel free to consult with us for more details.

Service"Delivery and Takeout Available"

We use the same ingredients that we serve in the restaurant for our delivery. We also accept orders for sushi bento boxes, shokado (Japanese-style lunch boxes), and catering services. Freshness is our priority, so we apologize, but our delivery area is limited to certain parts of Tachikawa City. We have various sizes of bento boxes available, ranging from one serving to seven servings, so please feel free to consult with us. You can also order delivery according to your preferences. ◆ Nishikicho, Shibazaki-cho, Akebono-cho: Delivery starts from two servings ◆ Takamatsu-cho, Hagoromo-cho, Fujimi-cho, Sakae-cho: Delivery starts from four servings.
