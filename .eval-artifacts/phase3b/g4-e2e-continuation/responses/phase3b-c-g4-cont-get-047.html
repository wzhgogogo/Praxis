【3-minute walk from Toranomon Station】A hidden gem restaurant for adults only! Private and semi-private rooms available for business meetings.

《Perfect for New Year parties, business meetings, and gatherings》Located on the 3rd floor of Kasumigaseki Building, "Kasumi Dining" is a "Japanese Sake Oasis" in the heart of the city, providing a rel...axing space to unwind from daily stress.
【Japanese Cuisine】 Enjoy beloved izakaya dishes crafted with care and creativity by skilled chefs, transforming them into delicious Japanese cuisine.
【Sake and More】 Savor the life-giving water, "Japanese sake," which serves as a social lubricant, alongside the world-renowned "Washoku" that energizes you for tomorrow.
We offer over 80 varieties of Japanese sake, including Junmai Daiginjo, Jikon, and Aramasa.

Food "Kaiseki Course Using Carefully Selected Ingredients"

The kaiseki course meals are all served individually. Please feel free to use them for entertaining important clients or during business meetings. We will do our best to accommodate any changes for allergies or ingredients that you may not prefer.

Drink"Over 50 Selected Sakes Curated by a Sommelier Who is Passionate About Japanese Sake!"

The theme is sake that allows you to see the faces of the makers. The owner, who is also a sommelier, personally travels across the country to carefully select over 50 types of sake available at all times. The brands change seasonally, providing a selection that never gets boring, allowing you to enjoy the seasons of sake alongside the four seasons of Japan.

Space"Enjoy Your Private Space: 10 Private Rooms Available"

We have a total of 10 private rooms available for groups of 2 to 8 people. Please feel free to use our various seating options, including table seats by the fully glass-walled windows and counter seats, to suit your needs.

Service"Presented by Sake Expert: Owner Sommelier Naoki Yamaguchi"

Owner Sommelier Naoki Yamaguchi Certified by the Japan Sommelier Association: Sommelier, first SAKEDIPULOMA certified by SSI: Sake Master, sake education instructor, exclusive taster, and sake advisor. His book "The Easiest Way to Choose Delicious Sake in the World" has surpassed a total of 90,000 copies sold. Our other staff members are also certified sake advisors, ready to suggest your preferred sake. Feel free to leave the choice of sake to fit your entertaining budget to us.
