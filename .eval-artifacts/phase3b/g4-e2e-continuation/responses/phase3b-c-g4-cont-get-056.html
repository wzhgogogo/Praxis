SpacePrivate rooms for various occasions

Feel free to enjoy your time without worrying about others around you. It's perfect for anniversaries, dates, business meetings, or any gathering you have in mind.

Food Luxurious dishes to celebrate special days

Our sushi, as shown in the photos, features tuna sourced from the famous tuna wholesaler Yamako in Toyosu. You'll be amazed at the high-quality ingredients we use in our other dishes as well, including Wagyu beef, snow crab, Japanese pufferfish, and shark fin.

SpaceQuality Counter Seats

A serene counter where you can enjoy your meal while watching the cooking process and engaging in lively conversations. It's also a favorite for those who may feel uncomfortable facing others directly. This spot is especially popular among foreign guests.

Food "Carefully Selected Sea Urchin Comparison from Across the Country"

It was featured on Nippon Television's "Baget." We have gathered the best sea urchins from across the country, including purple sea urchins from Hakodate, Hokkaido, and Bafun sea urchins from Murakami and Hokuto City, as well as saltwater Bafun sea urchins from Hokkaido, saltwater purple sea urchins from Mutsu City, Aomori, and purple sea urchins from Ishinomaki City, Miyagi. We hope you will try comparing them!
