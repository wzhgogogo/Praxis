Food Our pride at Ushimasa is definitely the Salted Beef Tongue!

At Ushi Masaya, we offer four types of tongue salt dishes. First up is the "Negi Shio Tan," where you can enjoy the flavor of green onions together with the tender tongue. Next is the classic "Jo Tan," made from high-quality tongue. Then we have the "Tokusen Atsukiri Tan," featuring thick cuts from the softest part of the tongue. Finally, there's the "Tan Moto," which serves four pieces of a rare cut that can only be taken from one part of the whole cow. Each dish is juicy and exceptionally delicious! Starting from JPY 1,580 (tax included).

Food A course at Ushi-Miyabi that guarantees satisfaction in both flavor and portion!

We offer a variety of courses that start with appetizers, feature beautifully grilled meat as the main dish, and finish with delicious rice dishes. These courses are perfect for casual gatherings with friends or important business banquets. Starting from JPY 5,800 (tax included), you can also add an all-you-can-drink option that includes draft beer for just JPY 2,200.

Food Delicious!! Premium Black Wagyu Upper Skirt Steak

Most of our customers order the premium skirt steak! It's also the most popular item for add-ons. At Ushi-Masa, we serve it thickly so you can enjoy both the flavor and texture. The regular skirt steak is just as popular and loved by many!

Space"Enjoy a Delicious Meat Banquet in a Horigotatsu Room!"

For gatherings of 4 to 15 people, we recommend our cozy tatami seating! We also have spacious table seating for around 10 guests, perfect for casual get-togethers or light celebrations with friends.
