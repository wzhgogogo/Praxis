Savor seasonal delights with our casual à la carte and course options. Enjoy a relaxed atmosphere perfect for any occasion!

A skilled chef, trained at "Nadaman" and with experience as the head chef at the famous "Shokkan" in Shibuya, has opened his own restaurant in Kichijoji. With the concept of "just the right Japanese c...uisine," we offer dishes that make the most of seasonal ingredients and pair perfectly with seasonal drinks.

Our menu boasts a wide range of options, from casual à la carte to full-course meals. Enjoy a delightful selection of Japanese sake and wine to your heart's content.

The interior creates a calm and sophisticated atmosphere, making it perfect for everyday dining as well as business meetings and gatherings.

Food "Casual A La Carte Delights: A Rich Selection from Seasonal to Classic Menus"

We offer a wide variety of à la carte options that you can enjoy casually. Our top recommendations include the "Assorted Appetizers," which allows you to savor both seasonal and classic menu items, and the "Soft-Boiled Egg Tempura," which features a soft-boiled egg wrapped in a fragrant seaweed batter. Additionally, since half of our menu consists of seasonal dishes, you can enjoy fresh and new culinary experiences every time you visit!

Food A Wide Selection of A La Carte Courses Luxuriously Utilizing Seasonal Ingredients

We offer a wide range of dishes, from à la carte to full course meals, using seasonal ingredients abundantly. In our half course called "Tsumakase Course," which focuses on appetizers, you can also order your favorite à la carte dishes as additional options. Additionally, our proud monthly special, "Seasonal Clay Pot Rice," is served as the finale of our full course, allowing you to enjoy a variety of flavors. Please savor the seasonal tastes to your heart's content.

Drink"Sake and Wine: Over 10 Varieties Each. Cheers with Your Favorite Drink Tonight!"

We offer a wide variety of drinks that pair well with our dishes. We always have over 10 types of sake available, and our selection changes with the seasons, allowing you to enjoy a range of flavors. Additionally, we have a selection of over 10 types of wine, primarily in bottles, including red, white, and champagne. We also have classic beers, shochu, and whiskey available. Why not take your time to savor your favorite drink?

Space"Enjoy a Meal in a Calm Space with Wood Grain Design ◆ Perfect for Everyday Use and Business Meetings"

The interior boasts a calm atmosphere with a wood grain design that gives a soft impression. You can enjoy your meal in a relaxed manner without any stiffness. We offer counter seats where you can closely observe the cooking process, providing a lively experience, as well as table seats for 2 to 6 people, with options for private reservations available. Feel free to use our space for a variety of occasions, from everyday meals to business entertaining.
