Enjoy Edomae sushi crafted by skilled artisans in a hidden gem in Akasaka.

5 minutes walk from Akasaka Station. "Sushiharu" opened in a quiet residential area of Akasaka 4-chome, close to Akasaka Sacas and Akasaka Biz Tower. The sushi rice is made with ingredients carefully ...selected and purchased every day, and cooked in a hagama pot to bring out the deliciousness of the rice. You can enjoy Sushi carefully prepared by our craftsmen at the counter right in front of you. We also carefully select Sushi that goes well with sushi. Recommended for those who want to relax and enjoy sake with Sushi in a calm, hideaway space.

Food Shari Cooked in a Nanbu Iron Kettle

The rice is characterized by being cooked in a cast iron pot from the South. We use a unique blend of rice that we have carefully researched and developed, paying close attention to the proportions. By using red vinegar, we enhance the richness, and by using grilled salt, we bring out the umami... The rice is prepared to allow you to appreciate its deliciousness while achieving a refreshing taste. Enjoy the sushi, which is filled with the craftsmanship and dedication of our artisans.

Drink"Prepared Sake that Pairs Well with Sushi"

We carefully select local sake from Miyagi Prefecture, where the owner is from, as well as regional sake from all over the country for each occasion. Our focus is on whether the sake is delicious to drink on its own, but more importantly, whether it pairs well with sushi and serves as a good accompaniment to food. As a result, we offer many dry and crisp sakes, allowing you to enjoy their compatibility while savoring sushi.
