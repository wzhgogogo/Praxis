Just a 2-minute walk from Nihonbashi Station, enjoy the renowned "Oni Himokawa" from a famous shop in Gunma and exclusive kamameshi.

Founded in 1894, Hanayama Udon has been a long-standing establishment in Tatebayashi, Gunma, now in its fifth generation.At our Nihonbashi location, we offer the revived "Oni Himokawa" and udon that p...erfectly balances softness and chewiness, crafted by the fifth-generation owner, Takaki Hashida.
We use carefully cultivated wheat from contracted farms in Gunma Prefecture.
Additionally, our Nihonbashi-exclusive "Kamameshi" is a special dish meticulously cooked to highlight the unique dashi flavor that only an udon shop can provide.
Enjoy the traditional taste in a calm atmosphere, right in the heart of the city.

Food The Power of "Onigama" - Three Consecutive Championships

Our pride, the wide noodles "Oni Himokawa," boast a chewy texture and a smooth, slippery finish. So thin that you can see through them, each bite releases a rich wheat flavor. The standout dish featuring these noodles is the "Oni Kama," made with Gunma Prefecture's barley-fed pork. Prepared simply to highlight the natural flavors, this dish perfectly balances the deliciousness of the udon and the pork. It's a champion bowl that has won three consecutive titles at the local udon competition. Be sure to try it at our Nihonbashi location!

Food Seven Flavors of "Hanayama Shippou Dipping Sauce" and the Sentiments Behind Traditional Patterns

At our Nihonbashi location, we offer a total of seven types of "Hanayama Shippō Tsuyu," including traditional "Denshō Tsuyu," "Hanayama Kake Tsuyu," and "Shiro Dashi Kake Tsuyu," along with popular options like "Goma Tsuyu" and "Uma Dashi Curry Tsuyu," as well as "Kuro Goma Sansho Tsuyu" and "Iri Sake Tsuyu." The interior decor and tableware feature traditional Japanese patterns such as Shippō and Asanoha. Among these, the Shippō pattern, symbolizing "connections" and "relationships" through its circular design, reflects our desire to cherish the many connections we make.

Food [Hanayama Udon Nihonbashi Store Exclusive] Two Types of Kamameshi Cooked with Udon Broth

Our specialty rice dish, cooked with our carefully crafted dashi, is a popular menu item exclusive to our Nihonbashi location. We offer two varieties: "Joshu Gomoku Kobameshi," packed with seven ingredients inspired by the seven treasures (Akagi chicken from Gunma Prefecture, shiitake mushrooms, maitake mushrooms, hanabira mushrooms, bamboo shoots, quail eggs, and carrots), and "Ebi Tempura Kobameshi," topped generously with succulent shrimp tempura. Enjoy the delightful aroma and taste of freshly cooked rice, made to order just for you.

SpaceEnjoy dining and shopping at Tsutsuji Saku Hanayama Udon Nihonbashi Store

At Hanayama Udon Nihonbashi, you can enjoy not just a meal but also some shopping. We offer udon for home, carefully selected dipping sauces, souvenirs, gifts, and exclusive products available only at our Nihonbashi location. The walls of our restaurant feature a stunning recreation of the azaleas in Hanayama Park, which inspired our name. It's a perfect spot for solo female diners, families, or meals with friends. Whether you're here for a meal, shopping, or just a break, feel free to drop by!
