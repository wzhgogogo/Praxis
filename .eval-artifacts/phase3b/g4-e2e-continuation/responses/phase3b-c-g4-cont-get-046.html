Enjoy a wide selection of carefully chosen Japanese sake and traditional Japanese cuisine in a private or semi-private room of a house. Perfect for meetings and entertaining guests!

【Perfect for entertaining guests and various course meals】【All private rooms】 We prioritize privacy with a total of 9 private rooms! Reservations are accepted for 2 to 10 guests.Located in Kagurazaka..., a city known for its food enthusiasts, our restaurant offers refined kaiseki cuisine and carefully selected sake from across Japan.
Our pride lies in our extensive selection of over 50 types of sake available at all times. The owner, who is also a qualified sommelier, personally curates the sake, changing the selection weekly to ensure you can enjoy something new every time you visit.

Transportation

3 minutes walk from Ushigome Kagurazaka Station, Exit A3, Toei Oedo Line
4 minutes walk from Exit 1a of Kagurazaka Station on the Tozai Subway Line
6 minutes walk from Iidabashi Station on JR and Tokyo Metro lines

■If coming from Kagurazaka Station
After exiting Kagurazaka Station at Exit 1a, walk left along Kagurazaka Street (towards Iidabashi).
Go down the hill and you will see a green sign for "Resona Bank" on your right. Turn left at the corner opposite "Ekimae Ryugaku NOVA Kagurazaka School" and you will see the bank.

■ If coming from Ushigome Kagurazaka
After exiting from Exit A3, go straight along Okubo-dori Street on your right.
Turn left at the "Kagurazaka-ue" intersection, then turn right at the corner of the building with the "Ekimae Ryugaku NOVA Kagurazaka School" sign and you will see it immediately.

346 meters from Kagurazaka.

Space"【Private Rooms Available】We have prepared a total of 9 private and semi-private rooms."

Private rooms that prioritize privacy are fully equipped with a total of 9 rooms. We have both large and small rooms available for 2 to 10 people, making it a safe choice for business meetings and dining.

Food "Enjoy the Seasonal Ingredients: Savor the Flavors of the Four Seasons."

Every month, we carefully select seasonal ingredients to prepare a menu that allows you to experience the four seasons.

Drink"Over 50 Selected Sakes Curated by a Sommelier Who is Passionate About Japanese Sake!"

The theme is sake that allows you to see the faces of the makers. The owner, who is also a sommelier, personally travels across the country to carefully select over 50 types of sake available at all times. The selection changes with the seasons, ensuring that you never get bored no matter how many times you visit, allowing you to enjoy the seasons of sake alongside the four seasons of Japan.

Service"Presented by Sake Expert: Owner Sommelier Naoki Yamaguchi"

Owner Sommelier Naoki Yamaguchi Certified by the Japan Sommelier Association: Sommelier, first recipient of the SAKEDIPLOMA certification, SSI certified: Sake Master, sake education instructor, exclusive taster, and sake advisor. His book "The Easiest Way to Choose Delicious Sake in the World" has surpassed 90,000 copies sold. Our other staff members are also certified sake advisors, ready to recommend your preferred sake. Feel free to leave the choice of sake to fit your entertaining budget to us.
