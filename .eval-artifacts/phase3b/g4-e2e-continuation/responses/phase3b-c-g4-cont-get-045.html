【3-Minute Walk from Jimbocho Station】A Grown-Up Space for Seasonal Japanese Cuisine and Sake Lovers! Three Private Rooms with Over 50 Types of Sake Available

Perfect for year-end parties and welcome/farewell gatherings!In the intellectually curious town of Jimbocho, a haven for sake lovers has emerged. Experience a rotating selection of sake and seasonal J...apanese cuisine that stirs the heart with a blend of tradition and innovation.
【Sake and Space】
Enjoy carefully selected sake that complements the atmosphere.
Our sommelier and sake expert has curated over 50 varieties of sake from across the country, including premium options like Junmai Daiginjo, Jikon, Hiraki, Aramasa, and Tazai.
【Great Access】
Just a 10-minute walk from 5 stations on 8 lines: Jimbocho, Takebashi, Awajicho, Ogawamachi, Shin-Ochanomizu, and Ochanomizu.

Drink"Over 50 Selected Sakes Curated by a Sommelier Who is Passionate About Japanese Sake!"

The theme is sake that allows you to see the faces of the makers. The owner, who is also a sommelier, personally travels across the country to carefully select over 50 types of sake available at all times. The selection changes with the seasons, ensuring that you never get bored no matter how many times you visit, allowing you to enjoy the seasons of sake alongside the four seasons of Japan.

Food "Seasonal Ingredients Arriving in Droves: Enjoy the Flavors of the Season"

Our daily special menu features dishes made with seasonal ingredients. You can enjoy a meal that reflects the four seasons of Japan, paired with carefully selected sake.

Space"Two Private Rooms with a Pleasant Atmosphere Perfect for Entertaining!"

We have private rooms and semi-private rooms that can be used for important receptions, farewell and welcome parties, as well as kick-off meetings and celebrations within the company. Please make use of our semi-private room for up to 8 people at a table, or the semi-private room facing the window that can accommodate up to 6 people, according to your needs.

Food A Variety of Sake Accompaniments to Pair with Japanese Sake.

We offer a variety of Japanese dishes that pair well with sake, in addition to our fresh seafood. Enjoy pairing with over 50 types of sake available at all times. Our kaiseki courses feature individually plated dishes. Please consider our restaurant for various occasions such as business meetings, important meals with loved ones, gatherings with friends, and welcome or farewell parties.
