Food Experience the emotion from a single piece, crafted with skill to create a delicious chain reaction.

To create a memorable experience from the very first bite, we use a slightly tangy white vinegar in our sushi rice to enhance the flavors of the ingredients. Our tuna is sourced from the specialized wholesaler Yamako in Toyosu, while other ingredients are carefully selected from Ueken. Among our seasonal specialties, the mildly sweet and tangy boiled shrimp is a standout dish that showcases our chef's skill. The fried eggplant, a beloved dish from Azabu-Juban's Hadano Yoshiki, is also exceptional. Additionally, we offer courses for both lunch and dinner. We invite you to come and enjoy!

DrinkSeasonal Selection of Sake and Wine Available

We offer a selection of drinks including sake, local brews, wine, and shochu. Our lineup changes with the seasons, so you can look forward to discovering new flavors. Enjoy your favorite drink alongside our signature sushi and exquisite dishes.

SpacePrivate Rooms Available ◆ Enjoy Our Carefully Crafted Sushi in a Sophisticated Space

The interior features a striking wooden counter, creating a quiet and sophisticated atmosphere. We also have private rooms where you can enjoy your meal in a more intimate setting, perfect for small gatherings. Experience a pleasant time at Sushi Nasu, where cleanliness and elegance come together.
