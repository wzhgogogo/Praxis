Food Ginza Exclusive!! Fresh Meat Yukhoe Available

Experience the exclusive premium "raw meat menu" available only at our Ginza location. Enjoy this exquisite selection paired with carefully chosen wines.

Space"Fully Equipped Private Rooms Ideal for Personal Space"

After work in Ginza, for girls' night out, birthday celebrations, and other special occasions. Enjoy yakiniku in a stylish space. We have private rooms available for up to 8 guests, perfect for business meetings or private gatherings. The open floor can accommodate up to 30 guests for exclusive use. Please contact us by phone for inquiries.

Drink"We have prepared a selection of wines carefully chosen by our sommelier, ranging from affordable options."

We offer a wide range of wines, from reasonably priced options to more premium selections. At the Ushigoro Bambina branch, we have a separate wine list available only at the Ginza location. We also have some hidden wines that are not on the list, so please ask our staff for more information.
