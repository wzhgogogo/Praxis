Food "Crispy on the Outside, Chewy on the Inside: Bite into Hot Skewered Katsu!"

By combining "a carefully crafted batter blended with yam," "originally developed panko," and "carefully selected high-quality Dutch lard for frying oil," we create a skewer that has a texture of "crispy on the outside ♪ and chewy on the inside ♪" The homemade sauce, which is refreshing yet rich, enhances the flavor.

SpaceCute ◎ Delicious ◎ A place you’ll want to tell someone about ◎

Affordable kushikatsu starts at 100 yen, and we've revamped our cute drinks and dishes! It's now a more welcoming place for women too! Whether you're coming alone or for a girls' night out, feel free to visit us!

Drink"Exceptional Drinking Experience! The 'Dodeka Series' - a Specialty of Dengana!!"

The "Highball," "Oolong Hai," and "Shochu Soda" that pair perfectly with skewered cutlets are now available in a satisfying "super-sized" option! For those who find the regular size insufficient, those who finish the regular size too quickly, or those who just want to drink to their heart's content today, why not enjoy a big, impressive drink and take a big gulp?
