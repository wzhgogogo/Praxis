Just a 3-minute walk from Kabukiza, enjoy "Japan's Best Udon" loved in Ginza for 130 years!

Established in 1894, Hanazawa Udon is a long-standing restaurant in Tatebayashi, Gunma. The founder, Kinsaburo Hashida, was inspired by local noodles he encountered in Nihonbashi and returned home wit...h a passion to create udon that surpasses that taste.
This spirit has been passed down through five generations, with the fifth generation, Takaaki Hashida, reviving the wide noodle known as "Oni Himokawa."
Their signature dish, "Oni Kama," has achieved a remarkable three consecutive victories in the Udon World Championship. The Ginza location continues to be loved by both tourists and business customers alike.
In the vibrant culinary scene of Ginza, come and enjoy a bowl that beautifully blends tradition and innovation.

Food The Power of "Onigama" for Three Consecutive Wins

Our signature ultra-wide noodles, "Oni Himokawa," boast a chewy texture and a smooth, slurp-worthy finish. They are so thin that they almost become transparent when lifted with chopsticks, releasing a delightful wheat flavor with every bite. A standout dish that showcases this charm is the "Oni Kama," made with Gunma Prefecture's barley-fed pork. Prepared simply to highlight the natural flavors, the umami of the udon and pork harmonizes perfectly. This bowl has achieved a remarkable three consecutive wins at the local udon competition, proving its exceptional quality.

Food Bringing Out the Power of Ingredients: Authentic Flavors from Local Gunma

Gunma Prefecture, the birthplace of Hanayama Udon, is home to many wonderful ingredients we take pride in. For over 130 years, we've built trust with local producers and preserved authentic flavors using traditional methods. We want to maximize the rich flavors of rare and wholesome ingredients. That's why we keep our cooking simple, allowing you to truly taste the essence of the ingredients. Each bowl reflects the passion of Gunma's producers. Enjoy the power and stories of the ingredients with every bite.

Food [Ginza Location Dinner Exclusive] Oni Bonara

Hanayama Udon is a traditional udon shop that also offers a variety of creative dishes with a free-spirited approach. One standout item is the "Oni Bonara," available exclusively for dinner at our Ginza location. This dish features our signature chewy wide noodles, "Oni Himo Kawa," paired with a rich and creamy carbonara sauce. By using Calpis butter, we add a smooth and deep flavor that sets it apart from anything else. It's the perfect dish for dinner with friends or family.

SpaceAzaleas Blooming in Ginza and Flavors of Gunma

Hanayama Udon Ginza is located just behind the Kabukiza Theatre. The vibrant pink azaleas painted on one wall symbolize the birthplace of Hanayama Udon in Tatebayashi, Gunma. This beautiful scenery welcomes our guests. Whether you're coming in small groups or larger parties, we cater to various occasions. Enjoy our signature wide noodles, "Oni Himo Kawa," along with dishes crafted from high-quality local ingredients and years of expertise, all in a sophisticated Ginza setting.
