Food A Chef's Special Course Featuring Seasonal Sushi Delights and a Finishing Ramen Dish

In the "Omakase Course of Sushi and Appetizers," we use carefully selected seasonal ingredients sourced daily from Toyosu. Each piece of sushi is accompanied by the most suitable condiments to deliver a rich and flavorful experience. To conclude the meal, we offer a fish bone broth white soup ramen and dessert, making it a complete dining plan. Please enjoy the exquisite dishes crafted by our chef, who has trained at a Michelin-starred restaurant.

Food Koshihikari Rice x Red Vinegar ◆ Carefully Cooked Rice in a Traditional Rice Cooker

Our commitment lies in the "shari" (sushi rice) made from Koshihikari rice produced in Fukushima Prefecture. We adjust the water level according to the season and cook it fluffy in a hangiri (a traditional wooden tub). Additionally, we use domestically produced sake lees to create our original blend of red vinegar, which has been aged for over ten years. We offer a unique flavor that enhances the quality of the ingredients, exclusive to our establishment.

Drink"Wine Supervised by a Sommelier: Enjoy the Seasonal Flavors of Japanese Sake"

Under the supervision of a sommelier, we have carefully selected four types of wine, along with a variety of drinks that can be enjoyed with sushi. Among them, we offer about ten different types of sake. We also have seasonal recommendations that are not on the menu, allowing you to experience the changing seasons through our selection of beverages, which is one of the many charms we offer.

SpaceThe calm interior is an ideal spacious setting for special occasions.

Located on the basement level, our shop exudes a calm, hidden retreat-like atmosphere. Inside, we have only 10 counter seats, creating a private space. It is perfect for special occasions such as anniversaries and celebrations, allowing you to relax and enjoy your time. Please feel free to visit us without any hesitation.
