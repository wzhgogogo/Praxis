Just a 1-minute walk from Ginza Station! We have a wide selection of Japanese sake, including Juyondai, Jikon, and Hiraki!

At Ginza Boku Jin, you can enjoy exquisite Japanese cuisine crafted by a chef with ten years of experience at Kiccho. We offer a variety of dishes made with fresh ingredients air-freighted from Aomori..., the chef's hometown. Our course includes a super premium all-you-can-drink option for 2 hours, featuring selections like Dassai and Hiraki. It's perfect for welcome and farewell parties, as well as various gatherings! One of the highlights is the chance to taste the elusive "Juyondai." Relax in our chic and tranquil space with easy access.

Food A Course Crafted with Carefully Selected Ingredients and Masterful Techniques

Indulge in a selection of exquisite dishes crafted by our skilled head chef, who has honed his craft over 10 years at Kiccho, using carefully selected ingredients. We offer various courses tailored to your budget and occasion. Among them, we highly recommend the "Luxurious Kinmedai Sashimi Course," featuring a stunning five-piece platter of fresh, air-freighted seafood from Aomori, allowing you to savor the rich flavors of Kinmedai.

Food Savor the Masterpieces Crafted by Experts Trained at Kiccho

Our head chef has honed his skills in traditional Japanese cuisine for 10 years at Kiccho. The masterpieces he creates are a delight for both the eyes and the palate. Among them, our pride and joy is the Sashimi Platter. Beautifully presented, this dish captivates anyone who sees it, and one bite will surely bring a smile to your face. The selection may vary based on market availability, but if you make a reservation, we can prepare a five-piece platter just for you.

Space[Fully Private Rooms] A Relaxing Space Perfect for Exclusive Use

Conveniently located near Ginza Station, Higashi-Ginza Station, and Yurakucho Station, Ginza Boku-jin welcomes you with a chic and calming atmosphere as soon as you step inside. Choose from a variety of seating options to suit your occasion, whether it's a cozy counter seat for intimate conversations or spacious tables perfect for groups. We also offer private rooms for 4 to 11 guests, making it ideal for business meetings, receptions, and various gatherings. We look forward to welcoming you for a wide range of occasions!

DrinkExperience the rare Junmai Daiginjo from Juyondai with our special contract!

Takagi Sake Brewery, known for its Junmai Daiginjo sake, is located in Murayama City, Yamagata Prefecture, a region famous for its rice. We have a special contract that allows us to offer rare seasonal sake from the renowned Juyondai brand. You can find various types of Juyondai, which are often hard to come by. Our seasonal selections are all limited editions crafted to match the rice's origin. Why not enjoy a glass of our limited-time sake tonight?
