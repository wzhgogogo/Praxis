The renowned hand-made soba from Ryogoku. Masterfully crafted soba and side dishes perfected through self-study.

A charming little shop awaits just off Hokusai Street in Ryogoku. Step through the noren curtain and open the sliding door to find a sophisticated space that beautifully blends traditional Japanese ae...sthetics with modern design. The owner, Takashi Hosokawa, is a true craftsman who only serves dishes he personally finds delicious. He sources high-quality buckwheat from all over Japan, purchasing it in its whole form and preparing it by removing the husk the day before use. The next morning, he grinds it with a stone mill and makes the noodles without any additives. Enjoy the authentic taste of soba...

Food "Enjoy the Differences in Soba Flour Origin with 'Seiro' for Seconds"

The production areas of buckwheat flour include Hokkaido, Ibaraki, Nagano, and Shikoku, spanning across Japan. We use fragrant buckwheat seeds that are grown with care. For customers who request a second serving of 'seiro' (steamed soba), we serve buckwheat from a different region than the first, allowing them to enjoy the difference in flavor.

Space"Enjoying Soba and Drinks from Afternoon at a Spacious Table, Even When Sharing"

The tables are spaciously arranged, allowing for a comfortable atmosphere without worrying about neighboring guests. You can casually drop in for just soba, enjoy a quick drink before your soba, and finish off with a bowl of soba. This is a stylish way to enjoy the experience.

Drink"Delicious Sake That Pairs Well with Soba and Soba Appetizers, Centered Around Junmai Sake"

Sake, like the dishes and snacks, is prepared only with what the owner, Mr. Hosokawa, thinks is delicious. As you enjoy the seasonal changes in the soba appetizers and sip your cup, a blissful time arrives.

Others We are looking for craftsmen and helpers.

We run our shop as a family business. We carefully store buckwheat at low temperatures, sort the grains, and mill them, and we also make soba noodles. If you are seriously studying soba, why not come and work with us at our shop? We are also continuously looking for helpers. (03-3626-1125)
