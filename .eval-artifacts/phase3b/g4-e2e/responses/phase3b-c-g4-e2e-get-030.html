Food Enjoy our Edo-Style Miso Beef Hot Pot made with A4 Kobe Beef Ribeye.

Only those recognized as A4 grade or higher after rigorous evaluation can be called Kobe beef. We deliver carefully selected cuts just for you! Enjoy the traditional Edo-style miso beef hot pot made with Kobe beef, which was born in the Meiji era. This is a top-notch beef hot pot experience that lets you savor history. The delicious Edo-style miso beef hot pot features a special blend of Edo black miso and Edo white miso from Sano Miso in Kameido, which has been around for 300 years. For business meetings or anniversaries, be sure to enjoy our Kobe beef!

ServiceWe offer banquet courses perfect for business meetings, anniversaries, and social gatherings.

Celebrate your special occasions with our Kobe beef course. We offer an omakase course perfect for business meetings, as well as a meat kaiseki course. For private gatherings, try our Wagyu beef sukiyaki course or premium beef hot pot course. It's all up to you! Enjoy a relaxed time at the counter or have important discussions in our semi-private seating. We also have table seating available for customers with strollers. We provide the perfect space and dishes for any occasion, along with top-notch service.

DrinkWe offer a great selection of all-you-can-drink sake!

Enjoy unlimited drinks with all these Japanese sakes! We offer a rotating selection each week, so there's always something new to try! We also have natural wines for our wine-loving guests to enjoy. Plus, you can extend your time for an extra JPY 1500!

SpaceExperience the lively atmosphere at our counter seats.

The only place you can watch the chefs in action is from our counter seats. The lively atmosphere and the incredible skills of our artisans add an extra spice to the deliciousness of your meal! Come and experience the exceptional service that is only available at the counter.
