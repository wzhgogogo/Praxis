A newly opened Edomae sushi restaurant that has impressed food enthusiasts. Lunch is available on weekends.

Introducing the popular sushi spot, Sushi Risaki, which has quickly gained a reputation as a must-visit in the heart of Shibuya. Its second location has just opened in Aoyama. The menu features a sing...le, reasonably priced omakase course, designed to make traditional Edomae sushi more accessible for everyone to enjoy. Each ingredient is carefully selected and prepared, ensuring that customers receive the freshest and most delicious offerings tailored to their preferences on that day.

This new establishment promises to bring even more exciting experiences to the dining scene!
