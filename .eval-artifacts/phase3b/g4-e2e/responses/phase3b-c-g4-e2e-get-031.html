Food "Familiar and Heartfelt Dishes with a Refined Taste."

Enjoy a more casual experience than a kaiseki restaurant, yet more substantial than a typical izakaya. Our seasonal ingredients shine in the omakase course, featuring 10 dishes for JPY 7,000 (tax included). For a little extra indulgence, savor our a la carte options like meat and potato stew, oden, and homemade side dishes, all crafted with care by our skilled chefs. We also offer a variety of delicacies and snacks to pair perfectly with your drinks.

Food Shabu-Shabu with Black Pork Enjoyed with Kujo Green Onions in White Soup Made from Chicken Broth

Our specialty, the Kirishima pork and Kujo green onion shabu-shabu, features a carefully crafted white broth that maximizes the umami of the chicken by simmering it slowly over time. You will enjoy shabu-shabu with black pork raised in Kirishima. Be sure to enjoy it with the perfectly complementary Kujo green onions. This dish is sure to pair wonderfully with sake.

SpaceA warm wooden Japanese space with elevated seating available.

You can enjoy the chef's knife skills right in front of you at our counter seats, which are perfect for solo visits. We also have table seats for groups to enjoy meals together, as well as raised seating areas where you can dine without worrying about your surroundings. Whether it's for your regular dinner or a leisurely meal with friends on the weekend, we offer a variety of seating options. Our space, filled with the warmth of wood, is ideal for your relaxation and will soothe your heart.

DrinkA Selection of Carefully Curated Sake to Enjoy with Food Pairings

We offer a selection of sake and shochu that enhance the flavors of our dishes. We also have seasonal recommended sakes, so feel free to ask our staff for suggestions!
