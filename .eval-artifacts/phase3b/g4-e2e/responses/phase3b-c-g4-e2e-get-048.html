A Must-Visit Sushi Spot in the Trending "Oku-Shibu" Area That Will Impress Foodies: Enjoy Masterful Sushi by Talented Chefs

Sushi Risaki, a sushi restaurant quietly gaining attention in the heart of Shibuya, honors the tradition of Edomae sushi and offers exquisite pieces crafted with skills honed at renowned establishment...s in Tokyo. Each ingredient is carefully selected, and the fish is meticulously prepared to perfectly match the size, temperature, and type of vinegar used in the rice. The restaurant pays great attention to every detail, including soy sauce, wasabi, and even the water, sourcing pristine water from the foothills of Mount Fuji. This is a must-visit spot before it becomes hard to book a reservation!
