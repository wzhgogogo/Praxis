Remarks

Founded in 1958, we have been serving our guests for over 60 years. Please relax and enjoy exquisite tableware, such as antique Imari, and our wonderful natural fugu cuisine. To ensure that our owner-chef can personally oversee the dishes served, we limit our service to one group per day. Please use the private elevator to access our establishment. In keeping with the art gallery district of Ginza, the interior is surrounded by paintings by Shiko Munakata. We also use valuable tableware that is over 150 years old on a regular basis. We regret to inform you that we cannot accommodate guests who do not understand Japanese, as this may pose a significant risk of damage to the tableware or the possibility of broken pieces contaminating the food.

Restaurant highlights

Ginza's Exclusive Private Room Experience: Enjoy Natural Fugu and A5 Omi Beef Sukiyaki and Shabu-Shabu for One Group Per Day

Welcome to Ginza Sugata, an exclusive fugu restaurant that accommodates only one group per day in a private room. Conveniently located just a 5-minute drive from Tokyo Station, this hidden gem offers ...traditional Japanese cuisine perfect for business meetings, gatherings, and special occasions.

Featured on NHK BS's "Bi no Tsubo," we invite you to indulge in our exquisite fugu dishes, including natural tiger pufferfish, as well as A5 Omi beef for sukiyaki and shabu-shabu, all served on beautiful antique Imari porcelain.
