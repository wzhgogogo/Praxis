Remarks

It is a cram school that includes meals.
The target audience is 5th grade elementary school students through junior high and high school students.

We provide meals for each cram school day, with the hope that parents can take it easy and not have to worry about meals.
We have renovated one room into a kitchen and obtained business permission from the health center to prepare our food.

Please use it for everything from regular study to test preparation, and high school and university entrance exams.

We also cater for allergies.
