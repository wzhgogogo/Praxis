Food The Intricate Design Philosophy Behind Each Sushi Piece

The thickness of the fish, the precision of the cuts, and the harmony with the rice all come together in a perfectly calculated sushi that melts in your mouth the moment you take a bite. This is sushi that impresses not with flash, but with its exceptional quality.

ChefInheriting and surpassing tradition, a new Edo-style that customers call "Otani-mae".

Since the day we opened our doors in Akasaka, Otani has been sharing his unique take on sushi, rooted in Edo-style traditions. The blend of Japanese heritage and experiences gained from training abroad creates dishes that are neither strictly traditional nor purely innovative, but rather one-of-a-kind creations that only Otani can craft. Before long, our customers began to say, "This isn't just Edo-style sushi; it's 'Otani-style' sushi."

Food Serene Counter and Elegant Private Rooms

Enjoy the refined atmosphere of our elegant counter seats, where you can appreciate the craftsmanship up close. We also offer private rooms that are perfect for important meetings or entertaining guests. The versatile space design allows you to choose the setting that best suits your occasion.

Food Michelin-Recognized Culinary Excellence

As a Michelin-starred establishment, our reputation is not just a fleeting recognition. It's built on daily preparations, precise temperature control, and the skillful art of sushi making. The techniques we've honed are acknowledged as world-class.
