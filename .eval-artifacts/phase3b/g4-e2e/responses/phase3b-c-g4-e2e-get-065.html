Food Carefully Grilled Free-Range Chicken Yakitori over Binchotan Charcoal by Skilled Craftsmen

We use carefully selected local chicken, expertly grilled one by one over binchotan charcoal. The infrared heat from the charcoal gives the outside a delightful crispiness while keeping the inside tender and juicy. Our skilled chefs masterfully control the heat to bring out the maximum flavor of the ingredients, creating a simple yet profound taste that showcases their craftsmanship. Perfectly paired with salt or our special sauce, each bite bursts with flavor that will keep you coming back for more. Come and enjoy our signature yakitori, where we pay attention to aroma, texture, and taste!

Food Our signature handmade soba, made fresh from scratch every day.

Our handmade soba, made fresh daily from scratch, is something we take great pride in. We carefully grind domestic buckwheat flour using a stone mill to bring out its rich aroma and flavor. Our skilled artisans adjust the water content according to the temperature and humidity, ensuring each noodle is crafted with care. The balance of smoothness and firmness in our soba is simply exquisite. It pairs perfectly with our dipping sauce, offering a simple yet profound taste experience. Come and enjoy our signature handmade soba!

DrinkExtensive Drink Menu

We've got all the classics covered, from draft beer and sake to shochu, along with fruity sours, highballs, and cocktails that are a hit with the ladies. Plus, you can enjoy local sake and craft gin soda that you won't find anywhere else. After kicking things off with a "Cheers!" and a cold draft, feel free to explore and compare drinks that match your mood. We also have a great selection of hidden gems that pair perfectly with our dishes. Enjoy a blissful moment as you sip and savor!

SpaceAll seats are in private rooms with doors, accommodating various occasions.

Our restaurant features fully private rooms with doors, allowing you to relax in a comfortable space. Enjoy your conversations and meals without worrying about your surroundings, making it perfect for dates, family dinners, or even business meetings and gatherings. We offer private rooms suitable for small groups to larger parties, catering to various needs. Experience a special moment in a serene Japanese atmosphere.
