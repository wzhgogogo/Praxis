Enjoy a full course featuring Japan's top brand duck, "Mojou Kamo," at this hidden gem in Nakameguro.

Just a 1-minute walk from Nakameguro Station, "Na Camo guro" is a specialty restaurant focusing on duck dishes, using Yamagata's finest Mogami duck. We work directly with trusted producers to serve it... at the optimal cooking temperature and aging period.
With delicate yet rich flavors and wine pairings carefully selected by our sommelier, we promise a dining experience that will change your perception of duck.
We offer 20 counter seats, 2 tables, and 2 private rooms, making it perfect for important meals with clients, dates, or anniversaries.

Our environmental and social efforts

Using sustainable ingredients

"Okura Village" in Yamagata Prefecture is known as the most beautiful village in Japan. The "Mogami Duck" raised in this blessed environment offers a deliciousness that overturns the conventional understanding of duck meat.

Community involvement

Located in the heart of the Mogami region in Yamagata Prefecture, Okura Village is a small community with a population of about 3,400 people. It is famous for its heavy snowfall, sometimes reaching nearly 4 meters, and is covered in snow for more than a third of the year. However, this also means that the abundant snowmelt provides clean spring water that nourishes the area. One of the new local specialties expected to gain recognition from Okura Village is "Mogami Duck."

Just a minute's walk from Nakameguro Station, our blue door marks the entrance to an adult hideaway where you can enjoy the finest duck dishes. Our signature dish, duck suki, has remained unchanged since we opened. Food "Specialty 'Mogami Duck Sukiyaki'."

Since the opening of our restaurant, our specialty has been "Mogami Duck Sukiyaki." Please lightly mix it with our original brand egg yolk, which is based on a red wine sauce, and enjoy it together.
