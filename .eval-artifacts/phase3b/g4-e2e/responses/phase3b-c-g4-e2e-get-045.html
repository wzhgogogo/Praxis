Food We recommend our seasonal special course. Enjoy the flavors of Japanese cuisine featuring seasonal ingredients.

For first-time visitors, we highly recommend the Seasonal Special Course (JPY 19,800). This standard plan allows you to fully enjoy our signature Japanese cuisine. We use a generous selection of seasonal seafood and vegetables, carefully chosen for their freshness. In a calm and relaxing atmosphere, indulge in exquisite Japanese dishes that showcase the beauty of the season.

Food Carefully prepared seasonal ingredients with artisan skills. Enjoy the encounter with today's dish!

At our restaurant, you can enjoy fresh and delicious seafood, thanks to our 17-year relationship built on trust with local fishmongers at the market. We focus on sourcing rare fish that you don't often come across. We also prioritize using fresh, farm-direct vegetables, and our chef puts in heartfelt effort and skill to prepare each dish with care. Since our ingredients change with the seasons, every visit brings the excitement of discovering a new dish.

DrinkRare brands and limited sake are a must-see. Delicious Japanese sake from all over the country is available.

What you definitely want to pair with your meal is the sake carefully selected and sourced by our owner from all over Japan. In addition to our regular offerings like Houhai from Aomori and Ikko Saba from Fukushima, we also have seasonal selections and rare brands available. Don't miss out on our limited-edition sake sourced from local breweries and popular brews crafted by graduates of Tokyo University of Agriculture.

SpaceThe warm and high-quality wooden space is perfect for dining with your loved ones.

Our restaurant, located near Iidabashi Station, is marked by a fluttering purple noren in a residential area. Step inside, and you'll find a refined space filled with the warmth of wood. The counter seats offer a view of the open kitchen, making it perfect for solo diners or private meals with family and friends. We also have table seating and private rooms, ensuring every spot is ideal for spending quality time with loved ones. We welcome inquiries for private reservations for our 8 counter seats, so feel free to reach out!
