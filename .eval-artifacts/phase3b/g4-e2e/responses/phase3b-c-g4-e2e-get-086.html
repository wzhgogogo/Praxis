A hidden gem for adults, nestled in a quiet residential area away from the hustle and bustle of Nakameguro. This house evokes the charm of a French apartment.

★The smoking process can take up to 24 hours! Behind the dedication of our craftsmen lies a surprising and delightful array of smoked dishes★ ■All our smoked items are homemade, perfectly paired with... draft beer, wine, shochu, Japanese sake, or cocktails for a blissful experience■
★Our establishment is an adult hideout. Just like the secret base you eagerly searched for as a child, it’s right here in Nakameguro. ≪Note≫ This is your secret base, so please don’t tell anyone!
