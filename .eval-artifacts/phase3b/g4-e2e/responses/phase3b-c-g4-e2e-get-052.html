Restaurant highlights

Just 5 minutes from Yoyogi-Hachiman Station ◆ Enjoy the essence of Edomae sushi without hesitation. Experience exquisite pieces crafted with skilled techniques.

Experience the essence of authentic Edomae sushi at the renowned Sushi Risaki Main Store, where you can indulge without hesitation. The chef carefully selects the freshest seasonal ingredients with ex...pert precision. Through meticulous preparation, the natural flavors of the ingredients are maximized. Each piece of sushi is harmoniously balanced with perfectly adjusted rice, considering the size and temperature of the fish, as well as the type of vinegar used, creating a blissful taste sensation in your mouth.
