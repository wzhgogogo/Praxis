Transportation

The restaurant is a 2-minute walk from Nakameguro Station on the Tokyu Toyoko Line and Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line.
Exit the ticket gate at the "South Exit" and turn right.
Keep the train tracks on your left and head towards "Yutenji."
After walking along the tracks for a short distance, you will find our restaurant located between pillars 57 and 58 under the elevated tracks.

210 meters from Naka Meguro.

Food Please enjoy our fresh off-the-morning selection of organ meats!

The offal we serve is all fresh, sourced personally by our owner from Shibaura every morning. Among the variety of offal, our top pick is the Maruchou. If you're unsure, just let us know if you prefer something crunchy, tender, or meaty, and we'll serve you the perfect offal! If you want to try a bit of everything, go for the Assorted Eight Types. If you're in the mood for red meat, don't miss our store-exclusive, daily-changing selection of rich, rare cuts. And of course, our sashimi, which relies heavily on freshness, is a must-try!

Food First things first! Be sure to order our popular "Liver Skewers" and "White Beef Tripe" early!

When you visit Manten, we highly recommend starting with the "Liver Skewers" and "White Senmai". Our skilled chefs carefully prepare fresh liver and senmai that we source each morning. They pair perfectly with beer or shochu! The "White Senmai" is meticulously processed by hand, offering a pure white appearance. With no unpleasant odor and a delightful crunchy texture, it truly showcases the natural deliciousness of senmai. Both items are popular and may sell out quickly, so be sure not to miss out!

Food We take pride in our dishes! Perfect for a satisfying finish or a tasty snack!

It's not just about meat dishes! We also focus on easy-to-enjoy small plates. One of our top recommendations is the Anchovy Nigiri, featuring a gentle flavor with a balanced saltiness. The subtle taste of mixed anchovies spreads lightly, making it a refreshing and easy-to-eat option. Perfect as a finishing touch after your meat dishes, or as a great companion for pre-drinks or after-parties. Enjoy a variety of these rich and delightful dishes!

ServiceAlways cheerful and smiling service! We provide delicious and enjoyable moments!

At Manten, we strive to provide a fun experience that you'll want to share with your friends. If you're unsure about the various types of hormone cuts, feel free to ask our staff. We'll carefully explain each cut and the best way to grill it. Discovering new hormone dishes can be a delightful adventure. Enjoy your time at Manten with excitement!
