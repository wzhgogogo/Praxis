【Authorized to Serve Raw Wagyu】You’ll want to come here even on a regular day.Indulge in the sound of meat sizzling and the aroma that melts your worries away, all while enjoying top-notch yakiniku in... a relaxed atmosphere.
We meticulously pursue "deliciousness" in every detail, ensuring you can fully enjoy the quality of the ingredients, flavors, textures, and aromas.
In a calming space that exudes warmth from wood, experience the comfort of a laid-back environment and savor the unique "juicy meat and happiness" that can only be found here. Enjoy the ultimate yakiniku experience designed for adults.

Introductory Course — 10 Dishes (Recommended for first-timers): 3 Salted Cuts including Premium Tongue and Outside Skirt, Spencer Roll, Nomero Salt, etc. On-the-Day Reservations OK / Group seating available for small parties to groups of 10+10 items

Starting with an umami-rich Wagyu Bakudan, the course continues with signature dishes such as a three-item salt assortment featuring premium beef tongue and outside skirt, rich-flavored premium liver, and a luxurious grilled sukiyaki-style large Spencer roll. You can also fully enjoy the charms of both beef and pork—from Nomero salt to Iwachu pork belly and chuck eye roll. With Mugen Kimchi, namul, and a seasonal sherbet, this satisfying course keeps you delighted until the final bite.

Food Indulge in Premium Yakiniku A La Carte to Suit Your Mood

The key is the perfect balance of lean meat and fat. We carefully select Japanese beef that meets the strict standards of meat professionals. Our attention to detail in marbling, texture, elasticity, and moisture levels makes our beef exceptionally delicious. Enjoy our top-quality yakiniku at Tōgyūmon, where meat lovers can indulge in a la carte options to their heart's content. From thick cuts of premium tongue and flavorful wagyu skirt steak to fresh beef sashimi and yukhoe, you can savor a wide variety of exquisite dishes and soups.

Service"Anniversary Surprise: Create a Memorable Celebration for Your Loved Ones."

The "Castle Cake" has a gorgeous appearance that makes you want to take a photo, almost like a wedding cake. With its impressive three-tiered design, why not celebrate and surprise your loved ones in a grand way? *The part that is edible is the first tier (the very top). Enjoy a wonderful time with your special someone in a calm atmosphere while indulging in our proud grilled meat.

Space"【Private Space】Enjoy High-Quality Wagyu Yakiniku in a Relaxing Atmosphere"

In order to enjoy our proud Japanese beef in a relaxed atmosphere, we have created a high-quality private space. Every detail, from the lighting and tables to the flooring and color scheme, has been meticulously designed to provide a luxurious feel while ensuring comfort. This exquisite space is perfect for business dinners, dates, anniversaries, and entertaining guests.

Food A Devoted Course to Fully Savor High-Quality Wagyu Beef.

【Authorized establishment for serving raw Wagyu beef◎】Indulge in luxurious Wagyu BBQ with thick-cut filet steaks and ribeye from premium black-haired Wagyu. Each dish is a masterpiece that pursues both 'taste' and 'beauty,' with a focus on presentation. We confidently offer satisfying BBQ using innovative and interesting combinations of ingredients in our 'Wagyu Equation.' For more details, please refer to the course section.
