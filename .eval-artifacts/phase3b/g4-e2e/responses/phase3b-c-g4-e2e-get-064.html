Food Maximum Indulgence! Uncovering the Secrets Behind the Deliciousness.

Only 2 out of every 10 cattle yield the rare and exquisite "Otoro Horumon" with its delightful texture, paired with the satisfying "Thick-Cut Chashu". These two stars are enveloped in a rich, flavorful broth infused with robust back fat and garlic. From the very first bite, you'll experience an explosion of umami in this extraordinary dish that looks and tastes indulgently beyond compare!

Food Special Squid Rice with Rich Umami

We packed tender, super-fresh squid with chewy glutinous rice soaked in flavorful broth. By slowly simmering it with a sweet and savory touch, the umami of the squid seeps deep into the rice. With every bite, you'll experience a rich and delightful taste, making this dish a true specialty of ours.

DrinkExtensive Drink Menu

We've got all the classics covered, from draft beer and sake to shochu, along with refreshing fruit-infused sours and highballs, plus cocktails that are a hit with the ladies. Don't miss out on our local sake and craft gin soda that you can only find here. After raising a glass with a cold draft, you can enjoy a variety of drinks that match your mood. We also have a selection of hidden gems that pair perfectly with our dishes. It's the perfect time to unwind and savor every sip.

SpaceAll seats are in private rooms with doors, accommodating various occasions.

Our restaurant features fully private rooms with doors, allowing you to relax in a comfortable space. Enjoy your conversations and meals without worrying about your surroundings, making it perfect for dates, family gatherings, or even business meetings. We offer private rooms that can accommodate small groups to larger parties, catering to various needs. Experience a special moment in a serene Japanese atmosphere.
