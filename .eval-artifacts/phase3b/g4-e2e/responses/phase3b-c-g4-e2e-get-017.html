【2-minute walk from Gaienmae Station! 4-minute walk from Aoyama-itchome Station】A restaurant and café with a view of the Jingu Gaien Ginkgo Avenue

We focused on deliciousness and ended up with a unique blend of international cuisine based on French cooking. We select cooking methods and seasonings from around the world to bring out the natural f...lavors of the ingredients.

Here, you can enjoy KIHACHI's signature dishes that transcend genres.

The second-floor main dining area offers a dynamic view of the ginkgo trees in Jingu Gaien, while the first-floor café and food hall features an open terrace along the ginkgo-lined street. We cater to diverse lifestyles and dining experiences.
