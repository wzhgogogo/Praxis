New Style Chinese experience for body and soul

Located in the heart of Ginza, Kinri Ginza is a high-end Chinese restaurant themed around food, beauty, nature, and creativity. Its new-school Chinese cuisine blends traditional techniques with Japan’s seasonal ingredients, offering health-conscious dishes including hot pot inspired by medicinal cuisine. The elegant dining, bar, and lounge are ideal for anniversaries and business occasions.
