Proudly serving Osaka's traditional Kushi-katsu and affordable drinks! Families with children and adult gatherings are all welcome!

Enjoy the vibrant atmosphere and traditional flavors of Osaka at Kushikatsu Tanaka! Bite into crispy, hot kushikatsu dipped generously in our secret sauce for an authentic taste of Osaka! At Kushikats...u Tanaka, don’t miss out on "Chinchiro"! If you're lucky, you might get it for free, and even if not, you can toast with a mega cup! Making your own takoyaki is sure to be a hit too! With affordable prices, you can raise a glass every day!
We also offer great services for kids, making it a family-friendly spot. Experience Kushikatsu Tanaka, where both adults and children can have a fantastic time!

Food Is it the thrill of spices or the punch of garlic? Which one do you prefer?

Introducing the upgraded "Unlimited Garlic Horumon Skewers"! Experience an even stronger punch of garlic and an addictive flavor that will blow your mind! The "Unlimited Spicy Horumon Skewers" feature a special spice blend of multiple herbs and spices in a perfect ratio, delivering a bold and exciting taste! Both unlimited skewers pair perfectly with drinks and are still available at an unbeatable price of JPY 50 (tax included JPY 55) each!

DrinkUnbelievable Price! "Unlimited Highball" for just JPY 110 (Tax Included) anytime!

Incredible value! Our "Infinite Highball" pairs perfectly with skewered cutlets and is just JPY 110 (tax included) for a glass anytime! Don't miss out on our "Infinite Skewers" at JPY 55 (tax included), which go great with our special sour drink for skewers at JPY 319 (tax included)! We offer two refreshing options: the "Tsundere," a dry drink with strong carbonation, lemon, and lime to cleanse your palate, and the "Yorisoi," which enhances the flavors of the skewers with its gentle sweet and sour notes of plum and grapefruit. Come enjoy this ultimate pairing that you can only experience here tonight!

Service【Kids Welcome】Enjoy Takoyaki and Ice Cream on the House! Great Perks Await!

Families with children under elementary school age are more than welcome! We have some great free perks for everyone to enjoy together. 1. A fun "Takoyaki Set (9 pieces)" is free for one group of children under elementary school age! 2. If kids win against our staff in a "serious rock-paper-scissors" game, they get a free soft drink (tie gets half off). This is for those under 19 only. 3. After the meal, kids can enjoy a complimentary "make-your-own soft serve ice cream"! Feel free to use our restaurant for family dinners or mom gatherings. (Note: Some services may not be available at all locations.)

Food Banquet Course with 120-Minute All-You-Can-Drink for JPY 4,000 (Tax Included) ~

Of course, you can enjoy our signature dishes like Kushi Katsu and Saiboshi! We also offer a course where you can make your own piping hot Takoyaki. With a 2-hour all-you-can-drink option, it's a hearty and satisfying course that will leave you completely satisfied!
