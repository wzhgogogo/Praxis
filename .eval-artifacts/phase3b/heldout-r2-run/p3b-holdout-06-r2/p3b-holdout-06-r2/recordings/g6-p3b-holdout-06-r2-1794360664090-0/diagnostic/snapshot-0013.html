Gather this autumn and winter at Hakata Hanamidori in Shinjuku Mitsui Building. Enjoy a heartwarming time around our special Mizutaki hot pot.

Enjoy the unique flavors of Hakata, featuring specialties like "Torikawa Maki Maki Kushi" (chicken skin skewers), rich "Mizutaki" (chicken hot pot), and succulent "Motsunabe" (beef tripe hot pot). Ou...r spacious restaurant can accommodate up to 60 guests for reservations. We also have private rooms for 6 people, perfect for various gatherings and business meetings. Additionally, we offer a sunken kotatsu private room that can host up to 30 guests, and private reservations are available.
Take your time and savor the deliciousness that Hakata has to offer!

Food Using our own brand chicken "Hanamidori"! Enjoy authentic Hakata-style Mizutaki and Motsunabe◎

One of Hakata's traditional dishes is Mizutaki. We are a casual izakaya branch of the long-loved specialty restaurant, Hakata Hanamidori. Here, you can enjoy a variety of delicious snacks and dishes that pair perfectly with drinks, along with our signature Mizutaki and Motsunabe. Experience the authentic flavors of Hakata in a relaxed setting. We invite you to come and taste it for yourself!

Food Our signature "Chicken Skin Rolls" - 10 skewers per person is a must!

Our signature "Chicken Skin Roll Skewers" are crispy and fragrant, carefully fried, marinated in our special sauce, and grilled to perfection. This labor-intensive dish is a must-try that pairs perfectly with drinks and is sure to become addictive. The more you order, the better the deal, with options for 5, 10, 15, or 20 skewers available!

Others Perfect for Welcome and Farewell Parties♪ All-You-Can-Drink Course including signature dishes from JPY 5000~

Perfect for welcome and farewell parties, drinking gatherings, reunions, and company banquets! Start with our signature "Torikawa Kurukuru Skewers" and choose from our popular courses featuring our proud "Mizutaki" or "Motsunabe" hot pot. We also offer luxurious courses where you can enjoy everything from "Hanamidori" chicken. Check out the details in our course menu!
