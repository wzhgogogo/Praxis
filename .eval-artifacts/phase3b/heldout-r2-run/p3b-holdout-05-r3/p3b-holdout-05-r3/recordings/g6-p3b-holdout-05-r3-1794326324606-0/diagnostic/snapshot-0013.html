Enjoy a variety of appetizers, pasta, seasonal Italian specialties, and a wide selection of carefully chosen wines.

Our cozy atmosphere is perfect for solo diners and families alike. Takeout is also available!

Food "Deliciously Baked Pizza in a Wood-Fired Oven. Each One is Made with Care."

The dough, which is carefully hand-kneaded every day, is made using only "wheat, water, salt, and yeast." After more than 24 hours of maturation and fermentation, the pizza is baked in a wood-fired oven at 500°C for about one minute, resulting in a crispy, fragrant crust with a chewy and moist texture that is truly addictive. The simple yet never-boring pizzas, made with high-quality ingredients, use items sourced from Campania, Italy, such as tomatoes, olive oil, and mozzarella. Enjoy it fresh and piping hot!

Food "Authentic Italian Cuisine is One of the Highlights. A Rich Menu Awaits!"

You can enjoy a variety of authentic Italian dishes, including different types of pasta such as penne and bucatini, as well as homemade appetizers. Our fish and meat dishes feature original menus that change with the seasons. Additionally, we offer carefully selected courses that allow you to fully enjoy our salads, pizzas, and main dishes. We also welcome wine bring-ins, cake orders, and can accommodate requests for meal arrangements, so please feel free to consult with us.

Space"Enjoying Meals and Conversations in a Relaxed Atmosphere"

At 【GINA】, you can enjoy the deliciousness of handmade dishes made with carefully selected ingredients and cooking methods without any hesitation. The first floor offers a close-up view of the pizza being baked, while the second floor provides a spacious area where you can enjoy meals and conversations at a leisurely pace. Both floors feature larger table seating, making it a welcoming space for families, local customers, as well as individuals and those with children. On weekdays, you can visit us relatively smoothly, while we recommend making a reservation on weekends. Takeout is also available!

DrinkA Rich Selection of Italian Wines to Enjoy with Pizza.

The diverse selection of Italian wines includes specialties, red, white, and sparkling wines, as well as a variety of after-dinner drinks like homemade limoncello and grappa. You can enjoy them alongside delicious dishes. For those who want to taste many different kinds in small amounts, we recommend ordering by the glass. We also offer suggestions based on your preferences and the meal you are having. Additionally, we have a variety of light snacks available, such as homemade pickles, olives, and oil-cured prosciutto.
