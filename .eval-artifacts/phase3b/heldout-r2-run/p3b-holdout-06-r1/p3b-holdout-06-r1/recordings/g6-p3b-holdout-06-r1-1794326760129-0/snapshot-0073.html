【Akita Prefecture Hinai Chicken Specialty Restaurant】 Rare Hinai chicken grilled over charcoal and fine sake from all over Japan. Private and semi-private rooms available.

【3-minute walk from Shinjuku Station South Exit】 *This is the Nishi-Shinjuku branch* At Imaiya, our chefs, known as "Yakikata-nin," have passed rigorous evaluations of around 100 items to master the ...art of grilling, bringing out the best flavors of our ingredients. Every day, our Yakikata-nin pour their heart and soul into each skewer, embodying the spirit of "Ikkyu Inkon" (one skewer, one soul). In addition to our grilled chicken, we offer seasonal dishes and a selection of fine sake from across the country. Our private rooms are fully enclosed with doors, making them perfect for business meetings and special occasions.

Transportation

3 minutes walk from the South or West Exit of JR Shinjuku Station *Please note that there is also restaurant at the East Exit.
After exiting the South Exit ticket gate, turn right and go straight across Nishi-Shinjuku 1-Chome intersection along Koshu Kaido.
When you see Matsumotokiyoshi on your right, turn into the narrow alley and go 15-20m ahead to the basement floor of the Okamoto Building on your left (Isomaru Suisan is on the first floor).

From the West Exit, take the escalator up to the ground level exit, pass by Keio Department Store, cross the crosswalk next to the bus stop for the airport, turn left, and when you see Mizuho Bank on your right, turn right.
The Okamoto Building is on the basement floor about 15 to 20 meters ahead on your left.

SpacePrivate Rooms Available A High-Quality Japanese Space Filled with the Warmth of Wood

Escape the hustle and bustle of the city and enjoy your meal in a serene Japanese space. Our restaurant features a warm wooden interior that blends modern design with a touch of nostalgia, creating a refined atmosphere for adults. Take your time and savor an elegant dining experience with your loved ones. We also offer private rooms for up to 10 guests, perfect for everyday meals, business meetings, or special gatherings. Various course options are available to suit your needs.

Food Enjoy Hinai Chicken Grilled over Charcoal

One of Japan's top three delicious chickens, Hinai Jidori, is raised freely in the vast nature of Akita, taking its time to grow. We focus on using female Hinai Jidori, known for its firm red meat and sweet fat, which deepens in flavor with every bite. The meat is skewered in a size that allows you to enjoy its plump texture and juicy taste, then carefully grilled over charcoal before serving.

Food "Imai-ya Craftsman 'Grill Master' Perfectly Cooks Exquisite Yakitori"

At Imai-ya, we have established a certification system called "Yakikata-nin" (grill master) since our founding to refine our grilling techniques. We have set over 100 strict internal standards and conduct certification exams. Only chefs who pass these standards are allowed to be called "Yakikata-nin" and are permitted to serve grilled dishes to our customers. We invite you to experience the "ultimate yakitori," crafted with the passion and pride of our "Yakikata-nin."

Drink"We offer a carefully selected collection of fine sake from across Japan."

The rich natural blessings of Japan and the crystallization of brewing techniques that continue to evolve day by day. Sake is born, nurtured, and matures in harmony with the climate, geography, and changing seasons of various regions. At Imaiya, we offer a carefully selected collection of fine sake from all over Japan.
