Others "Harvest It Yourself! Nutrient-Packed Fukuchi White Garlic with Minimal Odor - 6 Cloves"

We use the highest quality garlic, "Aomori Prefecture Fukuchi White 6 Cloves," directly delivered from farmers, in both our dishes and drinks! Thanks to our unique low-odor processing, the post-meal smell is less noticeable compared to regular garlic. Additionally, it boasts a rich and mild sweetness, umami flavor, high nutritional value, and benefits such as fatigue recovery and skin beautification✨. Every year, in June and July, all our staff go to the site to assist with the harvest. By deeply understanding garlic, we are able to maximize its appeal in our cooking and customer service!

Food "One Year’s Worth of Garlic in One Go?! Boost Your Immunity with a Variety of Garlic Dishes"

The garlic experts conduct daily research, offering a wide variety of original dishes that maximize the umami of garlic! With a commitment to homemade sauces, their unique garlic cuisine is sure to whet your appetite♪ Among them, the "Whole Fried Garlic," which has a 90% order rate!? The "Garlic Rice" with a hint of shiso, and the "Garlic Steak" that is carefully grilled are must-tries★ The highlight is the trending Peperoncino on Instagram, which uses more than ten times the usual amount of garlic! There are also garlic chips available for an extra kick, so you can add more to your liking♪

DrinkUnprecedented⁉️ A Garlic Drink with an Amazing Punch! Over 30 Types of Wine Available at All Times

A unique flavor just for here, with garlic in the drinks! We mix fresh garlic that has been soaked in alcohol and aged to perfection in just the right proportions. Our most popular drink is the "Garlic Lemon Sour." The "Garlic Sangria" and cocktails are also popular✨ We have staff with sommelier qualifications, and we offer a selection of 30 different wines to satisfy wine lovers! Additionally, we have a variety of drinks without garlic, such as craft beers, sangria, and sours, so those who prefer "normal drinks" can rest assured♪

Space"Hidden Bar Near the Station♪ Deepen Your Bonds with a Sensory Experience of Garlic♡"

The interior is a lively space filled with cheerful music. With an open kitchen, you can enjoy the live experience of watching the chef's skills and the flames rising when the meat is grilled! There are also garlic-themed decorations, cushions, and hats, making it perfect for social media photos! Whether you're coming from work, having a girls' night out, or meeting someone for the first time, conversations are sure to flow and connections will deepen! Of course, solo diners are also very welcome! We have counter seating, table seating, and as the season changes, terrace seating is becoming popular too! Feel free to drop by and recharge your energy!
