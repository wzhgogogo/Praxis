[Anniversary Event] Enjoy authentic creative sushi and rich oysters in a luxurious setting, along with a wide selection of Japanese sake for a perfect adult time.

At "Sai Uo no Bansan," we aim to exceed your expectations with our concept of "Sushi, Oysters, and Sake Rebellion." Our talented chefs, trained in Michelin-starred restaurants, create exquisite dishes... featuring seasonal fresh fish, including premium tuna sourced from Toyosu, all at a luxurious yet affordable price.
Our signature oysters can be enjoyed in various dishes, and we also offer an all-you-can-eat option for those who want to indulge! With around 80 different types of sake available throughout the year, you can experience a unique selection that changes daily, ensuring a memorable encounter with each visit.

Food Sourcing from Toyosu: Creative Sushi Crafted with Exceptional Fresh Ingredients and Skilled Techniques

We offer a selection of creative sushi crafted by chefs who have honed their skills at Michelin-starred restaurants. Our fresh, high-quality tuna, including medium and fatty toro, is sourced directly from Toyosu, allowing us to provide it at reasonable prices by purchasing whole bellies. Our "smoked sushi," which features the day's recommended sushi lightly smoked, offers a new flavor experience each time. Enjoy our carefully curated dishes that will delight both your eyes and palate to your heart's content!

Food "Indulge in the Rich and Melting Deliciousness of Oysters: All-You-Can-Eat Course Available"

For oyster lovers, our pride and joy: oysters! At our restaurant, we use the oysters that won second place in the 2024 Oyster-1 Grand Prix, allowing you to fully enjoy their smooth texture and rich flavor. We offer a variety of dishes including raw oysters, steamed oysters, and grilled oysters! In addition to a la carte options, we also have an all-you-can-eat course, making it perfect for oyster enthusiasts. Don't miss this opportunity to indulge in high-quality, delicious oysters!

Drink"Discover Your Favorites: A Selection of Japanese Sake That Even Connoisseurs Will Approve Of"

Our establishment specializes in sake. We offer a diverse selection of around 20 different types of sake, boasting a range and quality comparable to high-end sushi restaurants! Additionally, 12 to 13 of these varieties change based on our supply situation, allowing you to enjoy unique and rare sakes that you won't find again—truly a delightful experience! We also provide a sake map that categorizes the options into four types, making it easy to identify flavor characteristics at a glance, such as smooth types. You can easily find the flavor that suits your taste!
