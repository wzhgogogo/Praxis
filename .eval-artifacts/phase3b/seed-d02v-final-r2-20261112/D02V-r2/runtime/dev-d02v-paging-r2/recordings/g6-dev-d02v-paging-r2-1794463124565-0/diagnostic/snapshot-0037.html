Sourced through domestic air freight in small quantities, with excellent ventilation and roasters available at every seat.

Celebrating its 10th anniversary, Hitsuji Ichi has opened a new location in Nakano, following its branches in Shibuya, Gotanda, and Shinjuku. We offer fresh lamb sourced through air transport. Enjoy a... lively atmosphere at our counter seats or opt for our sunken kotatsu seating for groups and parties! With a wide selection and high quality that can only be found in Tokyo, don't miss out on this unique Genghis Khan experience crafted by a chef from Sapporo, Hokkaido.

Food [1% Distribution] [No Freezing] [Air Freight] Fresh Lamb

At Yagiichi, we are committed to our meat quality, focusing on air transportation and Australian sourcing. Currently, 99% of lamb is imported by sea. While shipping by boat keeps costs low, it takes about 2 to 3 weeks to arrive in Japan. In contrast, air freight can deliver in as little as 5 days. Our motto is "If it’s not available, we’ll create it." We never compromise on making delicious food!

Food Homemade Sauce

When it comes to Genghis Khan, the quality of the meat is important, but the real secret to the deliciousness at Yagiichi lies in our special sauce. This soy sauce-based sauce, with a hint of sweetness and infused with vegetables and fruits, is so addictive once you try it. We recommend adding garlic, sesame, and chili from the condiments on each table to create your own unique sauce, Yagiichi style!

Food Lamb × Cilantro × Sauce & Condiments

The unique flavor of fresh lamb pairs perfectly with the refreshing kick of cilantro. Feel free to add our complimentary coarsely ground chili and fresh grated garlic for an extra punch that you won't be able to resist! Be sure to give it a try! 【Details】 Jingisukan (Lamb BBQ) for JPY 1,100 Cilantro for JPY 450 Coarsely ground chili for free Fresh garlic for free
