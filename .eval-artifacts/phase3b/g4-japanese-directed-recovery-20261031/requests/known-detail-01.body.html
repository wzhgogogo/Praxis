We have welcomed a new head chef with Michelin experience, who skillfully utilizes traditional Japanese culinary techniques. Enjoy a course that stimulates all five senses, featuring playful dishes as... well as simple, unadorned plates, offering a fresh kaiseki experience.
■ Private rooms accommodating up to 14 guests, with smoking options available.
■ A nostalgic Japanese atmosphere that feels far removed from the hustle of Shibuya.

Food Chef's Special Seasonal Omakase Course

The omakase course consists of 9 dishes, and you can enjoy your choice of drinks from the menu. Please feel free to contact us for business meetings or banquets as well.

Space"Perfect Private Rooms for Various Scenes: A Japanese Space Like a Luxury Inn"

Just a 3-minute walk from Shinsen Station, Kappo Sanchou is a warm Japanese space that resembles a high-end ryokan, nestled in a back alley of Dogenzaka. The restaurant is housed in a renovated traditional Japanese building that is over 70 years old, featuring a calm and serene atmosphere. The private rooms have a modern Japanese table seating arrangement and can accommodate from 2 guests. As this is a smoking-friendly establishment, smoking is also permitted in the private rooms.

Drink"We offer sake that pairs well with the seasons and dishes."

In autumn, we have "hiyamaoroshi" and "akiagari," and in winter, we offer "shinsyu," with our selection changing according to the season and day. To ensure everyone can enjoy, we prepare Japanese sake that pairs well with seasonal dishes, as well as limited-edition sake that can only be enjoyed during that time.
