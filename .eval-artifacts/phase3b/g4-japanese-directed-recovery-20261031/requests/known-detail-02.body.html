Transportation

From the East Exit 1 of Nakameguro Station on the Hibiya Line or Tokyu Toyoko Line, cross the crosswalk and turn right. From Nakameguro Atlas Tower, walk along the river. It is just after crossing the bridge. It is a 3-minute walk.

Remarks

We operate two types of businesses: a dining bar and a hamburger specialty shop. During dinner time, we serve as a casual dining bar where you can enjoy Japanese, Western, and Chinese cuisine. From Wednesday to Saturday during lunch hours (10:00 AM - 3:30 PM), we operate as Kento's Burger, a specialty shop for original hamburgers. Both venues are kid-friendly and pet-friendly.
