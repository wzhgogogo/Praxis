A unique fusion of traditional Korean cuisine and the delicate flavors of Japanese culinary art.

A hidden gem of a Japanese-Korean restaurant nestled in a back alley of Nakameguro.With a calm and elegant atmosphere, it features counter seating, table seats, and private rooms, making it perfect fo...r business meetings, dates, or special occasions.
The dishes combine traditional Korean cuisine with the skilled techniques of Japanese cooking, using high-quality local ingredients.
We focus on direct sourcing, allowing you to enjoy the natural flavors of the ingredients without relying solely on the heat of chili peppers and garlic, resulting in a gentle taste.
Indulge in a course meal featuring the finest selected Wagyu "Tajima Beef" and domestic Ise lobster and blue crab marinated in soy sauce.

Food "Commitment to Ingredients: The Theme of 'Connection'..."

We are focused on the theme of "connections" with ingredients. We make an effort to visit the local areas as much as possible and learn about the culture and history of each country and region on a daily basis. Please enjoy our course meals that feature carefully selected dishes emphasizing "directly sourced, natural, and fresh" ingredients, while cherishing the passionate thoughts and stories of the producers.

Space"A Special Space Where Two People Can Enjoy a Relaxed Time Forgetting About Everything in a Spacious Setting"

As before the relocation, the interior of the store values the warmth of wood, creating a slightly dim atmosphere so that even couples can enjoy a spacious environment and forget about time while relaxing.

Drink"Of course Korean Soju, but also definitely try organic wine and sake..."

Of course, we have drinks like sake and Korean soju! We also offer organic wine and sake☆☆ In particular, we have a wide variety of sake. Please enjoy it paired with your meal. Depending on the type, the glasses are different, allowing you to enjoy the changes in aroma and flavor with our exquisite selections.

Food "Wakasa's Popular Delicacies Gathered in a Guest Meal Course"

Please enjoy the various dishes of Wakasa's pride, featuring Japanese and Korean cuisine. We offer a guest meal course that gathers popular dishes from Wakasa, as well as a chef's choice guest meal course that incorporates seasonal ingredients of the day and your preferences. Feel free to consult us regarding your budget and preferences.
