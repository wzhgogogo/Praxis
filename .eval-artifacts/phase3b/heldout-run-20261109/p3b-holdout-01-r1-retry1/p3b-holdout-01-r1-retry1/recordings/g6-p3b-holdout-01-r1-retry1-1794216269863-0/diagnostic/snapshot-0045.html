Restaurant highlights

Just a 5-minute walk from Kasai Station, it's La Copa, a Mexican restaurant! We're currently holding a Corona Fair!

We offer 10 types of Mexican beer, and you definitely have to try our frozen margaritas made with 100% fruit pulp and our mojitos made from an original recipe! We take pride in serving our dishes at t...he right temperature—hot dishes hot and cold dishes cold. Our nachos (chips) are fried fresh upon order to ensure a crispy texture.

We are also committed to using natural ingredients, and our homemade jalapeño paste pairs perfectly with our dishes, even better than Tabasco!

Enjoy our signature Mexican cuisine made with generous portions of ingredients. Our cozy and inviting restaurant features both table and counter seating, allowing you to choose the perfect spot for any occasion.

Warm-hearted Master and Mama are ready to serve you their delicious home-cooked meals and drinks. We look forward to welcoming you!
