A mysterious pink sign stands in the back of Nakano. Enjoy dried fish, seasonal vegetables, and meat! Our specialty is "Robatayaki" that delights all five senses!

Pet-friendly seating available for 7 guests (reservation required).Enjoy a lively atmosphere at this izakaya specializing in robatayaki. We offer a rich menu featuring dried fish from "Ginza Bansuke" ...such as mackerel and hokke, along with a variety of fresh seasonal vegetables. Pair your meal with around 40 types of one-cup Japanese sake sourced from all over the country! The casual setting is perfect for everyday use. Whether it's a quick drink after work, a date, a girls' party, family gatherings, or banquets, feel free to drop by anytime!

Food The King of Dried Fish "Bansuke" - Simply Delicious!

Robata-yaki, where you can enjoy the live experience of grilling right in front of you! In addition to our grand menu, we carefully select and procure fresh seasonal vegetables from Toyosu Market. The main offerings include dried fish such as mackerel, hokke, and anago from "Ginza Bansuke"! We also have miso-marinated silver cod and various meats available. Our meat selection includes Ooyama chicken thigh, chicken breast, and grilled beef skirt steak wrapped in magnolia leaves. Additionally, please enjoy our handmade dishes that reflect the seasons. We have classic izakaya staples like sashimi of seasonal fish!

Drink"One Cup: Always Over 20 Varieties. Based on Your Mood for the Day!"

We offer about 20 varieties of one-cup sake, carefully selected from across the country to provide a well-balanced range of flavors. You can choose from dry to sweet options according to your preference. The cute appearance of the one-cup sake is also popular among women, and we source it from a sake shop in Nakano. The lineup changes with the seasons, so it's a delightful point that you can enjoy something new every time you visit! Additionally, our signature drink is the Sui Gin Soda, made with a strong carbonation server. We also have a variety of classic beers available.

Food Not Just Grilled Dishes! A Variety of Side Dishes Available Too.

A casual space with a warm atmosphere, featuring attractive wood grain decor. The main open kitchen counter offers a prime spot where you can enjoy watching the cooking process right in front of you. Additionally, we have semi-private table seating for 6 to 8 people. It provides a sense of comfort that seamlessly blends into everyday life. Whether it's for a drink after work, a date, a girls' night out, or a family gathering, we welcome you to join us for a wide range of occasions, from the ordinary to the special. Pets are welcome, and we also have outdoor standing seating available!
