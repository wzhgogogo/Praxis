Enjoy authentic and delicious fugu at reasonable prices. Founded in Osaka about 45 years ago, "Genpin" specializes in Japanese pufferfish cuisine. The founder's vision was to make high-quality pufferfish accessible to everyone, and we are committed to providing genuine flavors at affordable prices.
