Restaurant highlights

The Best in Eastern Japan! Selling 2,000 bowls a day☆ A Slightly Luxurious New Sanuki Udon♪

Welcome to our Sanuki Udon specialty shop in Nakano and Shin-Nakano! Our popular premium meat udon is made exclusively with A5 grade black wagyu beef, delivering a bowl that will make you feel truly h...appy!

Today, we continue to bring smiles, health, and happiness through our food. We take great care in crafting our homemade "Shin Sanuki Udon," focusing on the texture of the noodles (chewy and stretchy), the aroma of the dashi, and the quality of each ingredient, from vegetables to seasonings.

We aim to create a place that brings smiles and delightful surprises to families and loved ones. We look forward to welcoming you!
