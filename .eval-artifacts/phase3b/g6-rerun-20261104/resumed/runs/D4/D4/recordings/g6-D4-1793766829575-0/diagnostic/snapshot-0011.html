Remarks

Instructions for Customers Bringing Dogs
1. Please ensure your dog is wearing clothes at all times (to prevent shedding and dander).
2. Bringing snacks is allowed.
3. Please refrain from bringing your own meals.
4. Do not feed your dog any food provided by the restaurant (including cabbage) or use any items meant for human use, such as plates.
5. We kindly ask that you do not bring pets in heat (during their menstrual cycle).
6. Please use leashes or carriers to prevent your dog from wandering around the store.
7. You may bring a maximum of 2 dogs per person.
Even if you do not fall under the above categories, we may refuse entry based on the staff's judgment on the day. Thank you for your understanding and cooperation in creating a pleasant environment for everyone.

Food Is it the thrill of spices or the shock of garlic? Which one do you prefer?

Get ready for the upgraded "Unlimited Garlic Horumon Skewers"! Experience an even stronger punch of garlic and an addictive flavor that will blow your mind! The "Unlimited Spicy Horumon Skewers" feature a special spice blend of multiple herbs and spices in a perfect ratio, delivering a bold and zesty taste! Both unlimited skewers pair perfectly with drinks, and the price remains the same at just JPY 50 (tax included JPY 55) each!

DrinkUnbelievable Price! "Unlimited Highball" Always Just JPY 110 (Tax Included)!

Unbeatable value! Our "Infinite Highball" pairs perfectly with skewered cutlets and is just JPY 110 (tax included) for a glass anytime! Don't miss out on our "Infinite Skewers" at JPY 55 (tax included) and the special sour drink designed to complement them for JPY 319 (tax included)! We offer two refreshing options: the dry "Tsundere" with strong carbonation and a zesty lemon-lime flavor to cleanse your palate, and the gentle "Yorisoi" that enhances the taste of the skewers with its sweet and sour notes of plum and grapefruit. Come enjoy this ultimate pairing that you can only experience here tonight!
