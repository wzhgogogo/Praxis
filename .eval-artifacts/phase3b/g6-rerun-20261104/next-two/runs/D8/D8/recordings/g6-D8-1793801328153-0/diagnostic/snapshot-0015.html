Food Course to Savor Exquisite Nodoguro

We offer a selection of fresh seafood, featuring exquisite flavors and carefully chosen fish by professionals. Our course includes 8 dishes, featuring Satsuma Black Wagyu Zabuton grilled over charcoal and Nodoguro fish cooked in a traditional style, priced at JPY 6,600. If you want an all-you-can-drink option for 2 hours, it's available for JPY 8,800. Of course, we also prepare sashimi using carefully selected ingredients delivered directly from the fishing port, and we can customize courses to fit your budget. Feel free to reach out to discuss your preferences!

Food "Charcoal-Grilled: The Thickest, Highest Quality Nodoguro Prepared in the Most Delicious Way in Japan"

Enjoy the finest nodoguro grilled in the traditional way! Nodoguro, known for its black mouth, is a delicious fish rich in fat, often referred to as the "fatty white fish." The traditional cooking method called "genshiyaki" involves slowly grilling fresh fish and vegetables over an irori (a traditional Japanese hearth) on skewers. The outside becomes crispy and fragrant, while the flesh remains fluffy. Please savor the traditional genshiyaki experience. Additionally, you can enjoy fresh fish delivered directly from carefully selected fishing ports and markets across the country.

Drink"Specialty Shochu! A Selection of Sake Chosen as Famous Japanese Liquor"

Nakamatta Shuzo, boasting a history of over a hundred years in Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture, offers a variety of limited sweet potato shochu, including the renowned 'Kirino,' which has been selected in the Japanese Sake Encyclopedia. This shochu has been recognized by those who have grown up in Kagoshima, having won the Excellence Award at the Kagoshima Prefecture Authentic Shochu Competition for two consecutive years. All of our shochu is crafted by hand, infused with sincerity and gratitude, and is served at Tsukiji. Enjoy a blissful moment with our rich variety of historically proud shochu and our proud specialties.

Space"Various Sizes of Private Rooms Available for Infection Control Measures"

We have various private rooms available for different occasions, including a room for 4 to 6 people with a view of the courtyard that is popular for entertaining overseas guests, an 8-person room convenient for small gatherings, and a completely private room for 12 people with a calm atmosphere. The room with a view of the courtyard is especially recommended for business meetings. Since these are private rooms, they are also ideal for infection control measures. Additionally, we are implementing various infection prevention measures to ensure that our customers can visit us with peace of mind.
