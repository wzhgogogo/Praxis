Food [Morning Harvest Selected Fresh Fish Platter] Featuring Natural Fresh Bluefin Tuna, Our Most Popular Menu Item!

Every day, our expert from Toyosu Market selects the finest natural bluefin tuna and giant botan shrimp, making this our most popular dish with nearly 100% cost efficiency. We also offer a variety of fresh seasonal fish sourced from all over Japan. If you visit us, you definitely don't want to miss out on this!

Drink"Chūmata Sake Brewery: Directly Operated Store of Chūmata Gōmeigaisya in Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture"

Nakamatta Sake Brewery, boasting over a hundred years of history in Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture, produces shochu with sincerity and gratitude, all by hand under the guidance of the renowned master brewer Tsutomu Kurose. We will serve these exquisite shochus in Hachobori. Please enjoy our gems, including 'Kirino,' selected in the Japanese Sake Encyclopedia, 'Satsuma Nakamatta,' awarded the Grand Prize at the Kagoshima Prefecture Sweet Potato Shochu Competition, and 'Kurose Tsutomu,' the final work of the esteemed master brewer Tsutomu Kurose.

Service"Perfect for Banquets: A Calm Private Space for Your Gatherings"

We have a variety of table seating available in a calm and relaxing atmosphere. We can accommodate parties and gatherings for groups as well. Additionally, we have seating options for customers who wish to spend a leisurely time, so please feel free to contact us. We also accept private reservations for parties (30 to a maximum of 40 people). If you are interested, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
