Food Enjoy a variety of courses featuring exquisite Nodoguro (Blackthroat Seaperch) (from JPY 12,200)

We offer a luxurious banquet course featuring specialties like grilled giant blackthroat seaperch, whole blackthroat seaperch rice cooked in a clay pot, and blackthroat seaperch shabu-shabu. Enjoy a variety of dishes that showcase this exquisite fish in different forms. Our blackthroat seaperch course is unique to our restaurant in Higashi-Ginza, and we guarantee it can't be beaten by any other place! We also have all-you-can-drink options available. We look forward to your reservation!

Space"Private Rooms Perfect for Anniversaries, Celebrations, Entertaining, and Dining"

We have completely private rooms that can accommodate up to 10 people, as well as semi-private rooms for 4 to 8 people. Whether you want to relax after work, enjoy delicious nodoguro, or celebrate special occasions such as anniversaries, business meals, or entertaining clients, our space is perfect for you. There are only 2 semi-private seats available, so please contact us early!

DrinkA Sake Brewery with Over a Century of History in Satsuma Ibusuki, Kagoshima Prefecture Offers Fine Sake

Nakamata Sake Brewery, boasting a history of over a hundred years in Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture, operates this direct store. Under the guidance of the renowned master brewer Tsutomu Kurose, we serve shochu that is made entirely by hand, with sincerity and gratitude, in Ginza. Our offerings include 'Kirino,' selected in the Japanese Sake Encyclopedia, 'Satsuma Nakamata,' which won the Grand Prize at the Kagoshima Prefecture Sweet Potato Shochu Competition, and 'Kurokoji Kirino,' which combines the best of white koji Kirino and black koji Nakamata. If you inquire, we can also provide aged shochu that has been stored in traditional ceramic jars for over three years, as well as limited edition shochu.
