Food "Luxurious Grilled Nodoguro (Blackthroat Sea Perch) Prepared Using the Primitive Cooking Method"

While the simmered dish is certainly delicious, by serving it grilled, it becomes a dish that can only be made with the highest quality, thick 500g+ nodoguro (blackthroat seaperch). You can enjoy the juicy richness of the fat when you take a bite, while also savoring the sweetness and umami of the flesh. Additionally, we have a wide selection of shochu chosen from the top 100 Japanese sake that pair well with nodoguro, so please find the perfect one for you.

Food Fresh Seafood Directly from the Fishing Port and Market Nakamata's pride in seafood offers a unique taste and texture.

We carefully select fresh fish directly from the source. The fish is flown in straight to our restaurant, ensuring top-notch freshness and flavor. We offer brand-name fish such as Bonito from Makurazaki in Kagoshima, Seki Horse Mackerel and Seki Mackerel from Saganoseki in Oita, and Nomo Horse Mackerel from Nomozaki in Nagasaki. Our skilled chefs prepare the day's catch with expertise and serve it to you.

Drink"Shōchū from Nakamata Sake Brewery in Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture, Selected as One of Japan's Top 100 Sake"

Nakamatta Sake Brewery, boasting over a hundred years of history in Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture, produces shochu with sincerity and gratitude, all by hand under the guidance of the renowned master brewer Tsutomu Kurose. We will serve these exquisite shochus in Hachobori. Please enjoy our gems, including 'Kirino,' selected in the Japanese Sake Encyclopedia, 'Satsuma Nakamatta,' awarded the Grand Prize at the Kagoshima Prefecture Sweet Potato Shochu Competition, and 'Kurose Tsutomu,' the final work of the esteemed master brewer Tsutomu Kurose.

SpacePrivate rooms available for 2 to 10 guests, perfect for parties and business meetings.

We offer private rooms for groups of 2 to 10, perfect for business meetings or small gatherings. Enjoy a relaxed dining experience in our completely private spaces. Our courses start from JPY 9,000 and include all-you-can-drink options. Choose a course that fits your occasion!
