Drink"Delicious Sake from Fukui Perfectly Paired with Your Meal ◎ For More Details, Please Ask Our Staff ♪"

We have about 7 types of sake from Fukui that pair perfectly with our dishes! The recommended sake changes each month, so you can enjoy something different every time you visit. We also have seasonal sake, so please savor the deliciousness of each season. Additionally, we offer refreshing and easy-to-drink "Gott's Famous Lemon Sour," "Carefully Selected Draft Beer," "Shochu," and "Plum Wine," as well as the very popular "Unsweetened Tea Highball" among women.

Food "Carefully Selected Seasonal Ingredients ☆ Fresh Seafood Dishes"

We carefully select seasonal and exceptionally fresh ingredients! You can enjoy the most delicious items of the season throughout the year. Our "Sashimi Platter," which uses plenty of seasonal ingredients, is exquisite—please give it a try! We also offer courses that feature abundant seafood dishes! Our recommendation is the "Chirihama Course for 4,000 yen"! It's perfect for various parties with an all-you-can-drink option included. Enjoy delicious food and great drinks—make your fun gatherings unforgettable at Gottsu!

Food "Delicious Dishes in Fukui Beyond Seafood! Plenty of Exquisite Cuisine!"

Not only do we offer incredibly fresh seafood dishes, but we also have options like "Saba Heshiko Yaki" and "Toro Saba Hamayaki"! Our local specialty, "Sauce Katsu Don," features a rich sauce from Amano-me. Contrary to its appearance, it is not heavy and is a popular dish that is easy to eat! Additionally, we have a variety of menu items using fresh ingredients delivered directly from the region, such as "Echizen Shio Salad," as well as many dishes that pair perfectly with alcohol. Enjoy them alongside Fukui's local sake!

Space"Perfect for Large Gatherings! Groups of Up to 40 Welcome!"

Leave your large group parties to us! Our second-floor seating, which is recommended for groups, can accommodate up to 40 guests. We have three TVs available! It's perfect not only for various parties but also for watching sports. Additionally, we offer counter seats, table seats, and sunken kotatsu seating. Feel free to use our space for anything from small gatherings to large events. Whether it's a girls' night out, company party, drinking party, banquet, or celebration, we will create a wonderful evening for you! We look forward to your reservation!
