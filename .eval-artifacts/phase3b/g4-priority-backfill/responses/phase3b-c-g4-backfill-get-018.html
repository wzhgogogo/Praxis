Food Edo-Style Sushi with Distinctive Red and White Vinegared Rice and Exquisite Tempura Crafted by Skilled Artisans

We have captured the "best moment" of the ingredients, enveloping their original deliciousness and charm in a delicate batter. The moment it enters your mouth, you will experience an explosion of umami and a lingering, quiet aftertaste... Please enjoy this tempura that allows you to savor the natural story brought by the "season." The serene entrance and the door made of cypress create a dignified atmosphere that makes you forget the hustle and bustle of the city. As you step inside with a sense of anticipation and pleasant tension, the counter made of white wood welcomes you refreshingly.

SpaceThe story of nature is brought by the "season." Enjoying the moment with seasonal ingredients throughout the four seasons...

The seasons that surround us. I believe they carry stories brought by the "seasonal ingredients." At "Kichijoji Sushi Iwai," you can enjoy sushi, tempura, and a variety of dishes made with an abundance of seasonal ingredients. What you can truly enjoy right now is the story encapsulated in the sushi, tempura, and dishes that showcase the flavors of the changing seasons. While feeling the transition of the seasons, take your time to enjoy your meal.

Food "Seasonal Ingredients at Their Best"

We carefully select seasonal ingredients and our skilled chefs prepare them to perfection, serving you the best flavors possible. Don't miss out on the chef's signature dishes, honed at a Michelin-starred restaurant. Come and enjoy!

Space[Private Rooms Available] Enjoy a blissful moment in an elegant Japanese space on special occasions.

We offer a wide variety of plans for both lunch and dinner to suit your budget and occasion. These are perfect for company receptions, celebratory meals, and special events such as weddings and funerals, making them ideal for your special day dining.
