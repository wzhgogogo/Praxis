[Kyobashi TODA Building] "Kyobashi Saiku" - A new neighborhood where art and culture intersect. <Private rooms available>

The concept is "Connecting Sake, People, and the City."Kyobashi has nurtured a culture of craftsmanship since the Edo period, and its artistic heritage still thrives today.This place connects Japanese... culture through the world heritage of "Washoku" (Japanese cuisine), traditional industry "Nihonshu" (Japanese sake), and traditional crafts like tableware and sake vessels, while showcasing the cutting edge of these traditions.
Here, you can enjoy the perfect blend of innovative Japanese cuisine and over 80 types of Japanese sake selected by Naoki Yamaguchi, all in a space where everything comes together.

Food Enjoy seasonal flavors paired with carefully selected Japanese sake by our sommelier.

We incorporate seasonal ingredients that change with each season into our monthly courses. For our all-you-can-drink courses, we also offer a selection of 10 carefully chosen types of sake by our sommelier. Be sure to enjoy!

Drink[Commitment to Sake] Over 50 carefully selected sake varieties by our sommelier!

Our bar features over 50 carefully selected Japanese sakes, personally chosen by our sommelier owner who travels across the country to find the best. With seasonal changes in our offerings, you'll always discover something new, allowing you to enjoy the flavors of Japan's four seasons alongside the seasonal delights of sake.

SpacePrivate Rooms: We offer a total of 7 private rooms.

We offer a total of 7 private rooms that can accommodate various occasions, such as business meetings and gatherings. Each room can host up to 6 guests.

Service[Provided by a Sake Expert] Owner Sommelier Naoki Yamaguchi

Owner sommelier Naoki Yamaguchi is certified by the Japan Sommelier Association as a sommelier and has also received the first SAKEDIPLOMA certification from SSI as a sake master. He is a lecturer on sake studies and a dedicated taster. His book, "The Easiest Way to Choose Delicious Japanese Sake in the World," has sold over 90,000 copies. Our other staff members are also certified sake sommeliers, ready to recommend your favorite Japanese sake. Feel free to leave the choice of sake to us based on your budget for entertaining guests.
