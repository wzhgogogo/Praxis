Food "Deep-Fried Skewers: Freshly Fried, Hot, and Crispy♪ Many Great Value Sets Available"

Our restaurant takes pride in our carefully crafted kushikatsu and Osaka's famous doteyaki. The kushikatsu is fried fresh upon order, so you can enjoy it crispy and hot! We offer a variety of value sets, including a 5-piece set, a seafood 5-piece set, an all-star 10-piece set, and a "thank you for your hard work" set that includes drinks. We also have courses with all-you-can-drink options, making it perfect for parties and gatherings!

Food "Osaka's Famous Dish 'Dote Yaki' Perfect for Pairing with Alcohol! Enjoy the Authentic Flavor Made with White Miso"

Our doteyaki is characterized by its rich flavor made with white miso. Unlike stews, it is served on skewers! We use beef tendon instead of offal, and it does not contain ingredients like tofu, konjac, or vegetables. We also offer a set that includes one authentic doteyaki and one doteyaki egg. Enjoy the true taste of Naniwa to your heart's content!

Space"Close to the Station: Enjoy Daytime Drinks in a Casual, Homey Space for a Toast"

Our shop, with its inviting and homey atmosphere, is perfect for casual meals and gatherings with friends! It's an ideal place to enjoy a lively time without any worries. While all seats are non-smoking, we have a designated smoking room, so smokers can feel comfortable as well. We open at 11 AM, making it great for daytime drinking too! Just a 1-minute walk from Akitsu Station on the Seibu Ikebukuro Line, our location is very convenient. Feel free to drop by!

Food "Perfect for Casual Drinks and Parties: All-You-Can-Drink Plan with a Rich Drink Menu"

We've got a fantastic all-you-can-drink course for alcohol lovers! Choose between a 1-hour or 2-hour option, and you can even extend your time, so you can relax and enjoy without worrying about the clock. With over 30 drink options including beer, sour cocktails, shochu, and highballs, there's something for everyone! Pair your drinks with our delicious dishes and savor a blissful moment.
