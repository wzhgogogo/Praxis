Space"Enchanted by Masterful Skills at a Serene Counter"

Please enjoy your meal at our L-shaped counter, beautifully crafted from white wood. The space is simple yet exudes a sense of cleanliness. We have meticulously focused on every detail to ensure our customers are completely satisfied.

Food "Omakase Nigiri Course: A Supreme Moment Crafted by Masterful Nigiri and Exquisite Dishes"

The sushi toppings are made exclusively from the finest natural ingredients. The owner personally visits the market every day to carefully select seasonal items. The perfectly cooked rice uses red vinegar, which is characteristic of Edomae sushi, enhancing the flavor of the toppings. Additionally, the wasabi, soy sauce, and high-quality seaweed from Ariake, all of which enhance the taste of the sushi, reflect the owner's dedication to every item that customers enjoy.

Space"Kaiseki and Sushi for Every Season: Perfect for Special Days with Loved Ones."

We offer a wide variety of plans for both lunch and dinner to suit your budget and occasion. These are perfect for company receptions, celebratory meals, and special events such as weddings and funerals, making them ideal for a memorable dining experience on your special day.

Food "Exquisite Mastery: A Culinary Masterpiece Like a Work of Art"

We carefully select seasonal ingredients and prepare them to be served at their most delicious state. Please enjoy the chef's pride, a masterpiece honed by experience as a craftsman in a Michelin-starred restaurant.
