ChefShichigosan Masayuki: Breathing New Life into Tradition and Evolving Deliciousness

Born in 1969, I jumped into the world of sushi at the age of 15 and have been dedicated to it ever since. While cherishing the tradition of Edo-style sushi, I constantly seek to evolve and capture the "deliciousness of today" by sincerely engaging with the ingredients. With nearly 40 years of experience, I pride myself on my keen eye and skills, bringing out the best flavors of each ingredient with care in every piece I craft. Each sushi I make reflects my way of life, and with that thought in my heart, I continue to face the art of sushi.

Food A Memorable Edo-Style Sushi Crafted with Tradition and Innovation

One of the charms of Sushi Jinnan lies in its unique blend of red vinegar and rice vinegar used for the sushi rice. This fragrant rice, with just the right amount of acidity, enhances the flavors of seasonal ingredients while firmly asserting its own character. Great care is taken to achieve harmony between the rice and the toppings, ensuring a perfect balance in each piece. As you take a bite, the aroma of the topping gently spreads, and the rice melts pleasantly in your mouth. Rooted in traditional Edomae techniques, each piece is crafted with meticulous attention and the skilled intuition of the chef, embodying their passion for the art of sushi.

Food A Moment of Fragrance, with a Touch of Surprise and Playfulness

A dish where Edo-style techniques meet a playful spirit. As the fish is smoked right before your eyes, the fragrant smoke enhances the natural flavors of the fish. This momentary smoking preserves the essence of the ingredients while adding a subtle smoky aroma as an accent. Each bite stimulates your senses, offering a truly unique experience that is only available right now. Enjoy a masterfully crafted dish that breaks free from tradition, infused with a touch of surprise and playfulness.
